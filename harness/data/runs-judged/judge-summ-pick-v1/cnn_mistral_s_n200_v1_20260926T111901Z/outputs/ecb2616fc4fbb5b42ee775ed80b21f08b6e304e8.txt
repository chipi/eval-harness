Ryan Gosling’s divisive directorial debut *Lost River*, set in a decaying Detroit, blends nihilism with hope, capturing the city’s crumbling beauty and resilience. Gosling, inspired by the Brewster-Douglass Projects, shot the film amid urban blight, weaving its threats and rebirth into a post-industrial gothic tale. The film, out April 10, contrasts destruction with signs of renewal, reflecting Detroit’s complex identity.
