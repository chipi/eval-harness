The VII Summit of the Americas, expected to highlight U.S.-Cuba rapprochement, was overshadowed by tensions as Venezuela accused the U.S. of plotting a coup, prompting sanctions and regional alliances. Venezuelan President Maduro, backed by Cuba and other leftist leaders, demanded the repeal of U.S. sanctions, escalating diplomatic friction. The summit became a "triangle of tension," with Latin American solidarity against U.S. policies threatening to dominate the agenda.
