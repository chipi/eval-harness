Mary Kay Letourneau, 53, and her former student-turned-husband Vili Fualaau, 31, reflect on their controversial marriage in an upcoming "20/20" interview, marking their 10th anniversary. Letourneau, who served prison time for their underage relationship, discusses plans to teach again and clear her sex offender status, while Fualaau addresses struggles with addiction and systemic failures. The couple, parents to two teen daughters, remains together despite their 22-year age gap.
