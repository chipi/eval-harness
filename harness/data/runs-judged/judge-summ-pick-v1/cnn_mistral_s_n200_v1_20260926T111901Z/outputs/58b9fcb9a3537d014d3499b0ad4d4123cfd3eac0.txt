The NCAA claims it has no legal responsibility to ensure the academic integrity of courses taken by student-athletes, despite promoting education as a core principle, in response to a lawsuit over a massive UNC academic fraud scandal. Critics argue the stance contradicts the NCAA's long-standing rhetoric about prioritizing athletes' education, while the organization compares its role to professional associations like the ABA. The UNC scandal involved fake classes and fraudulent grades to keep athletes eligible, raising questions about the NCAA's oversight and purpose.
