The 2015 MTV Movie Awards featured standout moments like Amy Schumer’s sharp monologue, Channing Tatum’s twerk for Jennifer Lopez, and Rebel Wilson’s censored joke, alongside misfires including Shailene Woodley’s rambling speech and Zac Efron and Dave Franco’s cringe-worthy bit.
