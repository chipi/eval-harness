Columbia Journalism School found "institutional failure" in Rolling Stone's flawed UVA gang rape article, citing lapses in reporting, editing, and fact-checking. The magazine apologized, but legal experts debate whether UVA or Phi Kappa Psi can sue for defamation, with hurdles including proving harm and individual member liability. While public entities like UVA can't sue, fraternity members may argue the "small group" exception applies, though financial damages must be shown.
