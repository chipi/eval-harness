A North Carolina judge ruled Monday that Craig Hicks, charged with killing three Muslim college students in Chapel Hill in February, is eligible for the death penalty. Hicks, 46, the victims' neighbor, was indicted on three counts of first-degree murder over the shooting deaths of Yusor Mohammad, her husband Deah Shaddy Barakat and her sister Razan Mohammad Abu-Salha. Police cited a parking dispute as a possible factor, while the victims' families and federal investigators are examining whether it was a hate crime.
