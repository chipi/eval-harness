Turkish singer-songwriter Kayahan Acar died of cancer Friday in an Istanbul hospital at age 66, five days after his birthday. First diagnosed in 1990, the year he placed 17th at the Eurovision Song Contest, he saw the disease return in 2005 and 2014. Known for his love songs, he recorded nearly 20 albums, with 1991's "I Made a Vow" his most popular; his last performance was in Istanbul on Valentine's Day.
