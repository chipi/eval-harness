Philadelphia police have added an attempted murder charge against Nyia Parler, 41, accused of abandoning her 21-year-old quadriplegic son in Cobbs Creek Park for more than four days while she travelled to Maryland to visit her boyfriend. The man, who also has cerebral palsy, was found Friday lying in leaves with a blanket, Bible and wheelchair nearby, suffering dehydration, malnutrition and a back wound. Parler, in custody in Montgomery County, Maryland, cannot be extradited until she completes an unspecified treatment.
