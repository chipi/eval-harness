UNRWA Commissioner-General Pierre Krähenbühl will make an emergency visit Saturday to Syria's Yarmouk Palestinian refugee camp to assess the humanitarian crisis and meet Syrian officials, U.N. staff and displaced residents. The agency cites deepening concern for some 18,000 Palestinians and Syrians, including 3,500 children, trapped in the camp near Damascus. Monitors say ISIS and Al-Nusra Front control about 90% of Yarmouk, which has been largely cut off from aid since November 2013 amid reports of malnutrition and medical shortages.
