Kim Ki-Jong has been charged with attempted murder, assaulting a foreign envoy and business obstruction over the March 5 knife attack on U.S. Ambassador to South Korea Mark Lippert, a Seoul court official said. Lippert required 80 stitches for a 10-centimeter facial gash and suffered cuts to his left arm and hand, but no permanent nerve or function damage is expected. Police said Kim, who had a 2010 suspended sentence for throwing concrete at Japan's ambassador, attacked Lippert in opposition to joint U.S.-South Korean military drills; his trial must begin within 14 days.
