Hundreds of mourners, including police on motorcycles escorting the hearse, attended the funeral of Walter Scott in Summerville, South Carolina, where the church's pastor called the killing "an act of overt racism" and family members said his death would bring change in America.

Scott, a black father of four and Coast Guard veteran, was shot five times in the back last week by white North Charleston officer Michael Slager after a traffic stop for a broken taillight; Slager was fired and charged with murder after a bystander's cell phone video emerged.

Family lawyers said Scott fled because of a bench warrant over more than $18,000 in unpaid child support, while a defense attorney suggested a reported struggle over Slager's Taser could reduce the charge to manslaughter.
