Cuba is attending the Summit of the Americas in Panama for the first time since 1962, arriving with more than 100 officials after Washington dropped its objections. Cuban government supporters and dissidents brawled in Panama's streets Wednesday, denting Havana's efforts to present itself as an open society. The State Department has recommended removing Cuba from its list of state sponsors of terrorism — a step Havana calls a precondition for fully restoring diplomatic ties — while US officials say Obama and Raul Castro may "interact" at the two-day summit.
