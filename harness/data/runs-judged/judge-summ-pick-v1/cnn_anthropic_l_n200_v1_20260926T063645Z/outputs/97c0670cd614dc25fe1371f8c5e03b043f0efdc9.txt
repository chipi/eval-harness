Alaska Airlines has apologised after removing cancer patient Elizabeth Sedway from a Hawaii flight because she lacked a doctor's note clearing her to fly. Sedway said she was treated "as if I'm a criminal or contagious" after telling a crew member she sometimes felt weak. The airline said staff consulted its medical advisory service with her wellbeing in mind, but conceded the matter "could have been handled differently"; it refunded her family's tickets and paid for their overnight accommodation. Sedway, who flew home to San Jose late Tuesday, said the delay caused her to miss a chemotherapy session.
