NASA astrophysicist Amy Mainzer has named an asteroid after Pakistani education activist and Nobel Peace Prize laureate Malala Yousafzai. Mainzer discovered Asteroid 316201 in June 2010, earning naming rights, and dubbed it "316201 Malala." The dark, roughly 4-kilometre-wide body orbits the sun between Mars and Jupiter every five and a half years. Mainzer said few asteroids have been named for the contributions of women, particularly women of colour.
