Eyewitness video of North Charleston, South Carolina, officer Michael Slager fatally shooting unarmed black man Walter Scott has exposed discrepancies in initial police accounts. Slager, who said Scott seized his Taser, has been fired and charged with murder; witness Feidin Santana says Scott never took the weapon. Police reports also conflict over whether CPR was performed on Scott, and the department has declined further comment citing a state investigation.
