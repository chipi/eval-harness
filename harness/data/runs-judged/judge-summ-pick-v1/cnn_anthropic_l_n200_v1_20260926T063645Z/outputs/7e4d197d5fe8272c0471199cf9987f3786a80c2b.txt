Jodi Arias was sentenced Monday to life in prison without the possibility of release for the 2008 murder of her ex-boyfriend Travis Alexander. The judge decided the sentence after two juries deadlocked on whether to impose the death penalty, taking execution off the table. Arias, 34, told the court she was "disgusted and repulsed" with herself; she will serve her term at a state prison near Goodyear, Arizona.
