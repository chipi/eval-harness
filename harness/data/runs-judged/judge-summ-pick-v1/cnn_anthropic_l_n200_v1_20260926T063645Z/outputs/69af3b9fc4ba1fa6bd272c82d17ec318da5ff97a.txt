Georgia Powers, the first woman and first person of color elected to the Kentucky State Senate, dismisses Rand Paul's presidential launch and his claim to be broadening the GOP's appeal to minority voters. She cites his questioning of the Civil Rights Act, his opposition to a stronger Voting Rights Act, his call to abolish the Education Department, and his stances on abortion and DREAMers. Powers says his record is "outdated" and disqualifying, and that "the American people deserve better."
