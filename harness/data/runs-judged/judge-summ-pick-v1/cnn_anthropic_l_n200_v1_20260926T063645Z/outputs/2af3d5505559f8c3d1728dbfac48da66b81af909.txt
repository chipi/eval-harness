Katie, a giraffe at the Dallas Zoo, gave birth to a roughly 6-foot-tall calf on Friday evening after an hour-long labor streamed live online by Animal Planet using 10 cameras. The newborn's gender and condition weren't immediately announced, but it was seen moving its ears, trying to stand and attempting to nurse. The calf follows a 15-month gestation and joins 4-year-old sister Jamie in the zoo's 12-giraffe herd.
