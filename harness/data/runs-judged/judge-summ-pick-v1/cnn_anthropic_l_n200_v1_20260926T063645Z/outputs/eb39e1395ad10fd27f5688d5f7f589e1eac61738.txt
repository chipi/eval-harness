Selena's family is creating a digital, hologram-like version of the late Tejano singer, 20 years after her death, using technology from Nevada firm Acrovirt LLC. Dubbed "Selena the One," the project aims to release new songs and videos, collaborate with current artists and tour in 2018. An Indiegogo campaign seeking $500,000 launches April 16, ahead of the Fiesta de la Flor tribute event in Corpus Christi, Texas, on April 17-18.
