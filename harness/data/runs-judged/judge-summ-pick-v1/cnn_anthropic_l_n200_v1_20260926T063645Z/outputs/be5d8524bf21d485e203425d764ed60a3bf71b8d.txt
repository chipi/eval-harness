Attorney General Eric Holder issued a memo Friday barring all Justice Department personnel from soliciting, procuring or accepting commercial sex, even in jurisdictions where prostitution is legal. Holder said paying for sex fuels demand for human trafficking and threatens the department's core mission; violators face suspension or dismissal, on or off duty.

The directive follows an inspector general report that found DEA agents overseas attended sex parties with prostitutes paid for by drug cartels, and that such misconduct cases were poorly handled.
