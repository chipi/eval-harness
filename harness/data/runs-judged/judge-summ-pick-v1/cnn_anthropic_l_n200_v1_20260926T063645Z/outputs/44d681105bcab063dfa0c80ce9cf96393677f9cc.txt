A Connecticut juvenile court judge on Wednesday denied motions to release 17-year-old "Cassandra C." from state custody and to allow visitation, her attorney said. The teen, now in remission after nearly six months of court-ordered chemotherapy for Hodgkin lymphoma, has been hospitalized since December, when a judge placed her in the custody of the Department of Children and Families after she fled treatment. Officials say the neglect petition will be withdrawn and she can return home once her final round of chemo ends, expected late April; her lawyers are weighing another appeal.
