Al-Shabaab claimed responsibility for an attack on Garissa University College in eastern Kenya, killing many and taking hostages, in the latest sign of worsening East African security. The Somalia-based group, which pledged allegiance to al Qaeda in 2012, has staged cross-border raids in Kenya since 2011, including the 2013 Westgate mall massacre. Analysts say military action alone won't stop it, as poverty, corruption, poor governance and exclusion continue to fuel recruitment.
