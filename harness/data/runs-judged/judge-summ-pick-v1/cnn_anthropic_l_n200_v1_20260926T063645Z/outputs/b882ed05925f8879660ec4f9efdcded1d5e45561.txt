Tuesday marked one year since Boko Haram militants seized 276 schoolgirls from a secondary school in Chibok, northeastern Nigeria; about 50 escaped and more than 200 remain missing.

Nigerian President-elect Muhammadu Buhari, who has pledged to crush the group, offered prayers for the victims, while Malala Yousafzai told the girls they would not be forgotten.

Boko Haram still holds territory in the northeast, and UNICEF says 800,000 children have been displaced by the conflict. Amnesty International reports abductions and killings continue.
