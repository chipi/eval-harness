Twitter fans marked "Rex Manning Day" on April 8, a nod to the 1995 film "Empire Records," coinciding with a "Lost" anniversary date.

CNN lists other dates immortalised by movies, including April 14 from "Titanic," April 25 from "Miss Congeniality," and October 3, known as "Mean Girls Day."

October 21, 2015, is also flagged as the date Marty McFly travels to in "Back to the Future Part II."
