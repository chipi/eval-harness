Indian police arrested four employees of ethnic-wear chain FabIndia after federal education minister Smriti Irani spotted a surveillance camera pointed at a changing room in a Goa store. Investigators confirmed the camera could photograph customers in the changing room; the store was sealed and the manager remained at large. The staff face voyeurism and privacy breach charges carrying up to three years in jail. FabIndia said it was "shocked" and would cooperate with police.
