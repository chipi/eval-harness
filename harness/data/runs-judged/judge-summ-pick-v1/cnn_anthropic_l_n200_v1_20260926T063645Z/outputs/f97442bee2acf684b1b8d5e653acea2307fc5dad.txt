Irish bookmaker Paddy Power has apologised after tweeting that Newcastle "have suffered more Kop beatings over the last 20 years than an unarmed African-American male," a reference to US police killings. The firm deleted the post and said "it was a joke, and no offense was meant," but social media users demanded the author be sacked. The company, known for publicity stunts, previously drew criticism for backing David Ginola's FIFA presidential bid and for a "money back if he walks" offer on Oscar Pistorius's trial.
