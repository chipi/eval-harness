Two Troy University students, Ryan Calhoun and Delonte Martistee, have been charged with sexual battery by multiple perpetrators over an alleged gang rape during spring break in Panama City Beach, Florida. Police in Troy, Alabama, discovered video of the alleged assault while investigating an unrelated shooting and passed it to the Bay County Sheriff's Office, which says the attack occurred behind a beach club between March 10 and 12. Both men have been suspended from the university, and investigators expect more arrests.
