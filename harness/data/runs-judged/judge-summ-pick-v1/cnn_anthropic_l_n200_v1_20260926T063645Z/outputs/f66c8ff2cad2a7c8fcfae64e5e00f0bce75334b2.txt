Liana Barrientos, 39, has been married 10 times — nine between 1999 and 2002 — and is believed to still be married to four men. She pleaded not guilty in the Bronx to two counts of offering a false instrument for filing, over a 2010 marriage license on which she claimed it was her "first and only" marriage. Prosecutors say the marriages were part of an immigration scam; she faces up to four years in prison and returns to court May 18.
