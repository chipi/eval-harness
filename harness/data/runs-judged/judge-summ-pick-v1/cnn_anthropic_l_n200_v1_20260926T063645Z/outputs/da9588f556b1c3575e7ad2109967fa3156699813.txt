A 21-year-old quadriplegic man with cerebral palsy was found in Philadelphia's Cobbs Creek Park after allegedly being left there for more than four days with only a blanket and a Bible. Police say his mother abandoned him Monday and travelled to Maryland to visit her boyfriend, telling family and officers her son was with her. He is hospitalised with dehydration, malnutrition, eye problems and an infected back wound; his mother was arrested in Maryland and faces charges including kidnapping and aggravated assault.
