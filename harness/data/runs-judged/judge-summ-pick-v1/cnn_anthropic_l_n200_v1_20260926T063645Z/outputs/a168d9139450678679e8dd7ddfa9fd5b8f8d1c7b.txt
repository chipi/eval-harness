Conditions in Damascus' Yarmouk camp have worsened since ISIS fighters stormed the Palestinian district two weeks ago, prompting government shelling and barrel bombing, residents say. ISIS has since withdrawn, leaving al Qaeda-linked Jabhat al-Nusra in control, an activist told CNN. About 18,000 of the camp's original 100,000-plus residents remain, largely cut off from food, water and aid, according to UNRWA.
