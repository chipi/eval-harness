Authorities in Goldsboro, North Carolina, are investigating whether the fatal shooting of Wayne Community College print shop operator Ron Lane was a hate crime. Police say suspect Kenneth Morgan Stancil III, a former work-study student dropped from the program in March, shot Lane once with a shotgun Monday; relatives said Lane was gay, and investigators are examining possible ties between Stancil and a white supremacist gang. Stancil was arrested early Tuesday while sleeping on a beach in Daytona, Florida, and is expected to be returned to North Carolina to face charges.
