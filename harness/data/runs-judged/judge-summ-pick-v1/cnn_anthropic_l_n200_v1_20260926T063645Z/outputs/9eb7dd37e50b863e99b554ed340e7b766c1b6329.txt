A Delaware father is in stable condition while his two teenage sons remain critical after the family fell ill at a rented villa at the Sirenusa resort in St. John, U.S. Virgin Islands. EPA preliminary tests found methyl bromide, a highly toxic pesticide banned for indoor use, in the unit; the boys' mother was treated and released. The Justice Department has opened a criminal investigation, and the rental agent has cut ties with pest-control firm Terminix.
