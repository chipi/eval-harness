7-Eleven is hosting its first Bring-Your-Own-Cup Slurpee Day Saturday, allowing customers to fill any sanitary container for $1.49. The promotion, which runs from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., kicks off peak Slurpee season with size restrictions enforced by in-store displays. The event is separate from the chain's annual Free Slurpee Day on July 11.
