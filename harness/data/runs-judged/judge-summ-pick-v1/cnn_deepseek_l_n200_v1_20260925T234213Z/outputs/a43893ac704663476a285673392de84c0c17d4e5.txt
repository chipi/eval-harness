Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" featuring Bruno Mars leads the Billboard Hot 100 for a 13th week, becoming the longest-reigning No. 1 of the 2010s and one of only 10 singles in history to achieve the feat. The track simultaneously tops the Digital Songs, Radio Songs, and Streaming Songs charts for a record-extending ninth week. Meanwhile, newcomer Natalie La Rose enters the top 10 with her debut hit "Somebody."
