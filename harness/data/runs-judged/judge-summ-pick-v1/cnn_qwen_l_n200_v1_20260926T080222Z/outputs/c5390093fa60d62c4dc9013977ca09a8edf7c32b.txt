Easter, celebrating Christ's Resurrection, falls on the first Sunday after the first full moon following the vernal equinox, leading to varying dates and separate observances between Western and Eastern Orthodox Christians. The holiday’s symbols—eggs and the bunny—stem from medieval European customs tied to Lenten traditions and spring fertility lore, while its timing aligns with Passover due to shared lunar calculations. Pope Francis marked Easter with calls for global peace, and a recent blood moon coincided with the holiday, reflecting its astronomical roots.
