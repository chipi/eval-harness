Cuba attends the Summit of the Americas for the first time since 1962 after the U.S. dropped its opposition, amid ongoing talks to restore diplomatic ties and reopen embassies. Tensions flared in Panama as pro- and anti-Castro groups clashed, while Cuba insists full normalization hinges on its removal from the U.S. terrorism sponsor list—a move the State Department has recommended. Despite warming U.S.-Cuba relations, Havana continues to strongly back Venezuela, a key ally, even as Washington downplays Caracas as a security threat.
