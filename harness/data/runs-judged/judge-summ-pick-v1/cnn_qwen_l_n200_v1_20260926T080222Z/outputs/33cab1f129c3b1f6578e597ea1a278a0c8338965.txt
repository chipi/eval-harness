Don McLean’s original manuscript for “American Pie” sold for $1.2 million at Christie’s, marking the third-highest price for an American literary manuscript. The 16-page draft, containing lyrics and notes, was auctioned as McLean, nearing 70, sought to secure his family’s future. The song, inspired by Buddy Holly’s death and long subject to interpretation, topped charts in 1972 despite its 8-minute length.
