Louis Jordan, 37, was rescued after drifting at sea for 66 days following a storm that capsized his sailboat off South Carolina. Despite broken supplies, scarce food and water, and multiple further capsizings, he survived by catching fish with laundry and collecting rainwater, and was found in surprisingly good condition by the crew of a container ship. Coast Guard officials confirmed his account, and he was hospitalized in Norfolk, Virginia, before planning to return to his marina home.
