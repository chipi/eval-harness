Ted Cruz, positioning himself as the leading GOP evangelical candidate, campaigned aggressively in Iowa, leveraging his faith-based appeal to connect with the state’s influential Christian conservative base. He aims to unite evangelicals, tea party supporters, and libertarians into a winning coalition, while other potential rivals like Huckabee and Santorum also eye the same bloc. With religious liberty and social issues driving engagement, Cruz’s team sees untapped potential in mobilizing evangelical turnout ahead of the 2016 caucuses.
