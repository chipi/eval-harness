Selena’s family is developing “Selena the One,” a digital embodiment using advanced hologram-like technology to walk, talk, sing, and dance as the late Tejano star. The project, led by tech firm Acrovirt LLC, aims to release new music, collaborate with artists, and tour in 2018—without replacing Selena but extending her legacy. A $500,000 Indiegogo campaign launches April 16 to support the initiative.
