**ISIS militants stormed Syria’s Yarmouk camp near Damascus, triggering brutal government airstrikes and shelling that have worsened conditions for the 18,000 remaining residents. Besieged by pro-regime forces and torn between Islamist factions, the camp—once home to 100,000 Palestinians—faces starvation, relentless attacks, and near-total isolation, with aid access severely restricted. Civilians, trapped amid ruins, vow to stay despite the escalating violence.**
