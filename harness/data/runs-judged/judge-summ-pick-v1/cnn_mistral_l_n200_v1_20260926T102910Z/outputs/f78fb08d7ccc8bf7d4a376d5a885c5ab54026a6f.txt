**Wire Summary:**
Mindy Kaling’s brother, Vijay Chokalingam, admitted to posing as Black to gain medical school admission in the 2000s, claiming it exposed affirmative action "privilege"—though he was only accepted to a mid-tier school. Critics argue his stunt ignored systemic racism and the broader need for diversity in medicine. The controversy follows other high-profile cases of false racial claims, sparking debate over identity and equity.
