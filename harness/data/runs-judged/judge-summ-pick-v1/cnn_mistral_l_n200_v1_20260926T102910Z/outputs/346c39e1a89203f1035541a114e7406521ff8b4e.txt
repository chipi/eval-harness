UNRWA chief Pierre Krähenbühl will visit Syria’s besieged Yarmouk refugee camp Saturday to assess the dire humanitarian crisis affecting 18,000 civilians, including 3,500 children, amid ongoing conflict and ISIS/al-Nusra control. The camp, once home to 160,000, faces severe aid shortages, malnutrition, and recent barrel bomb attacks. Krähenbühl will meet officials and displaced residents to explore relief efforts.
