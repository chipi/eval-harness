Jia Jiang, a Chinese-born American, quit his six-figure job to follow his entrepreneurial dreams, and in response to his own fear of rejection, embarked on a project where he subjected himself to 100 days of rejection. His blog and subsequent book, "Rejection Proof," aim to help others overcome their fear of rejection and turn it into opportunity. Jiang shares eight tips for making rejection work for you, including reframing rejection as a numbers game and not taking it personally.
