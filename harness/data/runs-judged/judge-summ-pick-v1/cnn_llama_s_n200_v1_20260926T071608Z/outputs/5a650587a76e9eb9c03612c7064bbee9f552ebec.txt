Tensions are running high at the VII Summit of the Americas in Panama City, where US President Barack Obama's meeting with Cuba's Raul Castro is being overshadowed by Venezuela's President Nicolas Maduro's accusations of a US plot to overthrow him. Maduro has rallied support from other Latin American leaders, including Cuba, after the US imposed sanctions on Venezuelan officials for human rights abuses. The developments have turned the summit into a "triangle of tension" between the US, Cuba, and Venezuela.
