Liana Barrientos, 39, has been charged with two counts of offering a false instrument for filing after she lied on a 2010 marriage license application, claiming it was her first marriage. Barrientos has been married 10 times, mostly in a short period between 1999 and 2002, and is believed to still be married to four men. Prosecutors say the marriages were part of an immigration scam to gain permanent residence status for some of her husbands.
