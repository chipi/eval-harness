Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" featuring Bruno Mars has reached a 13th week at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, the longest-leading song of the 2010s. The song is one of only 10 to spend at least 13 weeks at No. 1 in the chart's history. Natalie La Rose's "Somebody" featuring Jeremih debuts in the top 10 at No. 10.
