China formally charged former security chief Zhou Yongkang with bribery, abuse of power and leaking state secrets, making him the highest-ranking Communist Party official ever prosecuted for corruption. Zhou, 72, once controlled China's police, spy agencies and courts before his 2012 retirement; his trial will be held in Tianjin. Analysts view his downfall as a landmark moment under President Xi Jinping's anti-corruption campaign, linking Zhou's case to that of disgraced leader Bo Xilai.
