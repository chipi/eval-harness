Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied reports of cockpit video footage from Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by Bild and Paris Match magazines. Separately, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" to its flight school in 2009, raising questions about why he was allowed to fly. Investigators are examining whether Lubitz feared losing his pilot's license due to medical issues as a possible motive for the crash that killed all 150 aboard.
