Most Americans receive tax refunds averaging nearly $3,000, and free filing software has made the process easier, leaving taxpayers with positive feelings about Tax Day despite the system's complexities. This "happy ending" effect, similar to psychological findings about colonoscopy patients, masks deeper unfairness in the tax code, which burdens dual-income married couples and wage earners while allowing the wealthy to minimize taxes on capital gains. Service providers profit from this dynamic by offering free filing while upselling additional services once refunds are secured.
