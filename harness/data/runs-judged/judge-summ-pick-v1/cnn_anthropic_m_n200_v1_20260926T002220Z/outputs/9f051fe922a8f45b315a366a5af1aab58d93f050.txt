Online bloggers and journalists across Asia face growing legal persecution and violence as authoritarian governments crack down on dissent, with Singapore, Malaysia, China and Vietnam jailing critics under various charges. In Bangladesh, the danger has turned deadly, with two bloggers hacked to death in the past five weeks for writing critically about Islam, part of a pattern of killings carried out with near-total impunity. CPJ data shows online journalists are increasingly targeted worldwide, as they operate outside the protections and constraints of mainstream media.
