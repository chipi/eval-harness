Jesus and his disciples lived at subsistence level, relying on begging, hospitality, and donations rather than steady income, according to biblical scholars. Wealthy supporters—including women like Mary Magdalene and Joanna, and men like Joseph of Arimathea and Nicodemus—helped fund his itinerant ministry from their own resources. This financial arrangement, hinted at throughout the Gospels, reveals the practical realities behind Jesus' teachings on poverty and wealth.
