Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu criticized the Iran nuclear deal on CNN, arguing for continued pressure rather than accepting "this bad deal or war," while insisting his opposition isn't rooted in personal animosity toward Obama. Democrats like Sen. Dianne Feinstein urged Netanyahu to tone down his rhetoric, while Republicans like Sen. Lindsey Graham argued for maintaining sanctions until a new president negotiates a better agreement. Energy Secretary Ernest Moniz defended the framework, saying it would extend Iran's nuclear "breakout" time and block pathways to a bomb.
