"Game of Thrones" returns for its fifth season Sunday, premiering simultaneously in 170 countries for the first time. Expect major story arcs to converge, including Stannis's quest for the Iron Throne intersecting with the Night's Watch, and Tyrion's path crossing with Daenerys'. The season, which surpasses George Martin's book material, will also spotlight the Stark daughters and Cersei, plus growing unrest among Westeros' common people.
