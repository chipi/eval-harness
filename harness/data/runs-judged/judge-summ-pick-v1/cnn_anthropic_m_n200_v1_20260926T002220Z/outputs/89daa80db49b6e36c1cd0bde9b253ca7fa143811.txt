Kansas Gov. Sam Brownback signed a first-of-its-kind law banning the dilation and evacuation abortion procedure, commonly used in second-trimester abortions, except to protect the mother's life or health. Supporters called it a landmark step in restricting abortion, while Planned Parenthood and other critics warned it endangers women and represents the nation's most restrictive abortion law. Both sides expect further legislative and legal battles over similar measures in other states.
