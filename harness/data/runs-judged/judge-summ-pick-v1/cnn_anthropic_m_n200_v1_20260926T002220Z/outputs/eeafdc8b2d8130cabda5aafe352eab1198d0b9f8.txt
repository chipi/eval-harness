Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil botched "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas Outlaws game this week, joining a long history of memorable national anthem performances. Whitney Houston's Super Bowl XXV rendition set the gold standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 performance drew boos and presidential condemnation. Jimi Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock version and Michael Bolton's forgotten-lyrics attempt in 2003 round out the anthem's most notorious moments.
