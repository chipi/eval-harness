A North Carolina judge ruled Monday that Craig Hicks' case is "death penalty qualified" in the killings of three Muslim college students in Chapel Hill. Hicks, 46, is charged with first-degree murder in the February shooting deaths of his neighbors Yusor Mohammad, Deah Shaddy Barakat and Razan Mohammad Abu-Salha. Authorities have cited a parking dispute as a possible factor but have not ruled out investigating the case as a hate crime.
