Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash for signing a "religious freedom" law seen as enabling discrimination against LGBT people, a move meant to bolster his conservative credentials ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run. Similar bills are advancing in over a dozen states, and potential GOP candidates like Cruz, Huckabee, Jindal and Walker have echoed similar anti-same-sex-marriage stances, ensuring LGBT rights will be a major issue in the primary. Despite growing national support for gay rights, the controversy shows conservative opposition remains a potent political force.
