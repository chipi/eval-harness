Saudi-led coalition airstrikes have pounded Houthi-held Sanaa and other Yemeni areas for over a week, killing roughly 600 people, knocking out power for 16 million, and prompting the Red Cross to appeal for a humanitarian ceasefire. The UN Security Council discussed a Russian draft resolution calling for a halt to strikes but reached no decision, while chaos has also allowed al Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula to expand its control in eastern Yemen.
