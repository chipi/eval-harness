Marvel and other studios released a flurry of superhero images and casting news within 24 hours, including a poster of Paul Bettany as the Vision in "Avengers: Age of Ultron" and a look at Charlie Cox's Daredevil suit ahead of the Netflix series premiere. Additional reveals included Ben Hardy as Angel in "X-Men: Apocalypse" and Jamie Bell's CGI version of The Thing in the rebooted "Fantastic Four."
