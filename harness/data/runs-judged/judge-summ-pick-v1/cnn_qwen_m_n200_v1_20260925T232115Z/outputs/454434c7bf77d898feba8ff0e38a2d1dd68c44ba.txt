New psychological analysis confirms William Shakespeare and John Fletcher authored the 1728 play "Double Falsehood," largely debunking claims that publisher Lewis Theobald forged the work. Researchers used computer software to compare linguistic patterns, finding a decisive match with Shakespeare’s style in the first half and a shared signature with Fletcher in the second. While experts praise the study's objectivity, some scholars caution that the debate over the play's provenance may persist among skeptics.
