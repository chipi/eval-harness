Nina Dobrev announced her departure from "The Vampire Diaries" after six seasons, sparking backlash from fans concerned about the show's continuation and the fate of the "Delena" romance. While producer Julie Plec voiced full support for Dobrev's decision to pursue new opportunities, many viewers expressed anger and disappointment over the impending exit. Dobrev urged fans to remain engaged through the season finale in May, hinting at unexpected developments before her final episode.
