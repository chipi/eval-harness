Federal prosecutors have charged real estate heir Robert Durst with unlawful firearm possession after authorities discovered a revolver in his New Orleans hotel room. The 71-year-old, currently held without bail on a first-degree murder charge for the 2000 killing of Susan Berman, faces up to 10 years in prison if convicted on the new federal count. Durst awaits extradition to Los Angeles while his defense cites serious health issues, including hydrocephalus and cancer, amid ongoing FBI scrutiny of other cold cases linked to him.
