French prosecutors denied the existence of an onboard video from the Germanwings crash, contradicting media reports while confirming co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had a history of severe depression. Lufthansa acknowledged it knew of Lubitz's prior mental health struggles during his training, as investigators explore fears over his medical fitness as a potential motive. Recovery teams continue searching the French Alps site, with officials expecting to identify all 150 victims via DNA analysis by week's end.
