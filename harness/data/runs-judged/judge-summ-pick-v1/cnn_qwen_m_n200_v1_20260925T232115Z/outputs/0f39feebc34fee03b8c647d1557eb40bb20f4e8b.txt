Maryland police stated Nyia Parler cannot be extradited to Philadelphia on charges of abandoning her quadriplegic son in the woods until she completes an unspecified medical treatment. Parler faces multiple assault and endangerment counts after allegedly leaving her disabled 21-year-old son in Cobbs Creek Park for four days while visiting a boyfriend. The victim was discovered dehydrated and malnourished but is currently recovering in a hospital under the care of relatives.
