Amidst backlash over Indiana's new Religious Freedom Restoration Act, experts note that similar state laws have yielded few legal victories, primarily benefiting religious minorities rather than Christian denominations. Key precedents include Supreme Court protections for Native American eagle feathers and Brazilian sacramental tea, alongside settlements for Sikh and Jewish practitioners. Conversely, courts have rejected some claims, such as a Chicago church group's challenge to zoning laws, highlighting the limited scope of these religious liberty provisions.
