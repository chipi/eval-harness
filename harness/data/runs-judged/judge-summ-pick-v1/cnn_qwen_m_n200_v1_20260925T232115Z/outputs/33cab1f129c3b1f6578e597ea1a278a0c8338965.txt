Don McLean’s original manuscript for "American Pie" sold for $1.2 million at a Christie's auction, marking the third-highest price ever paid for an American literary manuscript. The 70-year-old singer-songwriter authorized the sale to secure financial stability for his family, noting that the 16-page draft includes extensive notes and unused lyrics. This transaction trails only Bob Dylan’s "Like a Rolling Stone" manuscript, which previously set the record for popular music manuscripts at $2 million.
