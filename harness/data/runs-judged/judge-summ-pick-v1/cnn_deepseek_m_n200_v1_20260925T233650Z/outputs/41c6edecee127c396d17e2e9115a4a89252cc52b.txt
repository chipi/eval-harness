California regulators fined Pacific Gas & Electric $1.6 billion Thursday for unsafe operation of its gas transmission system, including a 2010 pipeline rupture in San Bruno that killed eight people. Most of the record penalty—$850 million—is earmarked for pipeline safety infrastructure improvements, with $50 million for other safety remedies. PG&E CEO Tony Earley said the company is working to become the safest energy company in the US and has already paid more than $500 million in victim claims.
