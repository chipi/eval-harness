An Etihad Airways flight from Abu Dhabi to New York lost a passenger's pet cat after its carrier was damaged, allowing the animal to escape on the grounds of JFK Airport. The cat, Felix, remains missing nearly a week later as the airline investigates and works with ground handlers and third-party specialists to locate him. The owners, who paid $1,200 to ship the cat during their relocation, say they will continue searching until they have answers.
