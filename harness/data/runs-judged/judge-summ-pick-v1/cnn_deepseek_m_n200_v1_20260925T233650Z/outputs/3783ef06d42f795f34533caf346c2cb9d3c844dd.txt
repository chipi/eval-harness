A federal grand jury indicted Robert Durst, 71, on a charge of unlawful firearm possession after a .38-caliber revolver was allegedly found in his New Orleans hotel room last month. Durst, already held without bail on a first-degree murder charge in California, faces up to 10 years in prison on the new charge. He also faces state weapons and drug charges in New Orleans and awaits extradition to Los Angeles in the 2000 killing of Susan Berman.
