Saudi-led coalition airstrikes and ground fighting in Yemen have killed an estimated 600 people in just over a week, with Houthi rebels still controlling Sanaa despite heavy infrastructure damage and power outages affecting 16 million residents. The ICRC has called for a humanitarian ceasefire to deliver medical aid, but a supply flight remains grounded in Djibouti, while the UN Security Council adjourned without action on a Russian draft resolution to halt the strikes. Deposed President Hadi, holed up in Saudi Arabia, fired his former army chief of staff in a move with no practical effect, as AQAP exploits the chaos to expand its control in eastern Yemen.
