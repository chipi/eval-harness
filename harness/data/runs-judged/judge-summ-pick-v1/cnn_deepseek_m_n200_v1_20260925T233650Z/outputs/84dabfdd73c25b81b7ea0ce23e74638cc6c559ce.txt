Amnesty International's report "Their Lives on the Line" urges authorities to address attacks on Afghan women's rights activists, who face persecution from the Taliban, warlords, and government officials. The report follows the brutal mob killing of Farkhunda in Kabul, where women served as pallbearers for the first time, and cites U.N. figures showing a 20% rise in women killed in 2013. Amnesty says the Afghan government has done little to protect activists, with perpetrators rarely prosecuted.
