Twisted Sister has announced that its 2016 tour will be its last, titled "Forty and F*ck It" to mark the band's 40th anniversary. Singer Dee Snider confirmed the farewell on Facebook and said drummer Mike Portnoy will replace the late A.J. Pero, who died March 20. The band will play two shows in Pero's honor, in Las Vegas and Sayreville, New Jersey.
