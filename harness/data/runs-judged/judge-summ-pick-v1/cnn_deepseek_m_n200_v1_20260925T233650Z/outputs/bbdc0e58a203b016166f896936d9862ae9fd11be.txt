Kenya has frozen 86 accounts linked to suspected Al-Shabaab supporters following last week's Garissa university massacre that killed 147 people. The government is tracking suspect finances and launched airstrikes on Al-Shabaab camps in Somalia, while offering a $215,000 reward for the alleged attack organizer. Kenyans mourned the victims at Nairobi's Uhuru Park with 147 crosses and used #147notjustanumber to honor the dead.
