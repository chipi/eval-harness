Don McLean's "American Pie" sold for $1.2 million to an unnamed buyer . Bob Greene: The song is a folk ballad, beer-hall chant and high-art rock . Greene: McLean was a paperboy when Buddy Holly, Ritchie Valens and J.P. "The Big Bopper" died in a plane crash in 1959 . He says the song is alive and joyful, yet fretful about a world gone wrong .
