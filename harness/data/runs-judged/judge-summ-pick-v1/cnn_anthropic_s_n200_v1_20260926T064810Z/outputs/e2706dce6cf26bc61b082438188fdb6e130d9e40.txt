Amnesty International reports that governments worldwide are increasingly using the death penalty under the guise of fighting terrorism, citing examples from Pakistan and China. While global executions fell 22% in 2014 to 607 cases, death sentences surged 28% to 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria. The U.S. remains among the world's top executioners alongside Iran, Iraq, and Saudi Arabia, with China estimated to execute thousands annually.
