Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, who has been negotiating a nuclear deal with U.S. Secretary of State John Kerry, received a hero's welcome upon returning to Iran. Despite his polished public image, less-known facts about Zarif include discrepancies in his official birth year, his education in the United States during Iran's revolution, and his involvement with the Alavi Foundation under federal investigation. Zarif has spent more time negotiating with Kerry than any other foreign minister as the two work toward a June 30 deadline to finalize nuclear agreement details.
