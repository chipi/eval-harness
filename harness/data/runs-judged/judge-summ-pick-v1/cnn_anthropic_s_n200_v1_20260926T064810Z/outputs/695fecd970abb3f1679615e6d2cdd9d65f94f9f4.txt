7-Eleven is hosting its first Bring-Your-Own-Cup Slurpee Day on Saturday, allowing customers to fill any personal container—from buckets to trophies—for $1.49, the price of a medium Slurpee. Containers must fit through a 10-inch hole and be sanitary. The promotion kicks off peak Slurpee season and differs from the chain's traditional Free Slurpee Day in July.
