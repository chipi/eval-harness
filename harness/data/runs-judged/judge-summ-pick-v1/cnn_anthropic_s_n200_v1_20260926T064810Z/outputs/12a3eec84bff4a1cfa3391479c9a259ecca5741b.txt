NASA astrophysicist Amy Mainzer named an asteroid after Pakistani education activist Malala Yousafzai, who survived a Taliban shooting and won the Nobel Peace Prize. Asteroid 316201 Malala, discovered by Mainzer in 2010, orbits between Mars and Jupiter every 5.5 years and is about 4 kilometers in diameter. Mainzer chose to honor Malala because few asteroids are named after women, particularly women of color, and because educating girls globally increases human potential.
