Major U.S. corporations including Walmart, Apple, and NASCAR have publicly opposed religious freedom bills in Arkansas and Indiana, warning they could enable discrimination against LGBTQ individuals. Walmart's stance in its home state of Arkansas proved decisive, prompting Gov. Asa Hutchinson to pledge to veto the legislation unless revised. The corporate backlash reflects shifting American attitudes on gay rights and signals that major businesses now view LGBTQ equality as economically beneficial.
