Jesus preached against wealth while his ministry relied on financial support from wealthy followers, including women like Mary Magdalene and Joanna who provided resources from their own means. The Jesus community maintained a common purse and likely survived through begging, hospitality, and donations from affluent supporters despite his teachings on the perils of riches. Scholars suggest the ministry functioned at a subsistence level, with Jesus and his disciples walking, sleeping in friends' homes, and eating what was shared or caught.
