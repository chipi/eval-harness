Comedian Chris Rock tweeted about being stopped by police three times in seven weeks, highlighting racial disparities in traffic stops that disproportionately affect Black drivers. The posts sparked debate on social media, with some praising his documentation of the issue and others, including actor Isaiah Washington, suggesting he adapt his behavior—such as driving less expensive cars—to avoid profiling. Rock's commentary reflects ongoing frustration over police treatment of Black Americans, particularly following high-profile deaths like those of Eric Garner and Michael Brown.
