Richard Dysart, the Emmy-winning actor best known for playing senior partner Leland McKenzie on NBC's "L.A. Law" (1986-1994), has died at 86. Dysart won an Emmy in 1992 for the role and appeared in every episode of the acclaimed legal drama. He also had a prolific career in film and television, frequently portraying authority figures and doctors in classics like "The Thing," "Wall Street" and "Being There."
