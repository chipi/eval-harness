Kenya froze 86 accounts linked to Al-Shabaab supporters following the militant group's massacre of 147 people at a Garissa university. The government is tracking terror financing and launched airstrikes against Al-Shabaab camps in Somalia, while offering a $215,000 reward for information on the attack's alleged organizer. Kenyans mourned the victims—mostly students—at a memorial service in Nairobi as authorities faced criticism over the delayed response to the siege.
