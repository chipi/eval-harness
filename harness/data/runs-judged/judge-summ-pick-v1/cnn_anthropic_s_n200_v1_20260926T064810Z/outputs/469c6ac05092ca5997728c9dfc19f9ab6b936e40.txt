French prosecutors deny awareness of alleged cockpit video from Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting reports by German and French media outlets claiming to have viewed footage recovered from a phone at the crash site. Co-pilot Andreas Lubitz, accused of deliberately crashing the plane in the French Alps killing all 150 aboard, had disclosed a history of severe depression to his airline's flight training school in 2009, though he held a valid medical certificate at the time of the crash.
