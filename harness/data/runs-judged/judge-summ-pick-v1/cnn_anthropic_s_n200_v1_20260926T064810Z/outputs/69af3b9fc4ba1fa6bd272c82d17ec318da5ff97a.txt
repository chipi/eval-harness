Kentucky State Senator Georgia M. Davis McIntyre, the first woman and person of color elected to the state senate in 1967, criticized Rand Paul's presidential campaign announcement, saying his message of Republican inclusion rings hollow given his record of questioning civil rights and voting rights legislation. McIntyre argued that Paul's positions on the Civil Rights Act, voting rights, education, and immigration protections demonstrate outdated views that contradict his stated goal of expanding the party's appeal to minority voters. She concluded that Paul's record disqualifies him from the presidency and that Americans deserve a leader committed to protecting democratic progress.
