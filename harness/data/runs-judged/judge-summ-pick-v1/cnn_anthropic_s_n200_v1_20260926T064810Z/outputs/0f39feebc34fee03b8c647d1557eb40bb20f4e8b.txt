A Maryland woman was arrested after allegedly abandoning her 21-year-old quadriplegic son in Philadelphia woods for over four days while visiting her boyfriend in Maryland; the man was found covered in leaves suffering from dehydration and malnutrition. The mother, Nyia Parler, cannot be extradited to face charges including aggravated assault until she completes unspecified treatment in Maryland. The son, who also has cerebral palsy and cannot communicate, was hospitalized and is being cared for by his aunts who have sought guardianship.
