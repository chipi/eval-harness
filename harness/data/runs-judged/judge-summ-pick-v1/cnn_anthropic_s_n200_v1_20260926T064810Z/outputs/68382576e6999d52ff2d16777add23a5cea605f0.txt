A gang pulled off a daring heist at a London jewelry vault over Easter weekend, reminiscent of the 1971 Baker Street bank robbery—the first major physical heist in the city in over 40 years. Former Scotland Yard commander Roy Ramm explains that sophisticated criminals are typically working-class individuals motivated purely by money, relying on meticulous planning, inside information, and trusted networks to execute high-value crimes. Police will investigate possible inside involvement, analyze forensic evidence and CCTV footage, and track the movement of known criminals to solve the Hatton Garden raid.
