Ramalinga Raju, former chairman of Indian software giant Satyam Computers Services, has been sentenced to seven years in jail along with nine others for their roles in a $1.6 billion fraud case involving inflated profits and fictitious assets. The 2009 scandal, compared to the Enron case, devastated the fourth-largest Indian software services provider and its investors. Raju, who founded Satyam in 1987, was also fined $804,000.
