NASA scientists outline plans to search for extraterrestrial life over the next decade, with an estimated 40 billion Earth-like planets in habitable zones across the Milky Way. While primitive life forms may be common, intelligent civilizations capable of communication are likely rare due to the vast timescales required for their development and the short duration of their technological capabilities. Advanced telescopes like the James Webb Space Telescope will search for biosignatures in exoplanet atmospheres, though meaningful contact with alien civilizations remains highly improbable.
