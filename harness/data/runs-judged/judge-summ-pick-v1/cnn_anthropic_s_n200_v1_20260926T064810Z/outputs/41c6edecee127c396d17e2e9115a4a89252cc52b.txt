California regulators ordered Pacific Gas & Electric to pay a record $1.6 billion penalty for unsafe operation of its gas transmission system, including a 2010 pipeline rupture in San Bruno that killed eight people and injured over 50 others. Most of the penalty—$850 million—will fund pipeline safety infrastructure improvements. PG&E said it has already paid over $500 million in claims to victims and replaced more than 800 miles of pipe since the explosion.
