An Iranian military observation aircraft flew within 50 yards of a U.S. Navy helicopter over the Persian Gulf in an unsafe maneuver that U.S. officials believe may have been ordered by a local Iranian commander rather than authorized by top leadership. The incident, which occurred during nuclear negotiations between the U.S. and Iran, raised concerns about potential escalation, though no shots were fired and the helicopter safely disengaged. U.S. officials are considering filing a formal protest and noted the Iranian aircraft disappeared for two weeks afterward, suggesting the local commander may have been reprimanded.
