A Philadelphia mother was arrested after allegedly abandoning her 21-year-old quadriplegic son with cerebral palsy in Cobbs Creek Park for over four days while she visited her boyfriend in Maryland. The man was found suffering from dehydration, malnutrition, and other injuries; his mother now faces multiple charges including aggravated assault, kidnapping, and neglect. Police say the man's aunts had offered to care for him, making the abandonment unnecessary.
