Ted Cruz, the only official 2016 GOP presidential candidate so far, campaigned in Iowa emphasizing his evangelical Christian faith and religious freedom issues, drawing large crowds in the state known for its influential evangelical voter base. Cruz is employing a three-pronged strategy to win the nomination by appealing to evangelicals, tea party supporters, and libertarian-minded voters, with his campaign betting that mobilizing stay-at-home evangelical voters could significantly boost Republican turnout. However, Cruz faces stiff competition from other likely GOP candidates including Mike Huckabee and Rick Santorum, who won Iowa's caucuses in 2008 and 2012 respectively.
