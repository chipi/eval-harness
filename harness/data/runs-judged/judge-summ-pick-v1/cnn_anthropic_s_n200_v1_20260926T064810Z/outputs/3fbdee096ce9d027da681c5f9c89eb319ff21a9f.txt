Cuba attends the Summit of the Americas for the first time since 1962 after the U.S. lifted its decades-long diplomatic blockade. The two nations are negotiating to restore formal relations, though Cuba insists on being removed from the U.S. terrorism sponsor list before reopening embassies. The summit tests whether warming U.S.-Cuba ties can withstand Cuba's alliance with Venezuela, which the U.S. has criticized for human rights abuses.
