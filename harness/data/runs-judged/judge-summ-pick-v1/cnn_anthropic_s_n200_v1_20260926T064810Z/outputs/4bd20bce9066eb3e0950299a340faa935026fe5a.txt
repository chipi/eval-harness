Emy Afalava, a decorated veteran born in American Samoa, is suing for citizenship rights despite being subject to U.S. law his entire life, arguing the 14th Amendment guarantees citizenship to all born on U.S. soil. The federal government has denied citizenship to American Samoans, a practice rooted in early 20th-century racist Supreme Court decisions that treated island territories as second-class U.S. lands. A D.C. Circuit court decision on Afalava's case could soon resolve whether American Samoans finally receive the citizenship rights granted to residents of all other U.S. territories.
