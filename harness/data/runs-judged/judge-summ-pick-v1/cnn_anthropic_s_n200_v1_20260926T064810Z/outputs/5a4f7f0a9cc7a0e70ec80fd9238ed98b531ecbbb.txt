Officer Michael Slager shot Walter Scott in the back as he fled police in North Charleston, South Carolina; bystander video of the incident contradicts the officer's account and would likely have prevented the tragedy had Slager been wearing a body camera. The author argues that police body cameras are essential to reduce use-of-force incidents, restore public confidence in law enforcement, and expose racial bias in policing while broader social reforms are pursued. Though body cameras are not a complete solution to systemic racism in the criminal justice system, they can serve as an immediate deterrent and accountability measure.
