Computer analysis by University of Texas researchers suggests "Double Falsehood," a play published in 1728, was actually written by Shakespeare and John Fletcher over a century earlier, with publisher Lewis Theobald making only minor edits. The study analyzed word choice and linguistic patterns across works by all three authors, finding Shakespeare's signature dominated the first half and Fletcher's the second, with minimal traces of Theobald's writing. The findings could settle a long-standing scholarly debate over the play's true authorship, though some skeptics remain unconvinced.
