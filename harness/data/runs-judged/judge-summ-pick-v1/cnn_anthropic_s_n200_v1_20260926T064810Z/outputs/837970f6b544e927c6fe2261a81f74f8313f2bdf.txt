A Menzies Aviation ramp agent fell asleep in the pressurized cargo hold of Alaska Airlines Flight 448 and was discovered after banging and yelling alerted the pilot 14 minutes into the flight from Seattle to Los Angeles. The plane made an emergency landing and the employee was taken to a hospital as a precaution, passing a drug test before being discharged. Alaska Airlines is investigating how the worker ended up in the cargo hold after his team lead failed to locate him during pre-departure checks.
