President Obama's political investment in an Iran nuclear deal has weakened the U.S. negotiating position, making America appear more desperate than Iran despite Tehran's economic hardship from sanctions. The framework agreement announced falls far short of Obama's stated goal of Iran ending its nuclear program, instead allowing Iran to retain thousands of centrifuges and maintain most of its nuclear infrastructure while sanctions are lifted almost immediately. Critics argue the deal provides Iran political cover while failing to adequately prevent nuclear proliferation or regional instability.
