A Wayne Community College employee in North Carolina was shot and killed by a coworker Monday in what authorities are investigating as a possible hate crime; the victim, Ron Lane, was gay and the suspect, Kenneth Morgan Stancil III, had been terminated from a work-study program in March. Stancil fled on a motorcycle and was arrested Tuesday morning on a beach in Daytona, Florida, after traveling roughly 550 miles. Investigators are examining whether Stancil, who has facial tattoos, is affiliated with a white supremacist gang.
