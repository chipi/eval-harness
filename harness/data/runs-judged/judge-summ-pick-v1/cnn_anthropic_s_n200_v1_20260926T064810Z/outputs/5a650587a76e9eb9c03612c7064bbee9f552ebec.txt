The VII Summit of the Americas in Panama City was expected to focus on a historic handshake between President Obama and Cuban leader Raul Castro, but Venezuela's political crisis has created diplomatic tension. Venezuelan President Nicolas Maduro accused the U.S. of attempting a coup and banned several American politicians from entering the country, prompting Obama to issue sanctions; Cuba has publicly sided with Maduro, complicating the summit. The dispute threatens to overshadow the U.S.-Cuba reconciliation and could fracture hemispheric unity if other Latin American leaders align with Venezuela.
