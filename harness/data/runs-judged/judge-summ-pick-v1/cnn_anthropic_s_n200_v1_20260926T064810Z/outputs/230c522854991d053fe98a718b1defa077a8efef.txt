A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck with a hammer in an attempted mercy killing, and buried in a field, only to stumble to a nearby farm four days later. The one-year-old bully breed mix, now named Theia, is receiving treatment at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital for a dislocated jaw, leg injuries, and sinus damage. Her rescuer has launched a fundraising campaign that has already exceeded $10,000 to cover the dog's extensive medical procedures.
