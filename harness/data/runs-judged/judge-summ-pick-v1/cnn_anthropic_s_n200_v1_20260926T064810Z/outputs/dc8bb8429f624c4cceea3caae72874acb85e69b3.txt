A lapsed Catholic describes how Pope Francis's compassionate approach to faith—including his non-judgmental stance on homosexuality and emphasis on welcoming sinners—has rekindled her belief after years of alienation from the Church. The author credits the Pope's focus on Jesus's message of love over rigid rules and judgment for making her feel the Church is now an "open heart" rather than a place of rejection. She plans to return to Mass for the first time in decades.
