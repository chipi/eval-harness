7-Eleven is hosting its first "Bring Your Own Cup" Slurpee Day on Saturday from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. For $1.49, customers at participating U.S. stores can fill their own containers, provided they are sanitary and fit through a 10-inch in-store display hole. The promotion kicks off peak Slurpee season and is separate from the traditional Free Slurpee Day held in July.
