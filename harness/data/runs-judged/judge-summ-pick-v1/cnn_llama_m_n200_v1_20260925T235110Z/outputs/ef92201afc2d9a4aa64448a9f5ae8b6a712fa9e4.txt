WWI soldiers' graffiti discovered in underground city near Naours, France, featuring 1,821 names, including Australians, Brits, and Americans. The inscriptions, found in a network of carved-out rooms and pathways, date back to 1916 and provide a human connection to those who fought in the war. Photographer Jeff Gusky has chronicled the site, describing it as a "treasure trove" of historical significance.
