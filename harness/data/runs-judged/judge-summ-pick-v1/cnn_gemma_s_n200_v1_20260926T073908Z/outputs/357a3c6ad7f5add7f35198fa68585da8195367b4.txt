Saudi-led coalition airstrikes continue in Yemen, targeting Houthi rebels and causing widespread infrastructure damage, including a nationwide power outage. Over 600 people have been killed in less than two weeks of fighting, prompting a humanitarian crisis and calls for a ceasefire. The conflict stems from a power struggle between the Houthis and forces loyal to exiled President Hadi, with regional implications due to Saudi-Iran rivalry and a growing threat from al Qaeda.
