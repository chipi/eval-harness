Mindy Kaling’s brother, Vijay Chokalingam, admitted to fraudulently applying to medical school claiming to be African-American to improve his chances of acceptance. He received one acceptance despite the deception and is now writing a book about the experience, arguing it exposed preferential treatment for minority applicants. Kaling publicly disavowed her brother’s actions, calling them shameful, while the article argues his case ironically overlooks the systemic barriers faced by minorities and the need for affirmative action in healthcare.
