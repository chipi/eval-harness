A daring, meticulously planned burglary targeting safety deposit boxes occurred in London’s jewelry district over the Easter weekend, reminiscent of a movie heist. The crime, unseen in London for 40 years, likely involved inside information and a team with both intelligence and street smarts motivated by financial gain. Authorities are focusing on forensic evidence and intelligence gathering, mirroring investigations into past high-value robberies like Brinks MAT and the Knightsbridge deposit robbery.
