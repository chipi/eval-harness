. NASA outlines a plan to search for life beyond Earth in the next two decades, fueled by the discovery of potentially 40 billion habitable-zone planets in the Milky Way. While simple lifeforms may be common, the likelihood of finding intelligent, communicative civilizations is low due to timing and longevity challenges. Upcoming missions, including the James Webb Space Telescope and Mars/Europa exploration, will focus on identifying biosignatures like oxygen and water.
