The 2015 MTV Movie Awards featured a mix of hits and misses, highlighted by Channing Tatum's impromptu twerk for Jennifer Lopez and Amy Schumer's hosting. Schumer's monologue and onstage antics, including a kiss with Amber Rose, drew attention, while some jokes and performances fell flat. Robert Downey Jr. received the Generation Award, and Vin Diesel honored Paul Walker with a musical tribute.
