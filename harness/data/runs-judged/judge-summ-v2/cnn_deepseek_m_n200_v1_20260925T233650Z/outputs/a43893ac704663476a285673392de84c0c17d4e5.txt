Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars' "Uptown Funk!" notches a 13th week atop the Billboard Hot 100, becoming the longest-running No. 1 of the 2010s and just the 10th single ever to spend at least 13 weeks at the top. The song also leads Digital Songs, Streaming Songs and Radio Songs simultaneously for a record-extending ninth week. Natalie La Rose's debut hit "Somebody," featuring Jeremih, enters the top 10 at No. 10.
