Nearly 2,000 WWI soldiers' graffiti inscriptions have been discovered in an underground city beneath battlefields near Naours, France. The site, sealed in the 18th century and reopened in 2013, contains names of mostly Australian and British troops, including 55 Americans. Photographer Jeff Gusky, who has documented 1,821 names, calls the find a "treasure trove" connecting the present to men who fought a century ago.
