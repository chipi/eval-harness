Tejano star Selena, who died 20 years ago, will be revived as a "walking, talking, singing and dancing digital embodiment" called "Selena the One," her family announced. The hologram-like figure, created by Nevada tech company Acrovirt LLC, will release new music, collaborate with current artists and tour in 2018. An Indiegogo campaign launching April 16 aims to raise $500,000 for the project.
