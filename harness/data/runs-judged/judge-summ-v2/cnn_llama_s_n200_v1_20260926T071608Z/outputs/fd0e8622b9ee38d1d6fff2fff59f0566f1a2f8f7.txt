Universal's "Furious 7" is poised for a massive opening weekend in North America, with projections of $135-138 million, and has already earned a record-breaking $60 million internationally. The film, the latest in the Fast and Furious franchise, is getting a wide release in 4,003 US theaters and over 10,500 screens globally. It is on track to become the biggest opening of 2015 and one of the largest three-day openings in US history.
