Amnesty International's annual report on the death penalty says governments are using the threat of terrorism to justify executions, despite a 22% global decline in executions in 2014. At least 607 people were executed worldwide, with China, Iran, Iraq, and Saudi Arabia among the top executioners. The report notes a spike in death sentences, with 2,466 people sentenced to death in 2014, a 28% increase from 2013.
