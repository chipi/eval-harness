A hooded angel with black wings, created by artist Phillip Hyman, has become an icon of the Black Lives Matter movement after appearing near the site where Walter Scott was shot and killed by a police officer in North Charleston, South Carolina. The artwork, which Hyman created as a way to mourn with Scott's family, has been used in protests and has taken on a life of its own. Hyman says he is letting the artwork "do what it's supposed to do" as a symbol of the movement.
