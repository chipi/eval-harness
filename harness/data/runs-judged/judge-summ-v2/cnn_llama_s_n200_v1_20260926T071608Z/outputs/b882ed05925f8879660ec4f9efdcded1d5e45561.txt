Nigeria marked the one-year anniversary of the kidnapping of 276 schoolgirls by Boko Haram, with President-elect Muhammadu Buhari vowing to combat the group. The girls, aged 16-18, were abducted from a school in Chibok and have not been seen since, sparking global outrage and a #BringBackOurGirls campaign. Despite promises to rescue them, the girls remain missing, and Boko Haram continues to control parts of northeastern Nigeria.
