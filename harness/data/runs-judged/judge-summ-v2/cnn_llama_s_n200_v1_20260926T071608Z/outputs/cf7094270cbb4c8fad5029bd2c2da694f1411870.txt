Several people have survived incredible ordeals at sea, including being stranded for weeks or months with limited resources. Louis Jordan was rescued 200 miles off the North Carolina coast after his sailboat capsized, while Jose Salvador Alvarenga survived 13 months adrift in the Pacific. Others, including Ron Ingraham, Adrian Vasquez, and Hiromitsu Shinkawa, also survived against-the-odds experiences at sea or during natural disasters.
