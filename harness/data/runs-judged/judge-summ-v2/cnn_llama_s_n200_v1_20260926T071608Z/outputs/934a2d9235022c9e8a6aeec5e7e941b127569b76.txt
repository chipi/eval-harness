The Large Hadron Collider, the world's largest particle accelerator, has restarted after a two-year shutdown, with scientists completing final tests and circulating the first beams of particles. The $3.3 billion machine aims to recreate the conditions of the Big Bang and uncover secrets of the universe, including the origin of mass and dark matter. The LHC will collide particles at near-light speed, generating vast amounts of data to help scientists better understand the universe.
