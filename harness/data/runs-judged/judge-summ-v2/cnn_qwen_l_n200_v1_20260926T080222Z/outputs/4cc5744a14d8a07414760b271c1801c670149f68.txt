Blue Bell Creameries has shut down its Broken Arrow, Oklahoma, plant and issued a recall after listeria was found in a 3-ounce ice cream cup linked to a Kansas hospital outbreak. The CDC warns against consuming affected products marked with codes O–T, though the bacteria doesn’t match all patient samples; five hospitalized patients in Kansas fell ill, three fatally, after consuming Blue Bell milkshakes. This marks Blue Bell’s first recall in its 108-year history amid ongoing investigations into potential links to listeriosis cases.
