Marvel and other studios unveiled new character reveals across multiple superhero projects, including Paul Bettany as Vision in "Avengers: Age of Ultron," Charlie Cox's official Daredevil suit, Ben Hardy as Angel in "X-Men: Apocalypse," and Jamie Bell's CGI-rendered Thing in the "Fantastic Four" reboot. The flurry of announcements underscores the ongoing surge in superhero media ahead of major 2015 releases.
