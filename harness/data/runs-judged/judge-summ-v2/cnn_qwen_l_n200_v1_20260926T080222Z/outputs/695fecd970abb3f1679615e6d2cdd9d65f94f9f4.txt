7-Eleven launches its first Bring-Your-Own-Cup Slurpee Day at U.S. stores Saturday, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., offering $1.49 fills in any sanitary container that fits through a 10-inch hole—excluding garbage cans. The event kicks off peak Slurpee season and differs from the annual Free Slurpee Day on July 11.
