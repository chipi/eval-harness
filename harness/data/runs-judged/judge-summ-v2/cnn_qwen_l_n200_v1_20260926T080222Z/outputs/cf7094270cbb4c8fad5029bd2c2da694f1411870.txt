Multiple individuals have survived extraordinary ordeals at sea, including Louis Jordan (rescued after 66 days adrift off North Carolina), Jose Salvador Alvarenga (14 months alone in the Pacific), Ron Ingraham (12 days near Hawaii), Adrian Vasquez (26 days from Panama to near the Galapagos), and Hiromitsu Shinkawa (swept out to sea by Japan’s 2011 tsunami). Each relied on ingenuity, luck, and sheer will to survive with minimal food, water, or hope of rescue.
