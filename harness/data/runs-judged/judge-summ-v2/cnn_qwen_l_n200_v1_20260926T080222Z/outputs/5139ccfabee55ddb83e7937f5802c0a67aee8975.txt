The U.S. has lodged a formal complaint with Russia after a Russian SU-27 jet intercepted a U.S. RC-135U reconnaissance plane over the Baltic Sea in what the Pentagon called an "unsafe and unprofessional" maneuver in international airspace. The U.S. denies Russian claims the American aircraft had its transponder off, calling the assertion false; this marks the latest in a series of tense aerial encounters between the two nations.
