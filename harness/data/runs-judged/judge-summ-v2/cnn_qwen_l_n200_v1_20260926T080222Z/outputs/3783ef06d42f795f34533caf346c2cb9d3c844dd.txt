Federal prosecutors have charged Robert Durst, 71, with unlawful firearm possession after a .38 caliber revolver was found in his New Orleans hotel room; he is already held without bail on a first-degree murder charge in the 2000 killing of Susan Berman and faces possible extradition to Los Angeles, where conviction could bring the death penalty. Authorities also seized drugs, cash, a latex mask, and a UPS package containing over $100,000, while FBI agents review decades-old cold cases linked to Durst’s past residences. His attorney cites serious health issues, including recent brain and esophageal surgeries, and claims authorities are grasping at straws.
