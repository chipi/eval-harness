Liana Barrientos, 39, faces criminal charges for allegedly entering into 10 marriages—eight simultaneously—as part of an immigration scam, falsely claiming her 2010 union was her first. Prosecutors say several husbands, from "red-flagged" countries, sought residency through the fraudulent marriages, with divorces occurring only after approvals; one husband was deported in 2006. Barrientos pleaded not guilty and was separately arrested for subway fare evasion; she faces up to four years if convicted, with her next court date set for May 18.
