Swedish photographer Johan Bavman documents fathers taking full advantage of Sweden’s generous 480-day parental leave policy, which includes 60 non-transferable “daddy months” and a gender-equality bonus to encourage shared caregiving. Despite the policy, only 12% of couples split leave equally, but participating fathers report deeper bonds with their children and greater appreciation for parenting’s demands. Bavman aims to photograph 60 such fathers for an upcoming exhibition and book.
