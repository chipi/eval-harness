The Taliban has published a biography of its reclusive leader, Mullah Mohammed Omar, to dispel rumors of his death and reaffirm his leadership amid growing speculation. The 11-page document details Omar's early life, jihadi credentials, and daily routine, while omitting his ties to al Qaeda and the U.S. role in arming the mujahedeen. Analysts suggest the move is also a response to ISIS's rising influence in the region.
