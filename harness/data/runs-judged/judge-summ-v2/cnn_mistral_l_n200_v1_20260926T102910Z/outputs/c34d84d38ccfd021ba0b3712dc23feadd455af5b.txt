**Emmy-winning actor Richard Dysart, best known as Leland McKenzie on *L.A. Law*, has died at 86 after a long illness. Dysart, a versatile character actor who also played Truman and doctors in films like *The Thing*, won an Emmy in 1992 for his role in the NBC drama. A private memorial is planned; donations requested to the Will Geer Theatricum Botanicum.**
