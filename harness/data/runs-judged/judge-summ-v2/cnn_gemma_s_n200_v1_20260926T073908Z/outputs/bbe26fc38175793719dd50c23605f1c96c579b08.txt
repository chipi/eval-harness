Jia Jiang overcame his fear of rejection by intentionally seeking it out for 100 days, documenting his experiences which led to a viral video and a new book, "Rejection Proof." The project, born from a failed startup, aims to help others embrace rejection as a path to opportunity and personal growth. Jiang's story highlights that the fear of rejection is often more debilitating than rejection itself, and offers strategies for turning "no" into a stepping stone.
