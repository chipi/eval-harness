Guatemala faces an epidemic of gender-based violence, with nearly 10 women killed per 100,000 people and an estimated 98% of cases never reaching court, fueled by a legacy of civil war, deep-rooted machismo culture, and systemic impunity. Despite these challenges, organizations like CICAM and programs such as "Abriendo Oportunidades" are slowly changing attitudes through survivor advocacy and education, while the government has established specialized courts and task forces to address femicide. However, experts warn that international attention and immediate action remain critical as millions of women remain trapped in cycles of violence.
