Anthony Ray Hinton was released Friday after nearly 30 years on Alabama's death row for murders he did not commit; a judge dismissed charges after firearms experts determined the gun he was accused of using could not be matched to evidence in the 1985 killings of two restaurant managers. Hinton, who was 29 at the time and always maintained his innocence, said prosecutors should have tested the gun years earlier, and the Equal Justice Initiative cited race, poverty and inadequate legal representation as factors in the miscarriage of justice. The state declined to re-prosecute the case after the 2014 order for a new trial.
