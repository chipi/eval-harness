"Mad Men" concludes its final season Sunday after eight years as a cultural phenomenon depicting a 1960s advertising agency amid social upheaval. The show faces the challenge of sticking its landing following the moon landing finale of season six, with creator Matthew Weiner expected to navigate the latter half of 1969 and potential departures of key characters like Roger Sterling and Peggy Olson. The series has become emblematic of the era's disintegration, reflecting the collapse of old institutions and generational conflict.
