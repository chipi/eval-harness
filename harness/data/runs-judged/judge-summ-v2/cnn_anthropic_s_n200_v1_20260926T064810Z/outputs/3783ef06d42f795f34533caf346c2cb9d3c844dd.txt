Real estate heir Robert Durst, 71, has been federally charged with unlawful firearm possession after a .38 caliber revolver was found in his hotel room. Durst faces a maximum 10-year sentence on the weapons charge and awaits extradition to Los Angeles on a first-degree murder charge in the 2000 death of his friend Susan Berman, which could carry the death penalty. The FBI is also reviewing cold cases in multiple states where Durst lived over the past five decades.
