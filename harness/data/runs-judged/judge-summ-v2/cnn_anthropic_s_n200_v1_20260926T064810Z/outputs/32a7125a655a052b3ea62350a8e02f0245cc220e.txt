British banker Rurik Jutting, 29, was charged with two counts of murder after Hong Kong police discovered two Indonesian women dead in his Wan Chai apartment in November—one on the floor with neck wounds, another stuffed in a suitcase on the balcony. The victims, domestic workers Seneng Mujiasih, 29, and Sumarti Ningsih, 25, were identified as having been killed days apart. A court hearing to determine if there is sufficient evidence to proceed to trial was adjourned until May pending analysis of over 200 pieces of forensic and DNA evidence.
