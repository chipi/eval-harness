Philadelphia police charged Nyia Parler, 41, with attempted murder after she allegedly abandoned her quadriplegic 21-year-old son in woods for over four days while visiting her boyfriend in Maryland. The man, who also has cerebral palsy, was found Friday suffering from dehydration, malnutrition, and exposure to freezing temperatures. Parler is in Maryland receiving unspecified treatment before extradition to Philadelphia to face additional charges including aggravated assault and reckless endangerment.
