Paul Walker, who died in a car crash in November 2013 at age 40 while filming "Fast and Furious 7," is remembered as one of Hollywood's kindest actors, with co-stars and fans paying tribute ahead of the film's release. Producers rewrote the script and used special effects and Walker's brothers as body doubles to complete the film without killing off his character, ending with an emotional tribute. The release has sparked widespread mourning, with thousands attending memorials and co-star Vin Diesel naming his newborn daughter after Walker.
