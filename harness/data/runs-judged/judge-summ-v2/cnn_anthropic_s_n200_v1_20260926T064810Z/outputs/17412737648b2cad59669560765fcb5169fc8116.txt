Philadelphia woman Keonna Thomas, 30, was charged Thursday with attempting to travel to Syria to join ISIS after purchasing a visa to Turkey and booking flights to Barcelona. Two New York women, Noelle Velentzas and Asia Siddiqui, were also arrested Thursday on terror charges—Velentzas and Siddiqui for allegedly planning to build an explosive device for U.S. attacks. The arrests are part of a surge in prosecutions, with the Justice Department handling more than 30 cases in 18 months of people attempting to support terrorist groups, 18 of which involve ISIS.
