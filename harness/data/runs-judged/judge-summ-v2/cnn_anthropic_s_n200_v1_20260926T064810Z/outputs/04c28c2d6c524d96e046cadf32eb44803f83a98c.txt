Hundreds mourned Walter Scott at his funeral Saturday in South Carolina, where church leaders called his fatal shooting by white police officer Michael Slager "an act of overt racism" and a catalyst for change. Slager was charged with murder after a bystander's video showed him shooting the fleeing black man in the back, though his defense may argue it was a heat-of-passion manslaughter case stemming from a reported struggle over a Taser. Scott had fled the traffic stop due to an outstanding warrant for unpaid child support.
