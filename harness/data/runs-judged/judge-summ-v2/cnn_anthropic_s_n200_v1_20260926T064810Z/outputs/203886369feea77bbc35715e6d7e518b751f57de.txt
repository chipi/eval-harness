New research by the Anne Frank House indicates that Anne Frank and her sister Margot died at least a month earlier than previously believed, likely in early February 1945 rather than March 1945. Researchers re-examined Red Cross archives and survivor testimonies to conclude the sisters probably succumbed to typhus at Bergen-Belsen concentration camp before February 7, based on witness accounts of their symptoms. The timing means Anne died roughly two months before the camp's liberation in May 1945.
