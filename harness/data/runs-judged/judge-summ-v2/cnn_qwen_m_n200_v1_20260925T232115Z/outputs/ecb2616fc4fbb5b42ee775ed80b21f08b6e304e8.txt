Ryan Gosling’s divisive directorial debut "Lost River" uses decaying Detroit as a central character, capturing the city's ruin and resilience through a post-industrial gothic lens. The film originated from Gosling’s urgent desire to document the Brewster-Douglass Projects before their demolition, evolving into a narrative balancing destruction with signs of urban rebirth. Despite mixed critical reception, the project highlights Detroit’s paradoxical state of annihilation and regeneration ahead of its limited theatrical release.
