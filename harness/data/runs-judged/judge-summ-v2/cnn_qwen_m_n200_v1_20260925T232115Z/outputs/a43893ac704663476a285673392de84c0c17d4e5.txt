Mark Ronson’s “Uptown Funk!” rules the Billboard Hot 100 for a 13th week, becoming the decade's longest-leading hit and joining an elite group of singles with at least 13 weeks at No. 1. The track simultaneously tops Digital, Radio, and Streaming charts, maintaining a significant lead over runner-up Maroon 5’s “Sugar.” Newcomer Natalie La Rose enters the top 10 with her debut single “Somebody,” featuring Jeremih.
