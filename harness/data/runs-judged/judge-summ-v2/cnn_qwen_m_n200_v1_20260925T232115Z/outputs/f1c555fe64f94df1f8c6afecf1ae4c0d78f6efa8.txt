Anthony Ray Hinton was released from Alabama's death row Friday after nearly 30 years, following a judge's dismissal of murder charges due to flawed forensic evidence. The state declined to retry the case after experts testified the gun linked to Hinton could not be matched to the 1985 crimes. Hinton maintained his innocence throughout his incarceration, citing systemic injustice and inadequate legal representation as key factors in his wrongful conviction.
