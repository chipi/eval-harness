Guatemala ranks third worldwide in killings of women, with two women murdered daily and 98% of homicide cases never reaching court, according to UN figures. Activists blame the legacy of the country's 36-year civil war, entrenched machismo, corrupt police and weak courts, and the spread of drug cartels and gangs.

Abuse survivor Adelma Cifuentes, who lost an arm in an attack ordered by her husband, secured his 27-year sentence and now performs in a play with other survivors. The government has passed a femicide law and created specialized courts, while NGOs and girls' programs work to change social norms.
