New research from the Anne Frank House indicates Anne and her sister Margot died of typhus at Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than the previously accepted date of March 31, 1945. Researchers reviewed Red Cross and memorial archives plus survivor testimony, finding both sisters showed symptoms before February 7 and that typhus deaths typically occur about 12 days after onset. Exact dates remain unknown; Margot died first, weeks before the camp's liberation.
