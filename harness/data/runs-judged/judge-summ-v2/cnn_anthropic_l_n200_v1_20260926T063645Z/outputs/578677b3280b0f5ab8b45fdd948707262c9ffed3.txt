More than 25,000 people have been infected with Ebola over the past year, with over 10,000 deaths, in what the WHO called the most severe public health emergency of modern times. New cases have fallen sharply, with 30 confirmed in the week of April 5 — the lowest weekly total since May 2014 — and surplus treatment facilities in Liberia and Sierra Leone now being decommissioned. But Guinea reported unsafe burials and deaths identified as Ebola only post-mortem, suggesting undetected transmission chains could fuel new infections.
