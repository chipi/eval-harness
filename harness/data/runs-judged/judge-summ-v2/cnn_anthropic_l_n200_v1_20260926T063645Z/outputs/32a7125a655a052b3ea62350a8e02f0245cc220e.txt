Hong Kong police last November found two Indonesian women dead in a Wan Chai apartment — one on the blood-stained floor, the other in a suitcase on the balcony. Former Bank of America Merrill Lynch trader Rurik Jutting, 29, a British Cambridge graduate, was charged with two counts of murder and found psychologically fit to plead. A hearing to decide whether the case proceeds to trial was adjourned Thursday until May to allow analysis of more than 200 pieces of forensic and DNA evidence.
