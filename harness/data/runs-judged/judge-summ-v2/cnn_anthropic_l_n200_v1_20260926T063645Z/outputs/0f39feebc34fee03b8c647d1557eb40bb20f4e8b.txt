Maryland police say Nyia Parler, 41, arrested Sunday, cannot be extradited to Philadelphia until she completes unspecified treatment. Philadelphia police allege she left her 21-year-old quadriplegic son, who also has cerebral palsy, in Cobbs Creek Park for more than four days while visiting her boyfriend in Maryland. The man, found by a passerby covered in a blanket with a Bible and wheelchair nearby, was hospitalized with dehydration, malnutrition and a back wound; Parler faces aggravated assault and related charges.
