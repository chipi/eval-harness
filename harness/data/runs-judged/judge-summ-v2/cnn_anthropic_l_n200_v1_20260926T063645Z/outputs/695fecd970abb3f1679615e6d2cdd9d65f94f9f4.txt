7-Eleven held its first Bring-Your-Own-Cup Slurpee Day on Saturday at participating US stores from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., letting customers fill any container for $1.49. In-store displays with a 10-inch-diameter hole limited cup size, and containers had to be sanitary. The promotion is separate from the chain's annual Free Slurpee Day on July 11.
