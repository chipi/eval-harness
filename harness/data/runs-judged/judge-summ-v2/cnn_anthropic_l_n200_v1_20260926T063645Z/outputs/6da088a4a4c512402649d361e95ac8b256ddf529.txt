Former Maryland Gov. Martin O'Malley told reporters in Iowa that Hillary Clinton's status as Democratic frontrunner is not unassailable, saying "history is full of examples where the inevitable frontrunner was inevitable right up until" they weren't. O'Malley, who called Clinton an "eminently qualified candidate" but avoided direct attacks, polls at about 1% nationally and in Iowa, while Clinton leads by more than 40 points. Clinton is expected to launch her campaign Sunday via a social media video.
