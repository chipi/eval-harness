Don McLean's original manuscript for "American Pie" sold for $1.2 million at Christie's on Tuesday, the third-highest price for an American literary manuscript. The 16-page draft contains 237 handwritten lines, typed text, unused lyrics and extensive notes. McLean, who turns 70 this year, said he sold it to benefit his wife and children; the record for a popular music manuscript remains Bob Dylan's "Like a Rolling Stone," at $2 million.
