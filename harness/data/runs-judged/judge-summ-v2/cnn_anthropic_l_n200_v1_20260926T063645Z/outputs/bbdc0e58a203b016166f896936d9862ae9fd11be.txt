Kenya has frozen 86 bank accounts linked to suspected Al-Shabaab supporters following last week's massacre of 147 people at a university in Garissa, with officials saying more could follow. The government also launched airstrikes on militant camps in Somalia and offered a $215,000 reward for alleged attack organiser Mohamed Mohamud. Hundreds gathered in Nairobi's Uhuru Park to mourn, as authorities faced criticism over reports that a rapid response team was delayed for hours awaiting transport.
