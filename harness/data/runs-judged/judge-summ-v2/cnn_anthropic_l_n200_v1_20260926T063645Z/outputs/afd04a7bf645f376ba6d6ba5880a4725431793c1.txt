Nicole Kidman's "Grace of Monaco" will skip U.S. theaters and premiere on Lifetime on May 25. The Weinstein Co., which bought U.S. rights, opted to sell to the cable network after the critically panned film flopped internationally following its Cannes opening. The production was dogged by a public feud between director Olivier Dahan and Harvey Weinstein over the final cut, and by criticism from Grace Kelly's children, who called it historically inaccurate.
