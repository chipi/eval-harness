"Furious 7" is tracking for a $135 million-$138 million North American opening, the biggest domestic debut since November 2013 and the largest of 2015 so far. The film took a record $60 million internationally on Wednesday and Thursday, putting it on course for a worldwide bow near or above $300 million. It is Universal's widest release ever, playing in 4,003 US theaters and more than 10,500 screens across 63 territories, and is the last to feature Paul Walker, who died in a 2013 car crash during production.
