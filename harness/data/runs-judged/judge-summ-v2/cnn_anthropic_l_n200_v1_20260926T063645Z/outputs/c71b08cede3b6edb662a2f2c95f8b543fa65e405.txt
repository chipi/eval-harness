Robert Lewis Burns Jr., the original drummer for Lynyrd Skynyrd, died in a single-car crash in Cartersville, Georgia, late Friday, Georgia State Patrol said. Burns, 64, was not wearing a seat belt when his car struck a mailbox and a tree. He was part of the band's original 1965 lineup and left in 1975, two years before the plane crash that killed frontman Ronnie Van Zant.
