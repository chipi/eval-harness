A gang burglarized a Hatton Garden vault in London's jewelry district over the Easter weekend, evoking comparisons to heist films and the 1971 Baker Street bank robbery. Former Scotland Yard commander Roy Ramm explains that such sophisticated crimes require meticulous planning, trusted criminal networks, and often inside information, with cash and easily convertible valuables like gold and jewelry being preferred targets. Police will now pursue forensic evidence, CCTV footage, and criminal intelligence networks to identify the perpetrators.
