American Samoan veteran Emy Afalava, denied citizenship despite being born on U.S. soil, has filed a federal lawsuit challenging his status, with a ruling expected soon from the D.C. Circuit. The case echoes historic Supreme Court decisions—Dred Scott and Gonzalez v. Williams—that denied full citizenship rights based on race. American Samoans remain the only U.S. territory residents not granted citizenship, more than a century after annexation.
