Nyia Parler, 41, was taken into custody in Maryland after Philadelphia police said she abandoned her quadriplegic, cerebral palsy-afflicted son in a wooded park for over four days while she visited her boyfriend. The 21-year-old victim, found by a passerby suffering from dehydration, malnutrition and infection risk, is hospitalized; Parler faces aggravated assault and related charges but cannot be extradited until she completes unspecified treatment.
