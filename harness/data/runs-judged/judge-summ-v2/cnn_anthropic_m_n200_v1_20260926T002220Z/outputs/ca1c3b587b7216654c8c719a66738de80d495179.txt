Pacers forward Chris Copeland was stabbed early Wednesday near a New York nightclub, along with his companion Katrine Saltara and another woman, after a dispute with a 22-year-old suspect who was later arrested. Atlanta Hawks players Pero Antic and Thabo Sefolosha, who rushed to check on Copeland, were arrested on obstruction and disorderly conduct charges but were not involved in the stabbing itself. Copeland is in stable condition; both players deny wrongdoing and plan to contest the charges.
