Easter's shifting date is determined by the first Sunday after the vernal equinox's full moon, aligning it with Passover and causing Western and Orthodox dates to diverge. Pope Francis marked Holy Week in Vatican City by calling for global peace, while Jerusalem's Church of the Holy Sepulcher hosted Resurrection celebrations. Secular traditions like the Easter bunny and colored eggs originated in 17th-century Germany and can be adapted using Kool-Aid dye.
