Saudi-led airstrikes in Sanaa have destroyed infrastructure and cut power to 16 million, while ground fighting killed over 50 in Aden on Monday alone. The ICRC is demanding a humanitarian ceasefire as medical supplies remain grounded, with an estimated 600 dead since the campaign began. Deposed President Hadi, now in Saudi Arabia, fired his former army chief who had already defected to the Houthi rebels.
