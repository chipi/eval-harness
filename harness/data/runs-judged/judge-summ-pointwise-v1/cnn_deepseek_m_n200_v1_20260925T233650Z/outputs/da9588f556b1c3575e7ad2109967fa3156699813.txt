A Philadelphia woman was charged Sunday after allegedly abandoning her 21-year-old quadriplegic son in Cobbs Creek Park for more than four days while she traveled to Maryland to visit her boyfriend. The man, who has cerebral palsy and cannot communicate, was found with only a blanket and a Bible and was hospitalized with dehydration, malnutrition and an infected cut. The mother faces charges including aggravated assault, kidnapping and neglect of a care-dependent person.
