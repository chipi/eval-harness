FedEx has been indicted by the DOJ on 15 counts related to allegedly facilitating illegal prescription drug distribution and money laundering. The case hinges on whether FedEx can be held liable for the contents of packages it delivers, with the company arguing it qualifies as a “common carrier” and shouldn’t be responsible for unknowing transport of illicit goods. The DOJ alleges FedEx knowingly facilitated illegal activity despite warnings, while legal experts question the precedent of prosecuting corporations as individuals.
