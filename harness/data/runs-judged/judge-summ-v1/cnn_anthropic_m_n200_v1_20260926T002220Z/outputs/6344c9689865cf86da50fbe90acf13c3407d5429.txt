Guatemala has one of the world's highest femicide rates, with two women killed daily amid a culture of impunity rooted in the country's civil war and entrenched machismo. Survivor Adelma Cifuentes, who lost an arm in an attempted murder by her abusive husband, now uses theater to help other women, while government reforms, NGOs, and community programs offer some hope for change. Despite these efforts, weak law enforcement and deep-seated discrimination leave millions of women vulnerable.
