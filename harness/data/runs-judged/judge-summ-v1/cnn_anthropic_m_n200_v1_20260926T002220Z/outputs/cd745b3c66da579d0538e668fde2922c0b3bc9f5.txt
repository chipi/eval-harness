"Furious 7" earned a record $146 million over the weekend, with many moviegoers reporting they were emotionally moved by the film's tribute to actor Paul Walker, who died before filming was completed. The movie's producers used body doubles, CGI and Walker's brothers to finish his scenes, ultimately choosing to retire his character rather than kill him off, culminating in a montage set to the song "See You Again." Fans flooded social media with praise for the tribute, with many describing being brought to tears by the film's ending.
