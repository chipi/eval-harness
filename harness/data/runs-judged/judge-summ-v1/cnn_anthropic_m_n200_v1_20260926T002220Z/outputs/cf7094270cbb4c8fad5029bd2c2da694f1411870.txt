Several recent survival stories highlight remarkable ocean rescues, including Louis Jordan, found after weeks adrift off North Carolina, and Jose Salvador Alvarenga, who survived over a year in the Pacific after his companion died. Other cases include fisherman Ron Ingraham rescued after 12 days near Hawaii, teenager Adrian Vasquez found after 26 days with his two companions dead, and Hiromitsu Shinkawa, rescued clinging to debris miles offshore after Japan's 2011 tsunami. Each survivor relied on ingenuity, rationed resources, and luck to endure their ordeals.
