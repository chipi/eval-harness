Arsenal beat Burnley 1-0 thanks to an Aaron Ramsey goal, moving to within four points of leaders Chelsea, who have two games in hand and face QPR next. Coach Arsene Wenger praised midfielder Francis Coquelin as Arsenal extended their winning run to eight games, setting up a crucial title showdown with Chelsea next week. Elsewhere, Aston Villa beat Tottenham, West Brom lost late to Leicester, and Crystal Palace thrashed Sunderland 4-1, with Yannick Bolasie scoring the club's first Premier League hat trick.
