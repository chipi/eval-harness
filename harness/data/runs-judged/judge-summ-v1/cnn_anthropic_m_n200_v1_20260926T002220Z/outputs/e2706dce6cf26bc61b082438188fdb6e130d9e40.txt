Amnesty International's annual report says global executions fell 22% in 2014 to at least 607, even as death sentences rose 28% to 2,466, partly due to mass sentencing in Egypt and Nigeria. The group warns that governments, including Pakistan and China, are increasingly using the death penalty to combat real or perceived terrorism threats. Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and the U.S. remained the top executioners outside China, which keeps its execution data secret.
