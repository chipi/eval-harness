Kim Ki-Jong, the man accused of stabbing U.S. Ambassador Mark Lippert in Seoul on March 5, has been charged with attempted murder, assaulting a foreign envoy and business obstruction. Lippert suffered a deep facial gash requiring 80 stitches and cuts to his arm, but is expected to recover fully. Police say Kim, who has visited North Korea seven times and has a history of violent protest, attacked Lippert to oppose joint U.S.-South Korean military drills.
