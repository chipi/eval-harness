Kenneth Morgan Stancil III has been charged in the fatal shooting of Ron Lane, a print shop operator at Wayne Community College in Goldsboro, North Carolina. Authorities are investigating whether the killing was a hate crime, noting Lane was gay and Stancil has facial tattoos possibly linked to a white supremacist gang. Stancil was arrested sleeping on a Florida beach, about 550 miles from the crime scene, after fleeing on a motorcycle.
