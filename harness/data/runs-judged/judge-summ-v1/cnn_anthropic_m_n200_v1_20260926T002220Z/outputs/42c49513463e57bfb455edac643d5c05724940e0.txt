Lauren Hill, the Indiana college basketball player whose battle against brain cancer inspired people worldwide, has died at age 19. Hill, who was diagnosed with an inoperable brain tumor in 2013, fulfilled her dream of playing for Mount St. Joseph University after the NCAA moved up her team's season opener, and she helped raise $1.4 million for pediatric cancer research. Tributes poured in from figures including LeBron James and NCAA President Mark Emmert, who praised her courage and lasting legacy.
