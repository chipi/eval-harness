Al-Shabaab claimed responsibility for an attack on Garissa University College in Kenya that killed many and took hostages, marking an escalation of the group's activities across East Africa. The Somalia-based group, which split from Al-Ittihad al-Islami in 2003 and later pledged allegiance to al-Qaeda, has increasingly targeted Kenya since 2011, exploiting regional instability, poverty and governance failures to fuel recruitment. Analysts argue lasting security requires addressing these root causes, not just military responses.
