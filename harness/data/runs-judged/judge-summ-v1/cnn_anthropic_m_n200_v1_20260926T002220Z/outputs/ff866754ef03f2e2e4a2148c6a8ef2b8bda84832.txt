Ferguson is undergoing significant leadership changes following the DOJ's report on racial discrimination in its police and court systems, including the resignations of the city manager and police chief and the election of two new black city council members. The DOJ will soon negotiate police department restructuring, potentially including a merger with St. Louis County police, but activists warn that lasting reform depends on sustained community pressure during these negotiations. The article argues Ferguson's governance flaws—weak elected officials versus powerful unelected administrators—mirror problems in suburbs nationwide.
