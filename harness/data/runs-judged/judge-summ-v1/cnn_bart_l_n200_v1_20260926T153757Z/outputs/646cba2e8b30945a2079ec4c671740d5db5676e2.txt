Don McLean's "American Pie" sold at Christie's for $1.2 million. Bob Greene: The song is a hybrid of modern poetry and folk ballad, beer-hall chant and high-art rock. He says McLean was a paperboy when Buddy Holly, Ritchie Valens and J.P. "The Big Bopper" Richardson died. Greene: McLean has guaranteed that the memory of those great musicians lives forever.
