The U.S. is lodging a formal diplomatic complaint with Moscow after a Russian SU-27 intercepted an American RC-135U reconnaissance plane in international airspace over the Baltic Sea. The Pentagon condemned the Russian pilot's aggressive maneuvers as "unsafe and unprofessional," while dismissing Russian claims that the U.S. aircraft had its transponder switched off. This incident marks the latest in a series of tense aerial encounters between the two nations, echoing a dangerous close pass in the Pacific last year.
