Indiana Gov. Mike Pence signed a controversial religious freedom law to shore up conservative credentials for a potential 2016 White House run, drawing widespread backlash but gaining support among the GOP's right wing. The resulting furor is dragging the entire Republican presidential field farther right, forcing candidates to address a wave of similar state-level anti-LGBT bills. Despite national progress on gay rights, conservatives' focus on "religious freedom" legislation threatens to make cultural warfare a central issue in the 2016 election.
