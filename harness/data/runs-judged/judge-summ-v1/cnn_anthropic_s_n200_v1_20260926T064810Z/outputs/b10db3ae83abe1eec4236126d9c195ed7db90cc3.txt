Tokyo's Shibuya ward passed an ordinance Wednesday allowing same-sex couples to register for "partnership certificates" granting limited rights including hospital visitation and co-signing tenancy agreements, though not full marriage equality. The non-binding certificates, to be issued later this summer, represent a significant symbolic step in socially conservative Japan, where support for gay marriage among young adults reaches 70% despite overall opposition at 52.4%. Gay rights activists and lawmakers called the move a crucial first step toward marriage equality, though they emphasized that national legislation would be needed to achieve full equal rights.
