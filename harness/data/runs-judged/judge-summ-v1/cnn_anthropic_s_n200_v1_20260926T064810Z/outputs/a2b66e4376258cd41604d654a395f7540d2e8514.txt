Kanye West has settled a civil lawsuit with paparazzi photographer Daniel Ramos, whom he assaulted at LAX in 2013, with West issuing a public apology and the two shaking hands. The settlement came days before trial was set to begin; West had previously pleaded no contest to misdemeanor battery and was sentenced to probation, anger management and community service. Photographer's attorney Gloria Allred said the case "sent an important message" that celebrities are not above the law.
