Indiana Gov. Mike Pence signed a controversial religious freedom law allowing discrimination against LGBT individuals to shore up conservative credentials ahead of a potential 2016 presidential run, despite opposition from business leaders and his state's Republican mayor. The move reflects a broader push by multiple Republican-controlled states to pass similar legislation, potentially forcing 2016 GOP candidates to take hardline positions on LGBT rights during the primary. While national polling shows growing support for same-sex marriage, the political dynamics suggest cultural issues around LGBT rights will play an outsized role in the 2016 election.
