Actor John Travolta dismissed HBO's critical documentary "Going Clear: Scientology and the Prison of Belief," saying he hasn't seen it and doesn't plan to, calling it the work of disgruntled former members. Travolta, one of Scientology's most prominent celebrity members, credited the church with helping him survive his son's death and called the organization "brilliant." He argued he has had only positive experiences with Scientology over 40 years and wouldn't validate claims he hasn't personally experienced.
