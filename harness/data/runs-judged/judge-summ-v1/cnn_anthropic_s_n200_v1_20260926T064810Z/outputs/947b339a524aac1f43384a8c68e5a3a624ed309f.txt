Dzhokhar Tsarnaev was found guilty on all 30 counts in the Boston Marathon bombing trial, including 17 capital charges making him eligible for the death penalty. The case will now move to a penalty phase where jurors must decide whether to execute the 21-year-old, weighing aggravating factors like the heinousness of the crimes against mitigating factors including his youth and mental health history. His defense attorney faces a difficult task convincing the jury to spare his life, given the premeditated nature of the attack and civilian casualties.
