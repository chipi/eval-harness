Swedish photographer Johan Bavman is documenting fathers taking full parental leave in Sweden, which offers 480 days total—390 paid at 80% salary—with 60 mandatory "daddy months" to encourage equal parenting. Despite the generous policy, only 12% of Swedish couples equally share leave, with women still primarily staying home. Bavman aims to photograph 60 fathers to highlight both the joys and exhaustion of full-time parenting, challenging centuries of unrecognized maternal labor.
