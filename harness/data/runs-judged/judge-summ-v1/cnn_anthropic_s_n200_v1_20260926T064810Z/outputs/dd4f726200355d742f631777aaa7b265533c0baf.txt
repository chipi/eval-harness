Mary Kay Letourneau, a former teacher who served seven years in prison for an affair with her 13-year-old student Vili Fualaau, says she and Fualaau remain happily married ahead of their 10th wedding anniversary. The couple, now 53 and 31 respectively, will discuss their relationship in an ABC "20/20" interview, where Letourneau plans to reveal her intentions to teach again and seek removal of her sex offender status.
