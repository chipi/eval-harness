A jury found Dzhokhar Tsarnaev guilty on all 30 counts for the 2013 Boston Marathon bombing that killed three people and wounded hundreds of others. Survivors and victims' families expressed relief at the verdict while acknowledging that no verdict can fully compensate for their losses, with many viewing it as an important step toward closure and moving forward. The Boston community, united under the "Boston Strong" mantra, reaffirmed its resilience following the conviction.
