Saudi-led coalition airstrikes on Yemen's capital Sanaa have killed an estimated 600 people in just over a week, with fighting destroying infrastructure and cutting electricity to 16 million residents. The Houthi rebels still control the city despite the bombing campaign, while the International Committee of the Red Cross urgently appeals for a humanitarian ceasefire and aid access. The conflict stems from Houthi rebels forcing out President Hadi in January; Saudi Arabia intervened to reinstall him, complicating an already chaotic situation worsened by al Qaeda's presence in eastern Yemen.
