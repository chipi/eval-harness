Nina Dobrev announced she is leaving "The Vampire Diaries" at the end of season six, saying she always envisioned Elena's story as a six-season arc. Many fans expressed anger and sadness over the decision, particularly upset that the show will continue into season seven without her, potentially leaving her character's romance with Damon Salvatore unresolved. Producer Julie Plec said the show will continue focusing on the Salvatore brothers and ensemble cast.
