The FBI has confirmed that Malaysian bomb maker Marwan (Zulkifli bin Hir), a wanted member of Jemaah Islamiyah, was killed in a January raid in the southern Philippines. The operation resulted in 44 elite police commandos being killed and shattered a ceasefire with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front, threatening a fragile peace agreement in the region. Critics argue authorities overemphasized eliminating Marwan, whose actual threat may have been exaggerated, jeopardizing peace efforts in the southern Philippines.
