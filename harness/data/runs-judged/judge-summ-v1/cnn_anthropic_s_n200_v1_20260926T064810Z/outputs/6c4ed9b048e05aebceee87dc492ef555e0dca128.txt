Aaron Hernandez's defense rested Monday after prosecutors spent months presenting 131 witnesses in the former NFL player's trial for the 2013 murder of semi-pro player Odin Lloyd. Key evidence includes Hernandez's suspicious claim to team owner Robert Kraft that he was in a club at the time of the killing—before the time of death was publicly disclosed—and grainy video footage prosecutors say shows him holding a .45-caliber handgun minutes after Lloyd was shot. The defense challenged the prosecution's circumstantial evidence, including questioning the reliability of footprint analysis and suggesting objects in the video could be an iPad or toy gun rather than a weapon.
