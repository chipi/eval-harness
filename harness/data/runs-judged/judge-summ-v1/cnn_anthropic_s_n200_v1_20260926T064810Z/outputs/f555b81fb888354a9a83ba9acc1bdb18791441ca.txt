Jordan Spieth maintained a commanding four-shot lead heading into Sunday's final round at the 2015 Masters after a steady third-round performance, despite a double bogey at the 17th hole. McIlroy, Woods, Mickelson and Rose all made moves on "Moving Day" but failed to significantly close the gap on the 21-year-old leader. Spieth's 15 birdies through three rounds put him on pace to break scoring records at Augusta.
