"Furious 7" earned a record $146 million over its opening weekend, with audiences emotionally moved by its tribute to late actor Paul Walker, who died in 2013 before completing filming. Filmmakers used body doubles, CGI and Walker's brothers to finish his scenes, ultimately choosing to retire his character Brian peacefully rather than kill him off. The film's ending, featuring a montage of Walker scenes set to "See You Again" by Wiz Khalifa and Charlie Puth, resonated deeply with viewers who praised it as a sincere and elegant farewell.
