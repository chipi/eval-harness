A 32-year-old Massachusetts man faces murder charges after human remains were found in a duffel bag in Cambridge on Saturday. Carlos Colina was identified as a suspect through surveillance video, leading police to discover additional body parts at an apartment building; the remains belonged to 26-year-old Jonathan Camilien, who knew Colina. Colina is scheduled for arraignment April 14 and was previously charged with assault, battery and improper disposal of a body.
