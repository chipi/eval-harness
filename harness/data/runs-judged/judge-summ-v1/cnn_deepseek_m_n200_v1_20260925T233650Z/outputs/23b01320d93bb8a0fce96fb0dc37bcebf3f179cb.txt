The FBI has confirmed that Malaysian bomb maker Zulkifli bin Hir, known as Marwan, was killed in a January raid in the Philippines. The raid, which targeted Marwan in Maguindanao province, left 44 Philippine police commandos dead and shattered a ceasefire with the MILF. The FBI had offered a $5 million reward for Marwan, a Jemaah Islamiyah member accused of supplying IEDs and training terror groups.
