A Columbia Journalism School review found Rolling Stone's article about an alleged gang rape at the University of Virginia was the product of "an institutional failure" spanning reporting, editing, supervision and fact-checking. Managing editor Will Dana apologized to readers and to those damaged by the story, including the Phi Kappa Psi fraternity and UVA administrators and students. Legal experts say UVA cannot sue for defamation, but fraternity members could pursue a rare successful case if they clear procedural hurdles and show tangible damages.
