Chinese authorities have formally detained women's rights activist Wu Rongrong and four others on charges of "creating disturbances" after their March detention for planning an International Women's Day protest against sexual harassment. Supporters were briefly detained and an NGO raided, with the five facing release or formal arrest by April 13. The crackdown comes as Beijing prepares to mark the 20th anniversary of the Fourth World Conference on Women and consider its first anti-domestic violence law.
