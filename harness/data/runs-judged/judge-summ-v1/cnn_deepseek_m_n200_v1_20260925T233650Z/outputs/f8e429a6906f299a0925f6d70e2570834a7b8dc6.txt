The Obama administration said Thursday it is "not putting a timeframe on" a possible Iraqi-led invasion to retake Mosul, nearly three months after a U.S. official said up to 25,000 Iraqi troops were expected to return to the key northern city in April or May. Officials stressed that Iraqi forces and their allies are making progress against ISIS, including the recent recapture of Tikrit, but warned that winning Mosul is a complex endeavor that will take time to build.
