Adelma Cifuentes survived years of brutal domestic abuse in rural Guatemala, including an attempted femicide that cost her an arm, before securing justice with her husband sentenced to 27 years. Her case reflects a national crisis: Guatemala ranks third globally in femicides, with deep-rooted machismo, postwar impunity, and weak institutions fueling epidemic gender-based violence. Grassroots groups, legal reforms, and international efforts are slowly driving change, but most cases go unpunished and millions of women remain at risk.
