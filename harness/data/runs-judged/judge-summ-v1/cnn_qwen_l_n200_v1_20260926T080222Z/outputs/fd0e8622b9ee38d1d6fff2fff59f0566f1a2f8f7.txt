"Furious 7" is on track for a $135M–$138M North American debut—the biggest since late 2013—and a potential $300M global opening, fueled by massive interest in the late Paul Walker's final film. The Universal release, playing in over 14,500 screens worldwide (excluding China, Japan, and Russia), marks the studio’s widest rollout ever and is set to claim 2015’s top opening weekend.
