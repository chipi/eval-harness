Kenyans mourn 147 killed in Garissa University attack, the deadliest since the 1998 U.S. Embassy bombing, as social media campaigns like #147notjustanumber share victims' stories. Al-Shabaab militants targeted Christian students, killing 142 students and five security personnel; one attacker was identified as the son of a local chief. Churches held emotional Easter services under armed guard, recalling past attacks including the 2013 Westgate Mall siege.
