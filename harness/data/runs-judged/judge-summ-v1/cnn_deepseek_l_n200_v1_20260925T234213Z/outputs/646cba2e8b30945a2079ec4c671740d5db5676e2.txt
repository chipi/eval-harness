The original handwritten lyrics to Don McLean's 1971 epic "American Pie" sold at Christie's for $1.2 million. The 16-page manuscript of the eight-and-a-half-minute allegorical song about rock 'n' roll and American culture was bought by an unnamed buyer. McLean wrote the song, in part, as a response to the 1959 plane crash that killed Buddy Holly, Ritchie Valens, and J.P. "The Big Bopper" Richardson.
