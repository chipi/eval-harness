A judge ruled Monday that Craig Hicks, charged in the deaths of three Muslim college students in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, is eligible for the death penalty. Hicks, 46, was arrested in February for the execution-style shootings of Yusor Mohammad, Deah Barakat, and Razan Abu-Salha, with authorities investigating both a parking dispute and potential hate crime motives. The DOJ has opened a preliminary inquiry into possible federal hate crime violations.
