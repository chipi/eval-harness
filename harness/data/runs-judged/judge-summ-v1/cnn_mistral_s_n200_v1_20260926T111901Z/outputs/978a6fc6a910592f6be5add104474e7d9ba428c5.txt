**Indian software pioneer Ramalinga Raju and nine others sentenced to seven years in jail for a $1.6 billion fraud at Satyam Computers, dubbed India's biggest corporate scandal. Raju, Satyam's former chairman, admitted inflating profits with fictitious assets, leading to investor losses far exceeding the reported figure. The case, compared to Enron, saw Satyam, once India's fourth-largest IT firm, sold to Tech Mahindra after the 2009 scandal.**
