NBC premieres "A.D. The Bible Continues" Sunday, a sequel to History's 2013 hit "The Bible" from Mark Burnett and Roma Downey, running 12 weeks. Also debuting Sunday: NBC's "American Odyssey" with Anna Friel, while AMC's "Mad Men" begins its final episodes. Later in the week, FX returns "Louie" for a fifth season and launches Billy Crystal's "The Comedians," and Netflix releases "Marvel's Daredevil" on Friday.
