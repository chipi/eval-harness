Saudi-led coalition airstrikes hit Yemen's Defense Ministry Central Command in Sanaa, cutting power to 16 million people in Houthi-held areas, Yemeni officials said. About 600 people have been killed since the campaign began, including more than 50 in Aden on Monday. The ICRC has appealed for a humanitarian ceasefire, with an aid flight carrying 48 tons of medical supplies grounded in Djibouti, while a Russian draft UN resolution to halt the strikes stalled.
