Etihad Airways lost a US couple's two-year-old cat, Felix, after an April 1 flight from Abu Dhabi to New York's JFK Airport, with owner Jennifer Stewart saying the pet's plastic carrier arrived crushed and holed.

The cat remains missing nearly a week later. Etihad, which says pet losses are "extremely rare" and shipped over 200 animals last year, is investigating, has hired third-party specialists and pledged to review its pet handling procedures.

Stewart, who paid $1,200 to ship Felix, has enlisted a nonprofit's detection dog, while a wildlife biologist has set traps at the airport.
