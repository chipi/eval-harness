The US Justice Department has indicted FedEx on 15 counts, including conspiracy and money laundering, alleging the courier knowingly shipped drugs for illegal online pharmacies despite warnings from the DEA, FDA and Congress since 2004. FedEx denies wrongdoing, arguing it is shielded by the "common carrier" exception, which lets transport firms handle controlled substances in the normal course of business without liability. Unlike most companies, which settle via deferred prosecution agreements and fines, FedEx is contesting the charges and seeking dismissal.
