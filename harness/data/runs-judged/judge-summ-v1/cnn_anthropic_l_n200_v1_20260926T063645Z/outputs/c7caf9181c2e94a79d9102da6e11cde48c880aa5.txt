The US and its P5+1 partners reached a framework agreement with Iran in Lausanne, Switzerland, curbing Tehran's nuclear program to block a weapons capability. The deal would cut Iran's low-enriched uranium stockpile, reduce installed centrifuges by two-thirds and impose expanded IAEA inspections under a permanent Additional Protocol. Opponents in Congress and Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu are expected to push back, though supporters say criticisms rest on dubious assumptions about the talks' objectives and terms.
