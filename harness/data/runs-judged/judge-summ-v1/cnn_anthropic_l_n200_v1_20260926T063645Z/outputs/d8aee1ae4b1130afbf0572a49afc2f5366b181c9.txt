A Boston jury found Dzhokhar Tsarnaev guilty on all 30 counts related to the 2013 Boston Marathon bombings and the ensuing violence. Survivors and victims' families in the courtroom wept quietly as the verdicts were read, with many describing relief and accountability rather than celebration. Some, including amputee Rebekah Gregory, said no verdict could deliver full justice, and opinions remained divided over whether Tsarnaev should be sentenced to death.
