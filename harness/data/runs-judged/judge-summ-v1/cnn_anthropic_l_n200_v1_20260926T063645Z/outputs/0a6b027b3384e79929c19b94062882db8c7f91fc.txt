Arsenal beat Burnley 1-0 at Turf Moor thanks to a first-half Aaron Ramsey goal, extending their winning run to eight games and moving within four points of leaders Chelsea, who have two games in hand. The sides meet next week in a potentially title-deciding clash. Elsewhere, Aston Villa won 1-0 at Tottenham through Christian Benteke, Leicester beat West Brom 3-2 with an injury-time goal, and Crystal Palace thrashed Sunderland 4-1 as Yannick Bolasie scored the club's first Premier League hat-trick.
