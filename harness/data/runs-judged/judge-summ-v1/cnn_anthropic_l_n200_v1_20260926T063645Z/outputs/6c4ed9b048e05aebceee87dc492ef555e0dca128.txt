The defense in Aaron Hernandez's murder trial rested after less than a day, following months of prosecution testimony from 131 witnesses. Closing arguments in the case over the 2013 shooting death of Odin Lloyd are set for Tuesday, with much of the evidence circumstantial, including disputed footwear impressions and grainy security video prosecutors say shows a handgun. The former Patriots tight end has pleaded not guilty and faces a separate double-murder trial in Boston.
