Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin says no videos have been used in the Germanwings crash probe, after Bild and Paris Match reported a cell phone clip showing the plane's final seconds; French investigators called the reports "completely wrong." Lufthansa said co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered a "previous episode of severe depression," and is sharing his training and medical records with prosecutors. Investigators believe Lubitz may have feared losing his pilot's licence over medical problems; all 150 aboard died.
