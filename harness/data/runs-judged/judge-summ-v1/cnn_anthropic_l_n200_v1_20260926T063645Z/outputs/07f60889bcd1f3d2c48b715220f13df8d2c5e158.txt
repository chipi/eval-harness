Cape Verde, lacking oil, gas or minerals, is promoting its music and wider creative sector as an engine for economic growth, with backing from the prime minister and a musician-led culture ministry. Initiatives include the Kriol Jazz Festival in Praia, now in its seventh year, and the three-year-old Atlantic Music Expo, which connects local artists with international producers and journalists. Officials hope the global exposure will boost tourism, forecast to reach 20% of GDP by 2024, though a 2013 UN report found the creative sector still small.
