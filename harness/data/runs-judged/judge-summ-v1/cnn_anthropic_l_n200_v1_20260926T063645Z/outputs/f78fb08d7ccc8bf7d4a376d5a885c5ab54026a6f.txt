Mindy Kaling's brother, Vijay Chokalingam, says he posed as African-American to gain admission to medical school as an undergraduate, and is now writing a book about it titled "Almost Black." Kaling reportedly told him the stunt brought "shame upon their family." Critics note Chokalingam received only one offer, from a school ranked around 60th, and that he also faced racial profiling by police and store clerks while posing as black.
