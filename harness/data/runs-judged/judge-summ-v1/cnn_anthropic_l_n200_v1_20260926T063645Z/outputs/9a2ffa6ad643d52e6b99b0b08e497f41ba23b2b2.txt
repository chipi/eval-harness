Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu criticised the framework nuclear deal between Iran and six world powers on CNN's "State of the Union," saying a third option exists beyond "this bad deal or war" — maintaining pressure to secure better terms. He said the deal leaves Iran's nuclear infrastructure intact and that Tehran, guilty of "congenital cheating," cannot be trusted.

Democratic Sen. Dianne Feinstein said she wished Netanyahu "would contain himself," calling the deal "on the cusp of something that can be workable," while Energy Secretary Ernest Moniz said it extends Iran's breakout time from two months to a year. Republican Sen. Lindsey Graham urged keeping sanctions for two years until a new president takes office.
