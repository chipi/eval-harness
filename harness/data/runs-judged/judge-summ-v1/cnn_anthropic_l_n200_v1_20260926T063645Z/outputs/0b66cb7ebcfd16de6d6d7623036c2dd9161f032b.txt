Original "Twin Peaks" cast members have released a video and launched a #SaveTwinPeaks campaign supporting co-creator David Lynch in his pay dispute with Showtime, saying the revival without him would be "like pies without cherries." Lynch said Sunday he was quitting the nine-episode reboot because there was "not enough money offered to do the script the way I felt needed to be done." Showtime says it "continues to hold out hope" of reviving the series with both Lynch and Mark Frost; Kyle MacLachlan is the only cast member confirmed.
