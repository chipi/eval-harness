Amnesty International's report "Their Lives on the Line" says women's rights activists in Afghanistan face attacks from the Taliban, warlords and government officials, with perpetrators rarely punished. The group says Afghan authorities offer female defenders less protection than male colleagues, and police stood by during the mob killing of Farkhunda in Kabul. Activists say the funeral, at which women carried the coffin for the first time, has galvanised the women's movement.
