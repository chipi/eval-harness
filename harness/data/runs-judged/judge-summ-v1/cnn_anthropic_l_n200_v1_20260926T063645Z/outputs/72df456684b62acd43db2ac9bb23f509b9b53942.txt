Ted Cruz made his first Iowa swing as a declared presidential candidate, courting the state's large evangelical bloc with talk of his faith, his pastor father and defence of Indiana's "religious freedom" law. His first TV ads, airing around Easter, cite the "transformative love of Jesus Christ". Advisers say evangelicals are one of three target groups alongside tea party and libertarian voters, though rivals including Huckabee, Santorum, Bush, Walker, Carson, Rubio and Paul are also vying for religious conservatives.
