California regulators ordered Pacific Gas & Electric to pay a record $1.6 billion penalty over unsafe gas system operations, including the 2010 San Bruno pipeline explosion that killed eight people and destroyed 37 homes. Most of the sum is mandated spending on pipeline safety upgrades, including $850 million for infrastructure improvements. PG&E, which says it has paid over $500 million in victim claims and replaced more than 800 miles of pipe, apologised for the blast.
