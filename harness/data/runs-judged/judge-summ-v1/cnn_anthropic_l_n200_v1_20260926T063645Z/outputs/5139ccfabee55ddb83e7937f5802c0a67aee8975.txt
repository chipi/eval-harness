The U.S. is filing a diplomatic complaint with Moscow after a Russian SU-27 intercepted a U.S. RC-135U reconnaissance plane over the Baltic Sea on Tuesday, in what the Pentagon called an "unsafe and unprofessional" manner. Russia's Defense Ministry said the U.S. plane was heading toward its border with its transponder off, a claim U.S. European Command denied. A similar incident occurred a year ago over the Sea of Okhotsk, when a Russian jet flew within 100 feet of a U.S. plane.
