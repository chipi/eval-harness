Billboard runs down the hits and misses of the 2015 MTV Movie Awards. Highlights included host Amy Schumer's opening segment and monologue, Channing Tatum twerking onstage for Jennifer Lopez, Rebel Wilson's off-colour ad-lib, and Robert Downey Jr.'s Generation Award. Misfires included Schumer's botched archery gag, a poorly received "Gone Girl" joke, and Shailene Woodley's rambling Trailblazer speech.
