AMC's "Mad Men" returns for its final season, navigating the disintegration of the 1960s and the advertising world amid historical events like the Manson murders and Woodstock. Creator Matthew Weiner remains tight-lipped about the finale, though the series is expected to explore the agency's struggle for survival following its McCann Erickson acquisition. The conclusion may find protagonist Don Draper facing a ban on cigarette advertising, leaving him alone and inscrutable as the era ends.
