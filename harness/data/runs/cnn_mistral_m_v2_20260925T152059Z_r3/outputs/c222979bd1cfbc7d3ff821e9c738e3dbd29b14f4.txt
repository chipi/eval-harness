China held a mass public sentencing in Xinjiang in 2014, part of its "Strike Hard" campaign, as global death penalty use rose, with countries like Pakistan, Jordan, and Indonesia resuming executions amid terrorism and crime concerns. Amnesty International reports a 2014 spike in death sentences, though overall executions dropped, with 140 nations now abolitionist. The group urges an end to capital punishment, citing its ineffectiveness as a deterrent.
