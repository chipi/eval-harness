7-Eleven is hosting its first Bring-Your-Own-Cup Slurpee Day at US stores Saturday from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., letting customers fill a container of their choice for $1.49. Cups must be sanitary and fit within a 10-inch-diameter hole; garbage cans are not allowed. The event is separate from the traditional Free Slurpee Day on July 11.
