Philadelphia police have added attempted murder to charges against Nyia Parler, accused of leaving her quadriplegic 21-year-old son in a park for days while she traveled to Maryland. Parler, 41, remains in custody in Maryland receiving unspecified treatment and cannot be extradited until it is complete. The son was found Friday in Cobbs Creek Park with a Bible and wheelchair nearby, suffering dehydration, malnutrition and other injuries.
