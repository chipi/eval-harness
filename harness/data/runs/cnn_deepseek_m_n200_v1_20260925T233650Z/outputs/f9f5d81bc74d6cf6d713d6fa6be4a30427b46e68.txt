A South Korean court charged Kim Ki-Jong with attempted murder, assaulting a foreign envoy and business obstruction for stabbing U.S. Ambassador Mark Lippert in Seoul last month. Lippert was slashed in the face and arm during a March 5 event and required 80 stitches, but suffered no serious nerve damage. Kim, who has a history of unpredictable behavior, opposed joint South Korean-U.S. military drills.
