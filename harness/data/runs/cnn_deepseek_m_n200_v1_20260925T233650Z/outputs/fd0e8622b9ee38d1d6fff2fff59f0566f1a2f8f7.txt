"Furious 7" is eyeing a $135 million-$138 million domestic debut, the largest North American opening since fall 2013, with a projected $58 million-$60 million Friday gross. The Universal film, the last featuring the late Paul Walker, also earned a record-breaking $60 million internationally Wednesday and Thursday, with a worldwide debut potentially nearing or crossing $300 million by Easter Sunday. It opens in 4,003 domestic theaters and more than 10,500 international screens in 63 territories.
