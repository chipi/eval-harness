UNRWA Commissioner-General Pierre Krähenbühl will make an emergency visit Saturday to the Yarmouk Palestinian refugee camp in Syria to assess the humanitarian situation. The camp, largely cut off from aid since November 2013, is home to about 18,000 Palestinians and Syrian civilians, including 3,500 children, and remains under the control of armed groups including ISIS and Al-Nusra Front. Krähenbühl will meet with Syrian officials, UN staff and displaced camp residents.
