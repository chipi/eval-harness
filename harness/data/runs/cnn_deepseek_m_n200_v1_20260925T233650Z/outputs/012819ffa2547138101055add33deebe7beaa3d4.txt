The Taliban published an 11-page biography of Mullah Mohammed Omar, claiming he remains the group's leader, in an apparent effort to dispel rumors of his death. The document, released on the 19th anniversary of the Taliban's founding, also seeks to reassert Omar's jihadi credentials amid a leadership challenge from ISIS's self-declared caliph. Omar, who has not been seen or heard from in years, is described as overseeing the Taliban's leadership council and military commanders across Afghanistan.
