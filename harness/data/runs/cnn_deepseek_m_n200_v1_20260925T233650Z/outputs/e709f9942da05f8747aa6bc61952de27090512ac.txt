Obama tells the New York Times he is "absolutely committed" to maintaining Israel's military edge over Iran amid Netanyahu's criticism of the Iran nuclear deal. He says he would commit to defending Israel if attacked by any state, calling the deal a "once-in-a-lifetime opportunity" to take the nuclear issue off the table. The framework would cut Iran's centrifuges from 19,000 to 5,060, with final talks set to continue until June 30.
