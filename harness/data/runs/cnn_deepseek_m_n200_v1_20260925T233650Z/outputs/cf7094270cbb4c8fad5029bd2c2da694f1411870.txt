A CNN roundup details several real-life cases of people surviving days, weeks or months stranded at sea, including Louis Jordan, rescued off North Carolina after 66 days; Jose Salvador Alvarenga, who drifted from Mexico to the Marshall Islands over 13 months; and Ron Ingraham, picked up near Honolulu after 12 days. Other cases include Panamanian teenager Adrian Vasquez, adrift 26 days before being rescued near the Galapagos, and Hiromitsu Shinkawa, who clung to debris for two days after Japan's 2011 tsunami. Such survival stories are rare, with fatal outcomes far more common.
