Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil delivered a widely panned rendition of the national anthem before a Las Vegas Outlaws arena football game, joining a long list of famous anthem performances ranging from Whitney Houston's celebrated 1991 Super Bowl version to Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 outing. Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's lyric-flubbing 2003 performance round out the notable hits and misses.
