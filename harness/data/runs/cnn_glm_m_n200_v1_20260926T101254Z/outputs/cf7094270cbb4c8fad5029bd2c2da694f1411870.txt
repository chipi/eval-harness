Recent maritime castaways survived extreme ordeals adrift at sea through rationing, ingenuity, and luck. Survivors include Louis Jordan, rescued after 66 days off North Carolina; Jose Salvador Alvarenga, who drifted across the Pacific for over a year; Ron Ingraham, found after 12 days near Hawaii; and Adrian Vasquez, rescued near the Galapagos after 26 days. Hiromitsu Shinkawa also survived after being swept 15 kilometers out to sea by the 2011 Japanese tsunami.
