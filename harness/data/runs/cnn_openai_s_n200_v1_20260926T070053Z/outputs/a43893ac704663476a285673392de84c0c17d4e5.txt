Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars’ “Uptown Funk!” logs a 13th week at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, extending its record as the decade’s longest-leading hit and tying for 10th-longest run in chart history. Natalie La Rose’s debut “Somebody,” featuring Jeremih, breaks into the top 10, while Maroon 5’s “Sugar” and Ed Sheeran’s “Thinking Out Loud” remain the main challengers.
