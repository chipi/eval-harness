**China held a mass sentencing of 55 people, including three death sentences, in Xinjiang in 2014 as part of a "Strike Hard" campaign. Amnesty International's report highlights a global rise in executions, with Pakistan, Iran, and others using the death penalty to combat terrorism and crime, despite evidence it lacks deterrent effect. However, executions worldwide fell to 607 (excluding China), with 140 countries now abolitionist in law or practice.**
