Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil delivered a widely criticized rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas football game. The performance joins a history of controversial national anthems, from Roseanne Barr's "disgraceful" version to Jimi Hendrix's iconic Woodstock take. The article notes that performing the anthem is a high-risk endeavor that can make a celebrity a national hero or a disgrace.
