Indiana Gov. Mike Pence signed a religious freedom law sparking backlash, but the move was aimed at shoring up ultraconservative support for a potential 2016 presidential run. The controversy is dragging the entire Republican field further right on LGBT rights, with multiple states considering similar bills. Despite national polling showing increased support for same-sex marriage, the GOP primary process is forcing candidates to take hardline stances, potentially turning the 2016 election into a cultural war.
