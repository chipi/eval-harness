The Rev. Robert H. Schuller, the California televangelist who founded the "Hour of Power" broadcast and the Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at 88, his family said. He had been diagnosed with esophageal cancer in August 2013.

Schuller began preaching from a drive-in theater snack bar roof and built a global ministry on an upbeat "possibility thinking" message that critics dismissed as thin theology. His empire unraveled amid a family succession dispute, and Crystal Cathedral Ministries filed for bankruptcy in 2010 with debts topping $43 million.
