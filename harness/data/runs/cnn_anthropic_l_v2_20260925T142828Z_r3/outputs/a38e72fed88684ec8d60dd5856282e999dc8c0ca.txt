Five American aid workers monitored for three weeks at an Omaha, Nebraska, hospital after Ebola exposure in Sierra Leone have been released, none having contracted the virus, Nebraska Medicine said. The clinicians, who work for Boston-based Partners in Health, had contact with a colleague now in fair condition at the National Institutes of Health in Maryland. The CDC said the last of 17 patients under monitoring were expected to be released by Thursday; the West African outbreak has killed more than 10,000 since December 2013.
