Former Rep. Michele Bachmann, R-Minnesota, compared President Obama's Iran nuclear deal to Andreas Lubitz, the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing Flight 9525, calling Obama "a deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks." In a March 31 Facebook post, she wrote that survivors would ask why "among the smoldering remains of American cities."

Many commenters on her page called the remarks despicable and accused her of seeking attention. Bachmann previously said she urged Obama at a 2014 White House Christmas party to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities.
