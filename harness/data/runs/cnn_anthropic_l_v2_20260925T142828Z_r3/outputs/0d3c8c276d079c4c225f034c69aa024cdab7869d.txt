Andrew Getty, 47, grandson of oil tycoon J. Paul Getty, has died at his Los Angeles home, with the coroner's preliminary assessment finding no foul play, police said. Getty had "several health issues" and medication was recovered from the home; an autopsy is planned and no criminal investigation is underway. He had recently sought a restraining order against an ex-girlfriend, citing a 2013 diagnosis of a serious condition worsened by high blood pressure.
