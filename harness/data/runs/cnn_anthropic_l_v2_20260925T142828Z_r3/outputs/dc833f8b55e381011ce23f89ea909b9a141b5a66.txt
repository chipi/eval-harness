Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill, along with criticism from Apple, NASCAR and other companies, helped prompt Republican Gov. Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign the legislation unless its language was changed.

The retailer's stance, following its February decision to raise pay for 500,000 workers, marks a gradual shift at a company whose PAC has historically favored Republicans, and reflects broader business support for LGBT protections.

Former Minnesota Gov. Tim Pawlenty said Walmart's moves "foreshadow where the Republican Party will need to move," while Sen. Ted Cruz accused companies of following an "extreme left wing agenda."
