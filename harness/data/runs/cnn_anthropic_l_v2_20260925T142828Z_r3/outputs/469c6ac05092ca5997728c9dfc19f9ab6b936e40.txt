Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin says no videos have been used in the Germanwings crash investigation, after Bild and Paris Match reported a cell phone video showing the plane's final seconds. French authorities, including a Gendarmerie spokesman, called the reports "completely wrong," saying phones recovered at the site have not yet been analysed; Bild's editor-in-chief stands by the story. Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered a "previous episode of severe depression"; investigators suspect he feared losing his licence over medical problems.
