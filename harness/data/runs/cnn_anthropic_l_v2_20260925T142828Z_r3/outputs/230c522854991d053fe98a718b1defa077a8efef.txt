A stray dog in Washington state survived being hit by a car, struck on the head with a hammer and buried in a field, staggering to a nearby farm four days later. Now named Theia, the one-year-old suffered a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity, and is being treated at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital. Her foster carer's fundraising page has passed its $10,000 target, with surplus funds to go to other animals.
