Mötley Crüe singer Vince Neil drew widespread criticism this week for his rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas Outlaws arena football game. He joins a long list of celebrities judged on anthem performances, including Whitney Houston's acclaimed 1991 Super Bowl version and Jimi Hendrix's contentious 1969 Woodstock take. Others fared worse: Roseanne Barr was booed and rebuked by President George H.W. Bush in 1990, while Michael Bolton resorted to written lyrics in 2003.
