Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's decision to sign a religious freedom law seen as enabling discrimination against gays and lesbians is being read as a bid to shore up conservative credentials ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run. Jeb Bush quickly defended the law, while Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker have also staked out positions against same-sex marriage. The ACLU says Indiana's measure is one of 24 such bills introduced in 15 states this year, suggesting LGBT rights will be a major issue in the Republican primary rather than a sideshow.
