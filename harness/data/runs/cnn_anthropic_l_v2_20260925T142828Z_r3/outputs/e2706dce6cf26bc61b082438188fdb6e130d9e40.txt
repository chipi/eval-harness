Amnesty International says governments are using terrorism threats, real or imagined, to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifting of a moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang. Executions fell nearly 22% worldwide to at least 607 in 2014, but death sentences rose 28% to at least 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria. Iran, Iraq and Saudi Arabia executed more people than the US, the only country in the Americas to carry out executions; China's totals remain a state secret but are believed to run into the thousands.
