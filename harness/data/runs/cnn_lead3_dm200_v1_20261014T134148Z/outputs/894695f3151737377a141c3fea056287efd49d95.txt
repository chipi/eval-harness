Masks were donned, capes were tied and tights were squeezed into as thousands of comic book fans around the world dressed up in unison in an attempt to break a world record. Warner Bros and DC Entertainment attempted to set a world record for the largest gatherings of people dressed as DC Comics Superheroes within 24 hours. The April 18 event kicked off in Queensland, Australia, with a celebration at the Movie World Australia theme park and ended in Los Angeles, California, at the Hollywood & Highland Center.
