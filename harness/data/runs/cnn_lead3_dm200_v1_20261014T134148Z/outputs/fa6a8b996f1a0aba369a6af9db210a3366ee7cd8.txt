The time is right for Manchester United target Mats Hummels to make the switch to the Premier League, according to Germany legend Franz Beckenbauer. The World Cup winning defender has been consistently linked with a move to Old Trafford in recent seasons and with Dortmund likely to miss out on European football next season, Hummels is considering his future. Beckenbauer, who won 103 caps for West Germany, can believes Hummels is at the perfect point in his career to leave Jurgen Klopp's side.
