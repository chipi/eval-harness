A group of daring drift trike riders have captured some amaznig footage as they zipped down the world’s steepest residential road on tricycles. Harley Jolly, Tyson Barr and Nic Roy – known collectively as Slide Christchurch – videoed themselves descending New Zealand's Dunedin’s Baldwin Street on three-wheeled vehicles known as 'slide trikes'. The short clip opens with a warning message, before the riders take their position at the top of the hill, famed for its 35 per cent gradient.
