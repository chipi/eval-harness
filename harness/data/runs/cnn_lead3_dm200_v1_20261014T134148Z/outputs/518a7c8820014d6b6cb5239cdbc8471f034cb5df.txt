Former Manchester United and Burnley full-back Richard Eckersley is training with La Liga side Elche. Eckersley begun his career at the Red Devils but after failing to make an impact in Manchester, he rejected the offer of a new contract and signed for Premier League newcomers Burnley. But an unsuccessful three years in Lancashire, where Eckersley failed to make a league appearance, saw him make a move to MLS outfit Toronto FC on loan.
