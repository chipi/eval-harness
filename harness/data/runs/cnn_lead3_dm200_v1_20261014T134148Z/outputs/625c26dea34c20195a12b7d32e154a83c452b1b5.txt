The tragic death of a seven-week-old baby in his mother’s bed has led a coroner to demand new guidelines to warn parents of the dangers of sleeping next to newborns. ‘Well cared-for and loved’ Efan James was found lying face down and unresponsive the morning after Hannah James, 26, took him to bed. At the baby’s inquest this week, a coroner in Pembrokeshire said new parents were given ‘confusing’ advice over how tired they could be when they slept in the same bed as their child.
