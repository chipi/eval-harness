Just a quarter of voters think David Cameron is hungriest to win the election, a poll reveals today as the Prime Minister tries to step up his campaign passion. After criticism of a dull campaign from the chillaxing PM, Mr Cameron used an interview to insist he was working 'bloody hard' to secure a majority. But only 25 per cent of people think he wants to win most, compared to 43 per cent who believe Ed Miliband is most up for the fight.
