The real-life Margo Leadbetter has backed the Prime Minister to deliver The Good Life to Britain. Sheila Elkan, who lives in the house that featured as the Leadbetters' home in the classic 1970s TV series, backed David Cameron today after he launched his 'Good Life' manifesto. The 84-year-old great-grandmother-of-five has resided at the home in Hillingdon, north-west London, since 1986.
