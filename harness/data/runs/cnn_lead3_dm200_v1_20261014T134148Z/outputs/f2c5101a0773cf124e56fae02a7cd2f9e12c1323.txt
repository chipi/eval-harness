Countryfile presenter Ellie Harrison said that she completely 'accepts' that she will one day be replaced on the BBC show. The 37-year-old, who has defended her image against criticism that she's too 'Hollywood', told The Mirror: 'You're hot one year but completely out of favour the next. But the mother-of-two, who joined the programme in 2011, said she'd love to stay where she is for 'a long time'.
