Leighton Baines is well known for his love of music but it appears his appreciation does not extend to all genres - specifically the latest pop songs dominating the charts. The Everton defender, who is friends with rocker Miles Kane and Arctic Monkeys' front-man Alex Turner, regularly attends gigs and also plays the guitar. But in an interview with Match of the Day magazine, the England international has given his thoughts on six mainstream tracks which don't feature on his own iPod.
