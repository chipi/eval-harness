Amnesty International reports a global decline in executions in 2014 but a 28% rise in death sentences, citing governments' use of capital punishment to counter terrorism or unrest. While executions fell to at least 607 worldwide (excluding China, which executes thousands secretly), mass sentencing surged in Egypt and Nigeria. The U.S., Iran, Iraq, and Saudi Arabia remain among the top executioners, and most death penalty cases failed to meet fair trial standards.
