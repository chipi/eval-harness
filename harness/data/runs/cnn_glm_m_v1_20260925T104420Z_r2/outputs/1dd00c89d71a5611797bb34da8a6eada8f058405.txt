Indiana Gov. Mike Pence signed a controversial religious freedom law to shore up conservative credentials for a potential 2016 White House bid, drawing widespread backlash but gaining favor with the GOP's right wing. The resulting furor, echoed by similar bills in other states, is forcing the entire Republican presidential field to the right on LGBT issues. Despite national progress on gay rights, conservatives' focus on "religious freedom" legislation threatens to ignite a cultural war that could mobilize right-leaning voters in 2016.
