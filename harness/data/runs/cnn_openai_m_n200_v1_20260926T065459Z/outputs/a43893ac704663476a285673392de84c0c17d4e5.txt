Mark Ronson’s “Uptown Funk!” featuring Bruno Mars topped the Billboard Hot 100 for a 13th week, the longest No. 1 run of the 2010s and tied for the 10th-longest in chart history. Natalie La Rose’s debut hit “Somebody,” featuring Jeremih, entered the top 10, while Maroon 5’s “Sugar” and Ed Sheeran’s “Thinking Out Loud” held at Nos. 2 and 3.
