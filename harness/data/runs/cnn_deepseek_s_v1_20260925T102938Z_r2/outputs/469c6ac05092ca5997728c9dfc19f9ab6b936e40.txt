French prosecutor Brice Robin denied awareness of any video from Germanwings Flight 9525, urging anyone with such footage to hand it over. Reports of a cell phone video showing the crash's final seconds were dismissed as "completely wrong" by French authorities, who said no phones have been analyzed. Separately, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had battled severe depression in 2009, with investigators probing if he feared losing his pilot's license due to medical issues.
