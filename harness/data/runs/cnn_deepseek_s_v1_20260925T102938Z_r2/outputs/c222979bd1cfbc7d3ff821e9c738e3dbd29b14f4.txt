On May 28, 2014, China publicly sentenced 55 people in Xinjiang, including three to death, as part of its "Strike Hard" anti-terrorism campaign. Amnesty International's 2014 review shows a global rise in death sentences, with countries like Pakistan, Iran, and Indonesia using executions as a political or security response. Despite this, global executions fell to 607 in 2014, and 140 countries have now abolished the death penalty in law or practice.
