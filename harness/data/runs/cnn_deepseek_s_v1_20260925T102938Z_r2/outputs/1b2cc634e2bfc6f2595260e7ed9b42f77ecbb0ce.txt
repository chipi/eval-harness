Trey Moses, a star high school basketball recruit, asked Ellie Meredith, a freshman with Down syndrome, to be his prom date. The prom-posal, made during Ellie's P.E. class with flowers and a Taylor Swift-themed poster, was approved by her parents and has gone viral. Trey, who plans to major in special education, said Ellie is "easy to talk to" and "great."
