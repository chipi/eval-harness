Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif received a hero's welcome on returning to Tehran after reaching a framework nuclear deal with world powers. Less widely known details include his US education at San Francisco State and the University of Denver, his participation in a student takeover of Iran's San Francisco consulate during the revolution, and a US investigation into his alleged role with the Alavi Foundation. Zarif and US Secretary of State John Kerry face a June 30 deadline to finalise the agreement's details.
