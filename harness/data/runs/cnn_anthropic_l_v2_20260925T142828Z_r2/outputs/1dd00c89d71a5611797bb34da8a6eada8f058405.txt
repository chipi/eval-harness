Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's signing of a religious freedom law seen as enabling anti-LGBT discrimination is viewed as an effort to bolster his conservative credentials ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run. Jeb Bush was among the first to defend him, and similar bills have been introduced in 15 states, with Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker also opposing same-sex marriage. The commentary argues the controversy will push the GOP field rightward and make LGBT rights an unavoidable 2016 campaign issue.
