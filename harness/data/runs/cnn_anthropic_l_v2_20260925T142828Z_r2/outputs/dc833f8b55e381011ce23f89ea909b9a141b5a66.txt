Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill prompted Republican Gov. Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign it without changes, adding to a broad business backlash that also pressured Indiana Gov. Mike Pence to amend his state's law. The retailer's stance, following its February pay raise for 500,000 workers, is being cited as a bellwether of shifting opinion in the heartland, with former Minnesota Gov. Tim Pawlenty saying it "foreshadows" where the GOP must move. Republican presidential hopefuls split, with Jeb Bush softening his initial support and Ted Cruz attacking companies for following an "extreme left wing agenda."
