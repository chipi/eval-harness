A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck on the head with a hammer and buried in a field, staggering to a nearby farm four days later. Named Theia, the one-year-old is being treated at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital for a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity. A fundraising page set up by her foster carer has already passed its $10,000 target.
