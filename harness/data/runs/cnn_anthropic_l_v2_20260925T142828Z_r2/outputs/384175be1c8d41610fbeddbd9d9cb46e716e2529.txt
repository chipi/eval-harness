"Furious 7" opens Friday, more than a year after star Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40. Producers declined to kill off his character, instead rewriting the script and using special effects and Walker's brothers as body doubles. Co-star Vin Diesel, who named his newborn daughter after Walker, gave a tearful speech at Wednesday's Hollywood premiere; Walker's brother Cody said the film is "bittersweet" but "Paul would be proud."
