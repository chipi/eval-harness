Amnesty International's annual report says governments are invoking real or perceived terrorist threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifting of a moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang.

Executions worldwide fell about 22% to at least 607 in 2014, excluding China, which Amnesty believes kills thousands annually. However, death sentences rose 28% to at least 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria; the US was the only country in the Americas to execute, putting 35 people to death.
