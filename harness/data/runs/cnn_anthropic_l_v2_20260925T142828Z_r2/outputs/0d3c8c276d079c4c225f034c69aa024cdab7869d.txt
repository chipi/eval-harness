Andrew Getty, 47, grandson of oil tycoon J. Paul Getty, was found dead at his Los Angeles home, with police saying he appears to have died of natural causes. The coroner's preliminary assessment found no foul play, and an autopsy is planned; Getty reportedly had several health issues and medication was recovered from his home. In a recent restraining order request against an ex-girlfriend, Getty said he had been diagnosed with a serious medical condition in 2013 that made rising blood pressure life-threatening.
