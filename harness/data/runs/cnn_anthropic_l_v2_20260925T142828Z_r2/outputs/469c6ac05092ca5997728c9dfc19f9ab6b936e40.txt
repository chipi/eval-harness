Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin said no videos have been used in the Germanwings crash probe, disputing reports by Bild and Paris Match that a recovered cell phone video captured the plane's final moments. French officials called the reports "completely wrong," saying cell phones collected at the site have not yet been analysed.

Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered "a previous episode of severe depression"; investigators believe he may have feared losing his licence over medical problems.
