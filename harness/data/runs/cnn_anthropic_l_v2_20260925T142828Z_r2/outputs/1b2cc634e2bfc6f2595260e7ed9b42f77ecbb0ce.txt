Louisville, Kentucky basketball recruit Trey Moses asked Ellie Meredith, a high school freshman with Down syndrome, to be his prom date, surprising her in her P.E. class with flowers and a Taylor Swift-themed poster. Photos of the "prom-posal," posted on Twitter by a teacher, have gone viral. Moses, who is headed to Ball State next year, plans to major in special education; Meredith's mother said the school's Best Buddies program has helped her daughter make friends.
