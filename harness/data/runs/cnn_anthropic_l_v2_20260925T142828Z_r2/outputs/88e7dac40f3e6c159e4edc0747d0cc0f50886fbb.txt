The Rev. Robert H. Schuller, the California televangelist who founded the "Hour of Power" broadcast and the Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at 88. He had been diagnosed with esophageal cancer in August 2013, his ministry said. Schuller built a following with an upbeat "possibility thinking" gospel, but his ministry collapsed amid a family succession dispute and a 2010 bankruptcy filing citing more than $43 million in debts.
