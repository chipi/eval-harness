Mötley Crüe singer Vince Neil drew criticism this week for a poorly received rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas Outlaws Arena Football League game. The performance joins a long list of memorable anthem attempts, from Whitney Houston's acclaimed 1991 Super Bowl version to Roseanne Barr's 1990 rendition, which drew boos and a rebuke from President George H.W. Bush. Others include Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 ALCS performance, during which he needed written lyrics.
