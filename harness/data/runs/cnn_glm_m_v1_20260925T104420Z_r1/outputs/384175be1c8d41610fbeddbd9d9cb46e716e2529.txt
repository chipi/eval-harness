Paul Walker died in a 2013 car crash during production of "Furious 7," prompting filmmakers to rewrite the script and use his brothers as body doubles to complete his role. The film serves as a tribute to the late actor, with co-star Vin Diesel calling it "more than a movie" and fans mourning the loss of the "Fast and Furious" star. Walker's character is retired in a touching finale, honoring his legacy and the impact he had on audiences and colleagues.
