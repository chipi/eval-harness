French authorities deny the existence of a cell phone video from the Germanwings crash site, contradicting reports by Bild and Paris Match claiming to have seen footage of the plane's final seconds. Meanwhile, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz notified the airline of a severe depressive episode in 2009 but was still permitted to fly. Investigators suspect Lubitz deliberately downed the plane, killing all 150 aboard, fearing his medical issues would cost him his pilot's license.
