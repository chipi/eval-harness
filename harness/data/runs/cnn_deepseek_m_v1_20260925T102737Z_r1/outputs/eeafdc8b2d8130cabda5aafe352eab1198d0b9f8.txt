Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil delivered a widely panned rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas Outlaws arena football game. His performance joins a history of infamous anthem moments, from Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 version to Whitney Houston's celebrated 1991 Super Bowl performance. Jimi Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 lyric-flubbing rendition also made the list.
