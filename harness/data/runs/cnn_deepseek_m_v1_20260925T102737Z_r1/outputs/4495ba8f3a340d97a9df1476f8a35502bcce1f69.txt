Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, a key figure in nuclear talks with the U.S., is known for his jovial demeanor and has received a hero's welcome in Iran. Less known are details like his U.S. education, a 2013 Rosh Hashanah tweet exchange, and his involvement in the 1979 takeover of the Iranian Consulate in San Francisco. He has spent more time with Secretary of State John Kerry than any other foreign minister as they work toward a June 30 deadline for a nuclear deal.
