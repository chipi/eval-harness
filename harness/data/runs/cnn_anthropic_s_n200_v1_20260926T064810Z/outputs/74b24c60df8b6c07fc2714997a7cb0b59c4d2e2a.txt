Marvel Studios and other studios released a flurry of character reveals Thursday, including Paul Bettany's Vision in "Avengers: Age of Ultron," Charlie Cox's Daredevil suit, Ben Hardy as Angel in "X-Men: Apocalypse," and Jamie Bell's CGI-rendered Thing in the "Fantastic Four" reboot. The simultaneous announcements underscore the superhero genre's continued dominance in Hollywood. Ryan Reynolds' "Deadpool" also received a new photo release during the same promotional blitz.
