Ryan Gosling's directorial debut "Lost River," which premiered at Cannes a year ago to mixed reviews, finally receives theatrical release on April 10. The film began as Gosling's personal project documenting Detroit's decaying Brewster-Douglass Projects before they were demolished, evolving into a post-industrial gothic narrative about families struggling to survive urban decay. While depicting the city's destruction and abandonment, Gosling emphasizes the film also captures Detroit's resilience and ongoing rebirth through community efforts and urban renewal.
