Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" featuring Bruno Mars extends its reign atop the Billboard Hot 100 to 13 weeks, becoming the longest-leading song of the 2010s and just the 10th single ever to spend at least 13 weeks at No. 1. The track maintains strong performance across all metrics—digital sales, radio airplay and streaming—while Natalie La Rose's debut hit "Somebody" enters the top 10 at No. 10.
