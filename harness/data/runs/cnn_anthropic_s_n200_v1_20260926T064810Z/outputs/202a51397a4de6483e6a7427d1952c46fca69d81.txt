Avril Lavigne revealed she was bedridden for five months with Lyme disease after being bitten by a tick last spring, causing severe symptoms including inability to breathe, talk, or move. The Canadian singer recuperated at her Ontario home with support from her husband Chad Kroeger and mother while doctors initially struggled to diagnose her condition. Now 80 percent recovered, Lavigne is releasing new music and says the illness has given her a new perspective on life.
