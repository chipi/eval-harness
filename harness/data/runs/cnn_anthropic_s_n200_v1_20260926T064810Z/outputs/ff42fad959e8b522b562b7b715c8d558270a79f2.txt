A Secret Service supervisor was placed on administrative leave after a female employee accused him of sexual assault at an agency party, telling him she wasn't interested in his advances and later resisting his attempt to kiss her in the office. The incident marks another scandal for the agency, which has faced multiple high-profile missteps in recent months including security lapses at the White House and management failures. Director Joe Clancy called the allegations "very disturbing" and said workplace violence is unacceptable.
