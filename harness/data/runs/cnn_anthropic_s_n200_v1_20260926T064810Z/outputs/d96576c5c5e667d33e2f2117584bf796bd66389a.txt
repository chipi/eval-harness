China's former security chief Zhou Yongkang, a former member of the Communist Party's top decision-making body, has been formally charged with bribery, abuse of power, and leaking state secrets—making him the highest-ranking party official ever to face such charges. Zhou, who controlled police and spy agencies until his 2012 retirement, allegedly accumulated enormous wealth and abused his position; his case will be tried in Tianjin. His prosecution is seen as a major moment in President Xi Jinping's anti-corruption campaign and a demonstration of Xi's political power to hold even top-tier former leaders accountable.
