Chelsea Manning, imprisoned for leaking classified documents, has joined Twitter and will dictate tweets via phone to a communications firm since she lacks internet access in prison. Manning, serving a 35-year sentence, has nearly 26,000 followers and says she wants the account to be a two-way conversation. The Fort Leavenworth prison has authorized hormone therapy for Manning, who was convicted in 2013 of stealing and disseminating 750,000 pages of documents to WikiLeaks.
