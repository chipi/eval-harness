Robert Lewis Burns Jr., original drummer for Southern rock band Lynyrd Skynyrd, died Friday in a single-vehicle crash in Cartersville, Georgia at age 64. Burns was an unrestrained passenger when his car hit a mailbox and tree shortly before midnight. He had left the band in 1975 before its third album and was not involved in the 1977 plane crash that killed three members including lead singer Ronnie Van Zant.
