Former NFL player Aaron Hernandez was convicted of first-degree murder and sentenced to life in prison Wednesday for the June 2013 killing of 27-year-old Odin Lloyd. Lloyd's family members delivered emotional victim impact statements in the Fall River, Massachusetts courtroom, describing him as a devoted son, uncle and athlete who worked for a landscaping firm and played semi-pro football. Lloyd's mother told the court she forgives those responsible for her son's death.
