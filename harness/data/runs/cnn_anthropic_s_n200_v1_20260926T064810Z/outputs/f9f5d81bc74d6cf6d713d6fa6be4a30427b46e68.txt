Kim Ki-Jong has been charged with attempted murder, assault on a foreign envoy, and business obstruction for stabbing U.S. Ambassador Mark Lippert in Seoul on March 5. Lippert suffered a 10-centimeter gash to his face requiring 80 stitches and cuts to his arm, but is expected to recover without permanent damage. Police say Kim, who has a history of violence and visited North Korea seven times, attacked Lippert with a 10-inch knife to protest joint South Korean-U.S. military drills.
