# Summary

The article profiles several remarkable cases of people surviving extended periods at sea through ingenuity and luck, including a South Carolina sailor rescued after his boat capsized multiple times, a Mexican fisherman adrift for over a year in the Pacific, a Hawaiian fisherman found after 12 days, a Panamanian teenager who survived 26 days, and a Japanese man rescued from tsunami debris. These rare survival stories contrast sharply with the far higher number of maritime deaths, highlighting the exceptional circumstances and resourcefulness required to endure such ordeals.
