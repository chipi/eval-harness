On April 14-15, 2014, Boko Haram militants kidnapped 276 schoolgirls from Chibok, Nigeria; about 50 escaped, but over 200 remain missing one year later. President-elect Muhammadu Buhari marked the anniversary Tuesday, pledging aggressive action against the jihadist group that opposes Western education. The abductions sparked global outrage with the #BringBackOurGirls campaign, though Boko Haram continues to control parts of northeastern Nigeria and abduct civilians.
