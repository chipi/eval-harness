Mindy Kaling's brother Vijay Chokalingam claims he fraudulently applied to medical school as a Black applicant to expose what he views as unfair affirmative action advantages, receiving 11 interview invitations but only one acceptance to a mid-tier school. The article argues his stunt ignores both the real discrimination minorities face and the legitimate purpose of affirmative action in addressing severe underrepresentation of minority physicians, while noting the irony that his famous sister benefited from a diversity program early in her career.
