Amnesty International released a report documenting widespread attacks on women's rights activists in Afghanistan by the Taliban, warlords, and government officials, with little protection from authorities. The brutal murder of Farkhunda sparked unprecedented protests where Afghan women served as pallbearers at her funeral, defying tradition and signaling a growing resistance to violence against women. Despite facing threats and inadequate government support, activists like Selay Ghaffer continue their work, viewing the moment as the beginning of an uprising for women's rights.
