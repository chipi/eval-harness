A Columbia Journalism School review found Rolling Stone's article about an alleged gang rape at the University of Virginia was deeply flawed due to failures in reporting, editing and fact-checking. While UVA cannot sue as a government entity, Phi Kappa Psi fraternity members may have grounds for a defamation lawsuit if they can prove the false statements caused them individualized harm and financial damages. Such cases are rare but this situation presents stronger legal footing than typical defamation claims.
