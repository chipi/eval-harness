Indiana's new Religious Freedom Restoration Act has sparked national debate over whether such laws protect religious exercise or enable discrimination against gay people. Twenty states now have versions of religious liberty laws modeled on the 1993 federal RFRA, though legal victories under these statutes remain rare and have mostly benefited religious minorities rather than Christian denominations. The article details several cases arising from federal and state religious freedom laws, including disputes over Native American eagle feathers, a Brazilian church's sacramental tea, kosher prison meals, and a Sikh employee's ceremonial sword.
