The Pentagon reports coalition forces have retaken 25-30% of Iraqi territory from ISIS, primarily through conventional frontal assaults on the terror group's edges rather than strategic maneuver warfare. However, counterinsurgency experts warn that territorial gains alone don't indicate victory, as ISIS compensates for losses by launching spectacular attacks elsewhere to maintain its image of strength and recruitment appeal. The coalition's strategy lacks the sophisticated maneuver tactics and regional coordination that could more rapidly disrupt ISIS's command structure and supply lines.
