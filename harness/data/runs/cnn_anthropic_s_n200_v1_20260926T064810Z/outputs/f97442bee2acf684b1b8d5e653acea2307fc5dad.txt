Irish betting company Paddy Power deleted a tweet comparing Newcastle's losses to Liverpool with police violence against unarmed African-American men, calling it a joke while drawing widespread social media condemnation. Liverpool defeated Newcastle 2-0 at Anfield on Monday, with goals from Raheem Sterling and Joe Allen, moving the team to fifth place in the Premier League. The incident is the latest in a series of controversial publicity stunts by Paddy Power, which previously funded David Ginola's failed FIFA presidency bid and offered betting odds on Oscar Pistorius's trial.
