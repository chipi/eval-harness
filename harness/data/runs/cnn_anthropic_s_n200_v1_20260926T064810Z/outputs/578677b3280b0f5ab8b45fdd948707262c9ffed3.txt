Over 25,000 people contracted Ebola in the largest epidemic in history, with more than 10,000 deaths, though new cases have declined to 30 weekly as of April. While Liberia and Sierra Leone show significant progress with surplus treatment facilities, Guinea continues to struggle with unidentified cases and unsafe burials, raising concerns about unknown transmission chains. Survivors face permanent life changes, and the outbreak has exposed critical vulnerabilities in global public health systems.
