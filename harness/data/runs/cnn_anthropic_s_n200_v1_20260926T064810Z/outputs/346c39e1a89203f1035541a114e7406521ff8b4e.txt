UN Relief and Works Agency Commissioner-General Pierre Krähenbühl will visit Syria's Yarmouk Palestinian refugee camp Saturday to assess the humanitarian crisis affecting 18,000 Palestinians and Syrian civilians. The camp, controlled by ISIS and al Qaeda-affiliated groups, has been devastated by fighting since 2012, with its population plummeting from 160,000 to 18,000 and largely cut off from aid since 2013. The visit aims to explore ways to relieve widespread malnutrition and medical shortages in the camp located six miles from Damascus.
