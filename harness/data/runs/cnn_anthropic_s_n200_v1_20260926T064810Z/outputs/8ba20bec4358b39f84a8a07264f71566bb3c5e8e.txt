Chinese TV host Bi Fujian apologized after a video surfaced showing him cursing late Chairman Mao Zedong during a private dinner party, calling him an "old son of a b***h" while riffing on a revolutionary song. The incident sparked controversy in China, where disrespecting leaders is taboo, with critics demanding punishment while others defended him as simply joking in a private setting. State broadcaster CCTV said it would investigate the 56-year-old personality, known as "Grandpa Bi."
