Japan's Defense Minister Gen Nakatani told parliament that the country's Air Self-Defense Force has never encountered extraterrestrial UFOs, responding to a question from former wrestler-turned-politician Antonio Inoki. Nakatani said fighter jets have occasionally been scrambled for unidentified objects, but these have only been birds or earthly aircraft. Inoki, a WWE star and North Korea wrestling diplomat, claims to have personally witnessed a UFO but acknowledged uncertainty about alien existence.
