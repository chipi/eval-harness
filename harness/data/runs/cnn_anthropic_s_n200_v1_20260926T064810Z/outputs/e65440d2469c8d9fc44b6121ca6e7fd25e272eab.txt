The Department of Justice indicted FedEx on 15 counts related to transporting illegally prescribed pharmaceuticals, arguing the company knew it was delivering drugs from illegal internet pharmacies. FedEx argues it qualifies for a "common carrier" exemption under drug laws, which protects transportation companies from liability for contraband they unknowingly carry as part of normal business operations. The case raises questions about whether corporations should face criminal prosecution and whether holding delivery services liable for packages' contents makes legal or practical sense.
