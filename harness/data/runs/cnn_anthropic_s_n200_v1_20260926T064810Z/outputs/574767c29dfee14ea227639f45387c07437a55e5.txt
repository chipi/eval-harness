Nashville's district attorney banned prosecutors from offering female sterilization in plea deals, sparking outrage over a practice that has occurred four times in five years. The article argues sterilization as voluntary punishment likely remains constitutional since defendants can waive constitutional rights, and similar deals involving male defendants have gone largely uncontested. The author suggests public outcry stems from gender bias—castration of male sex offenders has been accepted practice, while sterilization of women is now deemed shocking.
