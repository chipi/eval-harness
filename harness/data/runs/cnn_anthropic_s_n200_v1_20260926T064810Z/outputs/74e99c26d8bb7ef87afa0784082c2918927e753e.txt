Italian Coast Guard rescued 978 migrants in the Mediterranean Sea off Libya on Friday, part of a surge in crossings this year. More than 10,000 migrants have arrived in Italy in the first three months of 2015, with at least 480 deaths reported—a dramatic increase from fewer than 50 deaths in the same period last year. Most migrants originate from West Africa, Somalia, and Syria, using Libya as a transit point, and often travel in overcrowded vessels operated by smugglers.
