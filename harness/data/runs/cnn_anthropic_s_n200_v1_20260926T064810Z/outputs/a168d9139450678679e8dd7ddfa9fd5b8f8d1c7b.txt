ISIS fighters stormed the Palestinian refugee camp of Yarmouk near Damascus in April, prompting Syrian government bombing and shelling that has devastated the already war-torn area. The population has plummeted from over 100,000 to roughly 18,000 residents, who face severe shortages of food, water and medicine while trapped between pro-government forces outside and anti-regime groups inside. International aid organizations have minimal access to deliver assistance to the besieged civilians caught in the grinding urban conflict.
