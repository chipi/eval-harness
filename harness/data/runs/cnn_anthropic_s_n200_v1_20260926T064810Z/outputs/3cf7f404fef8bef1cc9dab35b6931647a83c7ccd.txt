French investigators have completed evidence collection from the Germanwings Flight 9525 crash site in the Alps, which killed all 150 people aboard on March 24. The flight data and cockpit voice recorders have been recovered, and 150 DNA profiles have been isolated from over 2,000 samples collected. Authorities have determined that co-pilot Andreas Lubitz deliberately locked out the captain and engineered the plane's descent, and it has emerged he had concealed depression and medical leave notes indicating he was unfit for work.
