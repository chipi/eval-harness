Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" extends its reign atop the Billboard Hot 100 for a record-breaking 13th week, surpassing all other singles of the 2010s. The track also joins an elite group of only ten songs in chart history to hold the No. 1 spot for at least 13 weeks. Meanwhile, newcomer Natalie La Rose debuts in the top 10 with her single "Somebody."
