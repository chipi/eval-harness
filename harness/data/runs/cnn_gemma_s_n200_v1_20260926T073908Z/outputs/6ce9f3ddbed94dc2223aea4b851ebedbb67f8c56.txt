Five Chinese women's rights activists, including Wu Rongrong, have been detained for "creating disturbances" after planning International Women's Day protests against sexual harassment. Authorities have restricted access to legal counsel and medical care, with a decision on formal arrest expected by April 13th. The detentions are occurring as China prepares to celebrate the 20th anniversary of the Fourth World Conference on Women and consider its first anti-domestic violence law, highlighting a crackdown on independent activism.
