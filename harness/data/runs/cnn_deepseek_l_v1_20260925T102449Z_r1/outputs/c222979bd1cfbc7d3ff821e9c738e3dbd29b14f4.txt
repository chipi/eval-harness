Amnesty International reports governments increasingly used the death penalty in 2014 as a misguided response to terrorism and crime, citing mass sentencing in China and resumed executions in Pakistan. Although global executions dropped to at least 607, excluding secretive China, Egypt and Nigeria drove a spike in death sentences amid political instability. The organization maintains there is no evidence executions deter crime and urges remaining states to join the 140 that have abolished the practice.
