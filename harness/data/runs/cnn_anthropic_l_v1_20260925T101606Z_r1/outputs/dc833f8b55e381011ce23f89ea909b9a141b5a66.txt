Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill prompted Republican Gov. Asa Hutchinson to withhold his signature pending changes to the legislation, underscoring shifting attitudes on gay rights in conservative states. The retailer's stance, following its February announcement of pay increases for 500,000 workers, joined criticism from Apple, the NCAA, NASCAR and Angie's List, which paused an Indianapolis expansion over Indiana's similar law. The backlash has split Republican presidential hopefuls, with Jeb Bush softening initial support while Ted Cruz accused companies of following an "extreme left wing agenda."
