"Furious 7" opens Friday, more than a year after star Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40. Filmmakers declined to kill off his character Brian O'Connor, instead rewriting the script and using special effects, with Walker's brothers Cody and Caleb as body doubles. Co-star Vin Diesel, who named his newborn daughter after Walker, gave a tearful speech at Wednesday's Hollywood premiere.
