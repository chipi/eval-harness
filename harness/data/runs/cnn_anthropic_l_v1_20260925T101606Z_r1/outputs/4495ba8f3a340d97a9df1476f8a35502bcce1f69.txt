Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif returned to Tehran to a hero's welcome after reaching a framework nuclear deal with the US and other powers. Zarif was educated in the United States, holding degrees from San Francisco State University and the University of Denver, and both his children were born there. He has also faced a US investigation over the Alavi Foundation and, as a student, joined a takeover of Iran's consulate in San Francisco. Negotiators face a June 30 deadline to finalise details of the accord.
