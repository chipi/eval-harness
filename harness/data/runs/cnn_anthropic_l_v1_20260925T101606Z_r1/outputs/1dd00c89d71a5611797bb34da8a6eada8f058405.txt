Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's signing of a religious freedom law seen as enabling anti-LGBT discrimination is widely read as an effort to court the party's right wing ahead of 2016. Jeb Bush quickly defended the measure, while Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker have staked out similar ground, with 24 comparable bills introduced in 15 states this year. Commentators warn the issue will pull the entire Republican field rightward and revive culture-war politics, suggesting LGBT advocates are "winning" but have not yet won.
