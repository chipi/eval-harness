The Rev. Robert H. Schuller, the California televangelist who founded the "Hour of Power" broadcast and the Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at 88, his family said. He had been diagnosed with esophageal cancer in August 2013.

Schuller began preaching from the roof of a drive-in theater snack bar and built a ministry that reached millions with an upbeat "possibility thinking" message, though critics dismissed his theology as shallow. A family succession dispute and falling donations led Crystal Cathedral Ministries to file for bankruptcy in 2010 with debts topping $43 million.
