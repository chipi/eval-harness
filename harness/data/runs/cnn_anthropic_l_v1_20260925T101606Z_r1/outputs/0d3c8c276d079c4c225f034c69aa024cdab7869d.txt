Andrew Getty, 47, grandson of oil tycoon J. Paul Getty, was found dead at his Los Angeles home, with police saying he appears to have died of natural causes. The coroner's preliminary assessment found no foul play, and an autopsy is planned; Getty had "several health issues," and medication was recovered from his home. He had recently sought a restraining order against an ex-girlfriend, citing a serious medical condition diagnosed in 2013 that made rising blood pressure life-threatening.
