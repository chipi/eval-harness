Amnesty International says governments are invoking terrorism threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifting of its moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang.

Recorded executions fell 22% to at least 607 in 2014, excluding China, which Amnesty believes kills thousands annually. Death sentences rose 28% to 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria; Iran, Iraq and Saudi Arabia executed more people than the US, the only country in the Americas to carry out executions.
