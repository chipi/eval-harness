Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin said no videos have been used in the Germanwings crash investigation, after Bild and Paris Match reported a cell phone clip showing the plane's final seconds; a French Gendarmerie spokesman called the reports "completely wrong." Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered a "previous episode of severe depression," and says it has passed the records to prosecutors. Investigators believe Lubitz may have feared losing his pilot's licence over medical problems; Hollande said all 150 victims should be identified by DNA by week's end.
