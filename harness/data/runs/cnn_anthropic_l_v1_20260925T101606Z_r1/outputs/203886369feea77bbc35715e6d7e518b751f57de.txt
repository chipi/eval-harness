New research from the Anne Frank House indicates Anne Frank and her sister Margot died of typhus at Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than the previously accepted date of March 31, 1945. Researchers reviewed Red Cross, International Training Service and Bergen-Belsen Memorial archives along with survivor testimony, finding witnesses reported both sisters showing symptoms before February 7. Most typhus deaths occur about 12 days after symptoms appear; exact dates remain unknown, though Margot died first.
