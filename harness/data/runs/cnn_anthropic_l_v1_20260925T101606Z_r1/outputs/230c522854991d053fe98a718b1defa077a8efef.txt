A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck with a hammer in an attempted mercy killing and buried in a field, staggering to a nearby farm four days later. Now named Theia, the one-year-old was taken in by Moses Lake resident Sara Mellado and is being treated at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital for a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity. A fundraising page for her surgery has passed its $10,000 target, with surplus funds to go to other animals.
