Former Rep. Michele Bachmann compared President Obama's Iran nuclear negotiations to Andreas Lubitz, the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing Flight 9525, killing 150. In a March 31 Facebook post, the Minnesota Republican called Obama "a deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks" and referenced "the smoldering remains of American cities." Commenters on her page called the remarks despicable and a bid for relevance; Bachmann previously urged Obama to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
