Louisville basketball recruit Trey Moses asked Eastern High School freshman Ellie Meredith, who has Down syndrome, to be his prom date, surprising her in gym class with flowers and a Taylor Swift-themed poster. Photos of the "prom-posal," posted on Twitter by a teacher, went viral. Moses, who is headed to Ball State, plans to major in special education; Meredith's mother said the school's Best Buddies program has helped her daughter make friends.
