Mötley Crüe singer Vince Neil drew widespread mockery this week for a botched rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" before a Las Vegas Outlaws arena football game. The performance joins a long list of memorable anthem attempts, from Whitney Houston's acclaimed 1991 Super Bowl version to Roseanne Barr's 1990 rendition, which President George H.W. Bush called "disgraceful." Other notable versions include Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock performance and Michael Bolton's 2003 ALCS attempt, for which he needed written lyrics.
