Former Rep. Michele Bachmann compared President Obama's Iran nuclear deal to co-pilot Andreas Lubitz deliberately crashing Germanwings Flight 9525, saying Obama was "flying his entire nation into the rocks." The comparison drew widespread criticism on social media, with commenters calling it moronic, disrespectful and a desperate bid for relevance. Bachmann has long opposed Obama's Iran negotiations, previously telling the president to "bomb Iran" at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
