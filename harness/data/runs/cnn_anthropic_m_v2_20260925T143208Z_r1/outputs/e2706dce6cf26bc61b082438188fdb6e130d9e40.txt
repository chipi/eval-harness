Amnesty International's annual report says global executions fell 22% in 2014 to at least 607, but death sentences rose 28% to 2,466, partly due to mass sentencing in Egypt and Nigeria. The group warns governments are increasingly citing terrorism threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifted moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's crackdown in Xinjiang. Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and the U.S. remained top executioners, excluding China, whose death penalty data remains secret.
