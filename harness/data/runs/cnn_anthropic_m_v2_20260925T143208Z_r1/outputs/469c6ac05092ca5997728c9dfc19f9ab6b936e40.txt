Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied claims by German and French media of a cell phone video showing the final moments inside Germanwings Flight 9525, calling reports "completely wrong." Separately, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" to its flight school in 2009, raising questions about why he was allowed to fly. Investigators believe Lubitz's fear of losing his pilot's license due to medical issues may have motivated the crash, which killed all 150 aboard.
