Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil drew criticism for a botched national anthem performance at a Las Vegas Outlaws game this week, joining a long list of memorable renditions. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl performance set the gold standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 version was branded "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush. Jimi Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock rendition and Michael Bolton's 2003 flub, in which he forgot the lyrics, round out the anthem's most notable moments.
