Michele Bachmann compared President Obama to Andreas Lubitz, the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing a plane, likening Obama's Iran nuclear deal to a "deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks." The comment, posted on Facebook, drew sharp criticism from commenters calling it inappropriate and disrespectful. Bachmann has previously urged Obama to bomb Iranian nuclear facilities, including directly telling him so at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
