Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash after signing a "religious freedom" law widely seen as enabling discrimination against LGBT people, drawing criticism even as fellow Republicans like Jeb Bush defend him. The controversy highlights how deeply the GOP's 2016 presidential field is entangled with conservative positions on LGBT rights, with candidates including Ted Cruz, Bobby Jindal, Scott Walker and Mike Huckabee expressing opposition to same-sex marriage. The article argues no Republican candidate can avoid this issue in the primary, potentially pushing the party further right ahead of the general election.
