French prosecutors denied the existence of an onboard video from the Germanwings crash, contradicting reports by Bild and Paris Match that claimed to have seen footage of the final moments. Meanwhile, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had a history of severe depression, with investigators probing whether fear of losing his license motivated the deliberate crash. Recovery teams continue searching the French Alps site as authorities aim to identify all 150 victims via DNA analysis by week's end.
