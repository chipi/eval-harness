Former Rep. Michele Bachmann sparked backlash by comparing President Obama to the Germanwings co-pilot who deliberately crashed his plane, labeling the leader "deranged" over ongoing Iran nuclear negotiations. Critics condemned the Facebook post as moronic and disrespectful to the tragedy's victims, with some accusing Bachmann of seeking attention. The controversial remark underscores her long-standing opposition to the administration's diplomatic approach toward Tehran's nuclear program.
