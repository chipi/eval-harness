Mötley Crüe’s Vince Neil joins a roster of celebrities who faced public scrutiny for controversial renditions of the U.S. national anthem. High-profile performances by Whitney Houston, Roseanne Barr, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Bolton illustrate the fine line between patriotic acclaim and national disgrace. These instances underscore the intense pressure and potential backlash inherent in interpreting "The Star-Spangled Banner."
