Star basketball recruit Trey Moses asked high school freshman Ellie Meredith, who has Down syndrome, to prom, in a viral moment captured on social media. The gesture, part of a school program fostering inclusivity, moved Ellie’s parents, who praised the support she’s received. Trey, who plans to study special education, called Ellie "great" and "easy to talk to," while Ellie eagerly awaited dress shopping.
