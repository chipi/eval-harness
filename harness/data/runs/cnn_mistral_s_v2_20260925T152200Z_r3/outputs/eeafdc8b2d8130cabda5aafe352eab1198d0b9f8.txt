Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil faced backlash for a poorly received national anthem performance, joining a list of infamous renditions by Whitney Houston, Roseanne Barr, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Bolton. Houston set the gold standard in 1991, while Barr's 1990 performance drew presidential criticism. Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 version and Bolton's 2003 lyrics mix-up remain controversial.
