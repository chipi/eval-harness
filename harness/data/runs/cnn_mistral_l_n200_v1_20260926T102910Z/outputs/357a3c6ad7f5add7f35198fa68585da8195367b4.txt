**Saudi-led airstrikes in Yemen’s capital Sanaa have killed 600+ in two weeks, destroyed infrastructure, and cut power to 16 million, as Houthi rebels retain control. The Red Cross warns of a worsening humanitarian crisis, urging a ceasefire to deliver aid, while the UN Security Council remains deadlocked. Fighting rages in Aden, and al Qaeda exploits the chaos, expanding its foothold in the east.**
