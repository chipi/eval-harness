China’s former security chief Zhou Yongkang, once a top Communist Party leader, has been formally charged with bribery, abuse of power, and leaking state secrets—the highest-ranking official ever prosecuted for corruption. Zhou, who controlled China’s security apparatus, faces trial in Tianjin amid President Xi Jinping’s anti-graft campaign, though state secrets charges may limit public scrutiny. His downfall marks a historic crackdown on elite corruption, following the purge of rival Bo Xilai.
