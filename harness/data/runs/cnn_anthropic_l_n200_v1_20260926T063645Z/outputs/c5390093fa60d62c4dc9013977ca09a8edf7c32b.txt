Easter marks Christ's crucifixion and resurrection, celebrated Sunday by Pope Francis in a rainy St. Peter's Square and at Jerusalem's Church of the Holy Sepulcher. The holiday's date shifts annually — the first Sunday after the first full moon following the vernal equinox — and Orthodox Christians, who follow the Julian calendar, celebrate a week later, explaining the timing of the recent blood moon. The Easter bunny and eggs likely originated in Germany, with the first written mention in 1682.
