Singer Avril Lavigne says she was bedridden for five months with Lyme disease, telling People magazine she thought she was dying and could barely stand or breathe.

The Canadian performer, 30, believes a tick bit her last spring, and says doctors initially failed to diagnose her; she recovered at her Ontario home with help from her mother and husband Chad Kroeger.

Now "80 percent better," Lavigne is releasing a single this month in support of the 2015 Special Olympics.
