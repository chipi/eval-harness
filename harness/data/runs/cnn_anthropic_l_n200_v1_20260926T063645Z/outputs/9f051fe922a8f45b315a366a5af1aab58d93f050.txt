Bloggers and online journalists across Asia face rising legal and physical threats as governments curb Internet freedom through defamation suits, restrictive laws and imprisonment. Recent cases include teenage blogger Amos Yee's arrest in Singapore, the detention of five Malaysian Insider staff in Malaysia, and mass jailings in China and Vietnam. In Bangladesh, Washiqur Rahman Babu became the second blogger hacked to death in five weeks, following the killing of Avijit Roy, with such murders rarely resulting in convictions.
