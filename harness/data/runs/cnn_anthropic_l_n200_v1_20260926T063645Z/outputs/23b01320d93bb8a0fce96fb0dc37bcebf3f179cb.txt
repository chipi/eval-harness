The FBI has confirmed Malaysian bomb maker Zulkifli bin Hir, known as Marwan, was killed in a January raid in the southern Philippines and removed him from its Most Wanted Terrorists list. Marwan, indicted in California in 2007 and carrying a $5 million reward, was accused of supplying IEDs and training militants including Abu Sayyaf. The raid in Maguindanao killed 44 police commandos and 18 MILF fighters, shattering a three-year ceasefire and, analysts warn, jeopardising the Philippines' peace deal with the MILF.
