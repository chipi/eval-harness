An explosion at a paraxylene chemical plant in Zhangzhou, Fujian province, injured six people Monday night, sparking a large fire, Chinese state media reported. The blast occurred at an oil storage facility after a leak; five people were hurt by broken glass, and no toxic spill has been reported. Over 600 firefighters brought the blaze under control by Tuesday morning at the plant, which was relocated from Xiamen after 2007 protests and suffered a previous explosion in 2013.
