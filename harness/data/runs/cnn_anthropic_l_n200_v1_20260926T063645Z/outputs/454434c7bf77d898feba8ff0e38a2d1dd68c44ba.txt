Two University of Texas researchers say computer analysis shows the 1728 play "Double Falsehood" was written by William Shakespeare and collaborator John Fletcher, not publisher Lewis Theobald. The study, published in Psychological Science, built "psychological signatures" from 33 Shakespeare plays, nine by Fletcher and 12 by Theobald, finding only tiny traces of Theobald. Scholars welcomed the objective approach but doubted it would end the long-running authorship dispute.
