Marvel released a poster revealing Paul Bettany as the android Vision in "Avengers: Age of Ultron," and unveiled video of Charlie Cox's red suit ahead of the Netflix series "Daredevil."

Director Bryan Singer told Empire that Ben Hardy will play the winged mutant Angel in next year's "X-Men: Apocalypse," sharing a photo of the character.

A first look at Jamie Bell's CGI version of The Thing in the "Fantastic Four" reboot, due August 7, also emerged, along with a new image of Ryan Reynolds in "Deadpool."
