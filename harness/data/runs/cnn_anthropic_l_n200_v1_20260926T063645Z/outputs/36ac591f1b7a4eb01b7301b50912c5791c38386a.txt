Former Australia cricket captain and broadcaster Richie Benaud has died aged 84, having disclosed last year he was being treated for skin cancer. He played 64 Tests, never lost a series in 28 matches as captain, and was the first cricketer to reach 2,000 runs and 200 wickets. Tributes came from Australian PM Tony Abbott, who ordered flags flown at half-mast on the day of his funeral, as well as David Cameron, Shane Warne and Imran Khan.
