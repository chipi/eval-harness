Indiana Pacers forward Chris Copeland was stabbed in the abdomen and elbow early Wednesday near the 1OAK nightclub in Manhattan's Chelsea neighborhood; his companion Katrine Saltara and another woman were also slashed. A 22-year-old Brooklyn man was arrested with charges pending, and Copeland is in stable condition.

Atlanta Hawks players Pero Antic and Thabo Sefolosha, who were not involved in the stabbing, were arrested on obstruction and disorderly conduct charges after allegedly interfering with police at the scene. Both will sit out Wednesday's game against the Nets and say they will contest the charges.
