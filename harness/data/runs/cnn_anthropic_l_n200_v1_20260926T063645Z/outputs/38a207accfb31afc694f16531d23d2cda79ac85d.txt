Thai Smile, a subsidiary of Thai Airways, has unveiled an Airbus A320 painted with characters from Cartoon Network's "Adventure Time," including Finn, Jake and Princess Bubblegum. The theme extends inside the aircraft to overhead bins, head rests and air sickness bags. The jet, tied to a partnership with the new Cartoon Network Amazone water park near Pattaya, makes its first flight April 4 from Bangkok to Phuket.
