Anthony Ray Hinton, 58, walked free from a Birmingham, Alabama, jail Friday after nearly 30 years on death row for the 1985 murders of two fast-food managers. A judge dismissed the charges at the state's request after firearms experts found the revolver linked to him could not be matched to the crimes. His attorney, Bryan Stevenson of the Equal Justice Initiative, called the case a "textbook example of injustice" driven by race, poverty and inadequate legal representation.
