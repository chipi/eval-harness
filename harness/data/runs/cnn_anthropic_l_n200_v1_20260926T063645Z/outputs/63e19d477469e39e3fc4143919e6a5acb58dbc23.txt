Police in Malegaon, India, are requiring residents to submit "mugshots" of their cattle along with details of distinguishing features such as coloring, age and tail length, to build a database for enforcing Maharashtra's new beef ban. Over 100 owners have complied since the law made killing cows, bulls and bullocks a non-bailable offense punishable by up to five years in prison. Beef traders, who ended a month-long strike Wednesday, plan to challenge the ban in the state high court.
