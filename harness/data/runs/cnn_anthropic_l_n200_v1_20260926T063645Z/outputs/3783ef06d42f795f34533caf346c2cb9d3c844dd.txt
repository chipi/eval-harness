A federal grand jury has indicted real estate heir Robert Durst, 71, for unlawful possession of a firearm — a .38 revolver allegedly found in his New Orleans hotel room — a charge carrying up to 10 years in prison. Durst, held without bail in Louisiana, also faces state weapons and drug charges and awaits extradition to Los Angeles on a first-degree murder charge in the 2000 shooting of friend Susan Berman, for which he could face the death penalty. He denies involvement in Berman's death and his first wife's 1982 disappearance; his lawyer cites serious health problems and says Durst is "mildly autistic."
