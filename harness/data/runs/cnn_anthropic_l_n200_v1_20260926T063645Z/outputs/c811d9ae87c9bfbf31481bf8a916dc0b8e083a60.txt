Rolling Stone's retraction of its discredited UVA gang rape story leaves intact the undisputed core claim that the university has never expelled a student for sexual assault and faces ongoing scrutiny over its handling of cases, including a state-ordered review by O'Melveny & Myers and a federal Title IX investigation. Columbia Journalism School found the error could have been avoided had reporter Sabrina Rubin Erdely contacted "Jackie's" friends, who have since described a different account.

Survivors and advocates fear the retraction will deter victims from coming forward and has erased other students' testimony about a campus culture unsympathetic to victims, with some criticising the magazine for sensationalising an atypical case.
