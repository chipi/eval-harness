Kenyans took to social media with the hashtag #147notjustanumber to share stories and photos of the 147 people killed in last week's Al-Shabaab attack on Garissa University College, as authorities have yet to release a victims' list. The dead included 142 students, three security officers and two university security staff, making it Kenya's deadliest attack since the 1998 US Embassy bombing. Churches held Easter memorial services under armed guard; one of the slain gunmen was identified as the son of a government chief in Mandera.
