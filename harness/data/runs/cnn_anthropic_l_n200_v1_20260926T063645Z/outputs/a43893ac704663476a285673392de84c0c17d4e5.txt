Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" featuring Bruno Mars spent a 13th week atop the Billboard Hot 100, the longest run of the 2010s, surpassing Robin Thicke's "Blurred Lines." It becomes one of only 10 singles to lead the chart for at least 13 weeks since 1958, trailing the record 16 weeks held by Mariah Carey and Boyz II Men's "One Sweet Day." Newcomer Natalie La Rose entered the top 10 at No. 10 with her debut "Somebody," featuring Jeremih.
