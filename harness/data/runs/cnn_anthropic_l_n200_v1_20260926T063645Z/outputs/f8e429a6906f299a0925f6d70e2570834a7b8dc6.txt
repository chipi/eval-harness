The Obama administration says it is "not putting a timeframe on" an Iraqi-led operation to retake Mosul from ISIS, backing away from earlier US suggestions of an April or May assault involving up to 25,000 Iraqi troops. Officials cite progress against ISIS, including the recent recapture of Tikrit, but note Mosul is far larger — about a million people — and more strategically vital to the militants. Coalition airstrikes are already hitting ISIS positions near the city, though no US combat troops are planned and officials urged "patience."
