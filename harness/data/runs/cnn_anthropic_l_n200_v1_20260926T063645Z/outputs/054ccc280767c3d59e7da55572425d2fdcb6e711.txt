Former TSA employee Daniel Boykin, 33, was sentenced to six months in jail plus five and a half years of probation with GPS monitoring for secretly filming a female co-worker in a TSA-only restroom in Nashville. Investigators found more than 90 videos and 1,500 photos of the victim on his devices, and determined he had also entered her home multiple times. Boykin pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary and a computer act violation; the TSA said he worked in an administrative role and was fired last year.
