NFL Hall of Famer Deion Sanders publicly mocked his son on Twitter after the younger Sanders said he only eats "hood doughnuts." Sanders reminded his 912,000 followers that Deion Sanders Jr. has a million-dollar trust fund, a condo and his own clothing line, tweeting: "You're a Huxtable with a million $ trust fund. Stop the hood stuff!" Sanders Jr., a wide receiver at Southern Methodist University, retweeted his father's jabs.
