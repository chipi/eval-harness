Jia Jiang quit a six-figure job at 30 to pursue entrepreneurship, then launched a "100 Days of Rejection" blog project to confront his fear of being turned down after investors declined his tech start-up. A 2012 video of him asking a Krispy Kreme manager for Olympic-symbol doughnuts — she agreed — went viral, bringing talk show appearances, speaking invitations and job offers. His book "Rejection Proof" is released this week, arguing rejection can be turned into opportunity.
