Thieves broke into a safety deposit vault in London's Hatton Garden jewellery district over the Easter weekend, in a raid compared to the 1971 Baker Street bank tunnel burglary.

Former Scotland Yard commander Roy Ramm says such heists require months of planning, cloned vehicles, forensic clean-ups and usually inside information, and are motivated solely by money.

Detectives are examining possible insider involvement, CCTV, vehicle movements and forensic traces, while informers are tasked and stolen item details circulated to markets at home and abroad.
