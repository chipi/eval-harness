"Furious 7" opens Friday, more than a year after star Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40. Producers declined to kill off his character, rewriting the script and using special effects and Walker's brothers as body doubles to complete his scenes. Co-star Vin Diesel, who named his newborn daughter after Walker, gave a tearful speech at the Hollywood premiere; Walker's brother Cody said the film is "bittersweet" but that Walker "would be proud."
