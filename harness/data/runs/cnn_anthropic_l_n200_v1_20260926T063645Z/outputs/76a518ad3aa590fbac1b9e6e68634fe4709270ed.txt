French-language global broadcaster TV5Monde regained control of one of its 11 channels Thursday after what its director called an "extremely powerful cyberattack" knocked out its broadcasts, websites and social media accounts. ISIS logos appeared on the network's social media accounts, and the network said on a mobile site it had been "hacked by an Islamist group," though no group claimed responsibility. TV5Monde reaches 260 million homes worldwide and operates through a partnership involving the governments of France, Canada and Switzerland.
