Leaders from 35 Western Hemisphere nations gather in Panama City for the VII Summit of the Americas, where Obama and Cuba's Raul Castro are expected to meet for the first time since December's détente. Venezuela has overshadowed the thaw, with President Nicolas Maduro accusing Washington of plotting his overthrow and campaigning to repeal US sanctions on seven Venezuelan officials. Cuba's foreign minister has publicly backed Maduro, and it remains unclear how many other Latin American leaders will follow.
