"Furious 7" earned a record $146 million over its opening weekend, with many viewers saying the film's tribute to late star Paul Walker moved them to tears. Walker died in a November 2013 car crash before filming was complete; body doubles, CGI and his brothers were used to finish his scenes. Director James Wan said producers chose to retire Walker's character rather than kill him off, ending with a montage set to Wiz Khalifa and Charlie Puth's "See You Again."
