Louisville basketball recruit Trey Moses asked Ellie Meredith, an Eastern High School freshman with Down syndrome, to be his prom date, arriving at her P.E. class with flowers and a Taylor Swift-themed poster. Photos of the invitation, posted on Twitter by a teacher, went viral. Moses, who will play at Ball State next year, plans to major in special education; Meredith's mother said the school's Best Buddies program has eased her daughter's struggles making friends.
