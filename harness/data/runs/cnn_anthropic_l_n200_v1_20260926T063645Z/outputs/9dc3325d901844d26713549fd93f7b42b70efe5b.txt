Rapper Nelly was arrested on drug charges in Putnam County, Tennessee, after a state trooper stopped his tour bus on Interstate 40 for missing regulatory stickers and smelled marijuana. Troopers searched the bus and found methamphetamine, marijuana, drug paraphernalia, several handguns and 100 small Ziploc bags. Nelly, 40, whose real name is Cornell Haynes, was charged with felony drug possession and released on bond.
