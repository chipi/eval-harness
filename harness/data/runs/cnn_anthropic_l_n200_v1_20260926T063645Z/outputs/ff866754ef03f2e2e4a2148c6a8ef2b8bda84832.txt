The Justice Department will begin negotiations with Ferguson to overhaul its police force after finding a pattern of racial discrimination, including racist emails and predatory court fines. The city manager and police chief have resigned, and voters elected two black city council members, splitting the council evenly by race. Commentators say lasting reform depends on continued activism and on addressing governance flaws — weak elected officials and low-turnout off-year elections — common across St. Louis County and US suburbs.
