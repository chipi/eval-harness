Loretta Lynch's wait for a full Senate confirmation vote as US attorney general has stretched into a sixth month — longer than the previous eight nominees for the post combined. Supporters, including the NAACP and members of the Congressional Black Caucus, say race is a factor in the delay; Republicans deny it, citing a stalled human trafficking bill and Obama's immigration executive actions. Lynch's father, retired Baptist preacher Lorenzo Lynch of Durham, North Carolina, said he expected a fight, while the state's two Republican senators say they will not back her nomination.
