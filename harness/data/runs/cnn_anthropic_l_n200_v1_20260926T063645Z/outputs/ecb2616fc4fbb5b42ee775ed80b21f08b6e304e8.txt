Ryan Gosling's directorial debut "Lost River" opens in limited US and UK release April 10, a year after a Cannes premiere that drew boos and harsh reviews. Gosling said the film grew out of speculative footage he shot of Detroit's Brewster-Douglass Projects before their demolition, with the script written during filming. He described the city as "dangerous" and full of "impending threat," but said dereliction "doesn't define Detroit," pointing to signs of rebirth such as the Heidelberg Project.
