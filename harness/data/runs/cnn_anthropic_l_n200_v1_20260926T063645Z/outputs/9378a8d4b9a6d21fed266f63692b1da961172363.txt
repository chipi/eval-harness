An Iranian military observation aircraft flew within 50 yards of an armed U.S. Navy MH-60R helicopter over the Persian Gulf this month, U.S. officials told CNN. No shots were fired and the helicopter deliberately broke away, but officials worry the unsafe maneuver was ordered locally, raising fears Iran's commanders lack full control of forces in the region. Washington is weighing a formal protest; the incident coincided with nuclear talks in Switzerland and U.S. patrols watching for Iranian arms shipments to Yemen's Houthi rebels.
