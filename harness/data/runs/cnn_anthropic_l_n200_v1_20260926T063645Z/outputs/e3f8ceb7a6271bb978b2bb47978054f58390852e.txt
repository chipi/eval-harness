Democratic long-shot presidential hopefuls Martin O'Malley and Jim Webb shared a stage at the Polk County Democrats dinner in Des Moines on Friday, meeting each other for the first time that day. The former Maryland governor worked the room and delivered an applause-laden stump speech, while the former Virginia senator stayed near his seat and gave a subdued, biographical address focused on governance, economic and criminal justice reform. Both trail far behind Hillary Clinton, polling at 1-3% as she prepares to announce her campaign.
