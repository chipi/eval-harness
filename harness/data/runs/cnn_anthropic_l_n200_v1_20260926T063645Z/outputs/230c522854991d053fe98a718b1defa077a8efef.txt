A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck on the head with a hammer and buried, staggering to a nearby farm four days later. Named Theia, the one-year-old suffered a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity, and still needs surgery to breathe. Her foster carer's fundraising page has passed its $10,000 target, with extra money to be given to other animals.
