Commentary argues that large tax refunds — received by 8 in 10 US taxpayers, averaging nearly $3,000 — plus free online filing create a "happy ending" that masks the system's flaws, citing Nobel laureate Danny Kahneman's colonoscopy research showing pleasant endings shape overall memories. The author says the US income tax has become effectively a wage tax that penalises two-earner marriages, offers most filers no charitable deduction and lightly taxes the wealthy's capital income. Tax-prep firms benefit by upselling services such as audit protection to refund recipients.
