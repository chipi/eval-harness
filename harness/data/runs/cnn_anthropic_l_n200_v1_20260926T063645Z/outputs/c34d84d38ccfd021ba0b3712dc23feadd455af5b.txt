Richard Dysart, the Emmy-winning actor who played senior partner Leland McKenzie on NBC's "L.A. Law," died Sunday at his Santa Monica home after a long illness at age 86, his wife said. Dysart appeared in every episode of the 1986-94 series, winning the supporting actor Emmy in 1992 after four consecutive nominations. His other credits included "The Hospital," "Being There," "The Thing," "Pale Rider" and "Wall Street," plus stage work in "That Championship Season," for which he won a Drama Desk Award.
