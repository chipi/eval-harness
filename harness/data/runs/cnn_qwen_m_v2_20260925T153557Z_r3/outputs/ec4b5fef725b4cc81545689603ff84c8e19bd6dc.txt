Former Representative Michele Bachmann sparked outrage by comparing President Obama to the Germanwings co-pilot who deliberately crashed his plane, labeling him a "deranged pilot" steering the U.S. toward disaster via the Iran nuclear deal. Critics condemned the remark as morbid and disrespectful, with many accusing the former congresswoman of seeking attention through divisive rhetoric. The controversy arises as negotiations between world powers and Tehran approach a June 30 deadline for a comprehensive agreement.
