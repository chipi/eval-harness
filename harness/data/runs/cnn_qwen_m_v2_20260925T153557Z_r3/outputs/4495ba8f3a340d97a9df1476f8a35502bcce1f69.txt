Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, hailed as a hero for securing a nuclear breakthrough with the U.S., is an American-educated diplomat who spent more time negotiating with Secretary of State John Kerry than any other counterpart. Despite his polished public image and U.S. academic background, Zarif participated in early revolutionary consulate takeovers and faced a settled U.S. investigation into alleged sanctions evasion via the Alavi Foundation.
