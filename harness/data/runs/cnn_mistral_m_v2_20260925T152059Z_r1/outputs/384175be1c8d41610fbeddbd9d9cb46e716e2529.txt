Actor Paul Walker, star of the "Fast & Furious" franchise, died in a 2013 car crash mid-filming *Furious 7*; the film used CGI and body doubles to complete his role and includes a tribute to him. Fans and co-stars, including Vin Diesel, have honored his legacy, with tributes and emotional premieres marking the release. The film serves as a poignant farewell to Walker, celebrated for his kindness and humility.
