Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil faced backlash for a poorly received rendition of the U.S. national anthem at a Las Vegas football game, joining a list of infamous performances by Whitney Houston, Roseanne Barr, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Bolton. Houston set the gold standard in 1991, while Barr's 1990 version drew presidential criticism. Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock performance polarized audiences, and Bolton's 2003 rendition was mocked for its lyrical errors.
