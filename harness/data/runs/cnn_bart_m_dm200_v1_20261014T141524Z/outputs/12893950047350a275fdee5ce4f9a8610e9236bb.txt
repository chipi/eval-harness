Jamie Silvonek, 14, and Army Spc Caleb Barnes, 20, have been charged with homicide and criminal conspiracy in the murder of her mother, 54-year-old Cheryl Silvonk, who was stabbed to death March 15 . The girl's mother found out about their relationship in early March and ordered the couple to break up, according to court documents . The teen texted Barnes hours before the stabbing, 'She threatened to throw me out of the house. I want her gone,' the documents said . The 14th-grader's attorney said the girl tearfully told him she missed her mother and claimed she was coerced by her soldier boyfriend and afraid for her life .
