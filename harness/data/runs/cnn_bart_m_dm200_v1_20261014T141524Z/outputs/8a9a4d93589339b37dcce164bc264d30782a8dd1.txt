Prevention of Terrorism Act allows authorities to detain terrorists for two years without charge or trial . It also allows for unlimited extensions after those two years expire - something that will be decided by a government-appointed terrorism board, not a judge and jury . Bill effectively reintroduces the controversial prospect of indefinite detention without trial in Malaysia - something Prime Minister Najib Razak originally repealed in 2012 . Human Rights Watch described it as 'a giant step backwards for human rights'
