French free diver jumped into Dean's Blue Hole in the Bahamas in 2010 . Guillame Néry is seen at the edge before taking the plunge . The hole is 660ft (200 metres) deep, although he doesn't go to the bottom . In the video he seemingly reaches the bottom of the hole before swimming back to the surface . But the video is still incredibly impressive, as he holds his breath for minutes on end . Free divers are able to hold their breath for more than 20 minutes .
