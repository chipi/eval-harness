Francis Pusok, 30, had been seen riding a horse through the desert of San Bernardino on Thursday afternoon . He was caught by a helicopter and Taser-jolted with a Taser after he fell off the horse . He tried to get up but they stunned him again before kicking him in the groin . By the end of the two minutes, there were eight deputies surrounding and beating him . San Bernardino County sheriff, John McMahon, has ordered an inquiry into the incident .
