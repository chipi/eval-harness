Sir Alex Ferguson enjoyed a trophy-laden 26-and-a-half years in the Manchester United hotseat between 1986-2013 . The Scot was appointed as the Red Devils manager in November 1986 at the age of 45 before announcing his retirement at the end of the 2012-13 season aged 71 . Ferguson won 38 trophies - including 13 Premier League titles and two Champions League successes .
