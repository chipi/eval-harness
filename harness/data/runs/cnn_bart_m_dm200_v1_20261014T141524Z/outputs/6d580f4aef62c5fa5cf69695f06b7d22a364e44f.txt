Jessica McCarty was arrested on March 20 and faces three counts of first-degree murder for the deaths of Lacey McCarty, seven, Phillip McCarty and five-month-old Christopher Swist in Palm Bay, Florida, in a botched murder-suicide . McCarty allegedly strangled the infant and drowned and strangled her daughter, Palm Bay Police Chief Mark Renkens said . Police say none of the three children were shot or stabbed, but lacerations were found in the home . Christopher, the father of the youngest child, lost his fight for life in the days following the attack . He had nicknames for all of the children and coached the two oldest in little league baseball
