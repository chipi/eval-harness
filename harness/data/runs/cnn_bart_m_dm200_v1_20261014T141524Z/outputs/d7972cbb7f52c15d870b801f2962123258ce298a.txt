Lib Dem leader is touring the country in a bright yellow election battlebus fitted out with a radio studio . Aides say it is part of a deliberate strategy to get the Deputy Prime Minister out of London to meet people as they go about their daily lives . He has flown down a zipwire at a Go Ape park, tested the water at Bath Spa, planted flowers in Newtown, painted a picture at a nursery in Sheffield and watched racing wheels coming off the production in Chippenham . Food and drink has played a big part in the campaign, from pulling pints in a Bath pub, to sipping cocktails in the sun in Southampton .
