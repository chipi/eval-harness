Cui Hongfang, 73, died after she was knocked over by the 38-year-old Canadian woman . Her husband said the tourist was rushing down a steep set of steps on the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall when she bumped into his wife . Police interviewed the tourist, identified by Chinese media as Debra Fortin, and ruled the death an accident . Victim's family filed a civil lawsuit against the tourist and demanded she be prevented from leaving China .
