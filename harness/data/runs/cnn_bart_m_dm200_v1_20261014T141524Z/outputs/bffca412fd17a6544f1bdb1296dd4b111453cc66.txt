Aaron Hernandez was taken to the MCI Cedar Juntion prison in Walpole, Massachusetts on Wednesday after he was sentenced to life in prison without parole for the 2013 murder of Odin Lloyd . The 700-inmate facility is about a mile and a half away from Gillette Stadium, where he used to play for the New England Patriots . It's reported that he will eventually be moved to another facility in the coming weeks . The food at Cedar Junction was described by a Boston Globe reporter as 'marginally edible'
