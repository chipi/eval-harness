A recently released affidavit from November 2014 that was recently unsealed reveals translator Xiaoming Gao shared information with an alleged Chinese spy . In the affidavit, an FBI agent claims that Gao was paid 'thousands of dollars' to provide information to individuals she thought to be Chinese intelligence officers . Despite these claims however, she was never prosecuted by the government . Gao admitted to the meetings, and also admitted to reporting on her 'social contacts' in the United States . The US attorney's office declined to prosecute .
