Zhou Qunfei, 45, was born into extreme poverty in a village in rural China . She left home as a teenager to work in a glass-processing factory in Shenzhen, where she was put to work shaping glass for watches . She would send her salary home to her father, who was nearly blinded in an accident . Her company Lens Technology supplies Apple with its scratch-resistant window glass . Shares in the company were worth 78 Yuan each on March 31, making the 45-year-old worth £4.9 billion .
