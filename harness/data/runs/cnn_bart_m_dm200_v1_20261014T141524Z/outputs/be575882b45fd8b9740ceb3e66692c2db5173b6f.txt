Samantha Crossland stole £22,642 from Child's Play nursery in Dewsbury, West Yorkshire . The 30-year-old had been promoted to manager at the nursery by its owner Lynda Quigley . She had been told she faced a custodial sentence unless the money was paid back . Crossland was sentenced to a 12-month community order and 180 hours unpaid work . Her parents Michael and Susan won more than £1.2 million on the lottery in 2008 .
