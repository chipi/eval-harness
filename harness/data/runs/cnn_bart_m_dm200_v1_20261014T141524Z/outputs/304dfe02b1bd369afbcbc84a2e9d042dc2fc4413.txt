Hamza Parvez, 22, travelled to Syria in May 2014, telling his parent he was going to Germany . Along with his best friend Mohammed Nasser, a Roehampton University graduate, went to Syria and joined Islamic State . He is now living with a group of English speaking fighters, originally as far as Belgium, Finland and Australia . Like Hamza, all his friends appear to be struggling with life as a bachelor, with several taking to social media to voice their annoyance at being unable to escape their lonely life in Syria .
