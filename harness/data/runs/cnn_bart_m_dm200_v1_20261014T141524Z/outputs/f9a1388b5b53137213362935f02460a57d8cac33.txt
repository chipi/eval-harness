British physicist Stephen Hawking has sung Monty Python's Galaxy Song . Song is being released digitally and on vinyl for Record Store Day 2015 . Professor Hawking, 73, appeared on film alongside Professor Brian Cox . He can be heard in the video whizzing through space in his wheelchair . The song is a recreation of the song from 1983 film The Meaning of Life . It is a cover of the show's famous Galaxy Song, released digitally this week .
