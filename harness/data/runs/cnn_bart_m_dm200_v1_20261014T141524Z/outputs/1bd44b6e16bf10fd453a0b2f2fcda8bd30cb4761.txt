Hafeez Batthi, 33, and his wife, Khalida, 26, were racially abused by a middle-aged woman on a Sydney train on Wednesday afternoon . Stacey Eden, 23, overheard the racist rant and stood up for the Muslim couple . The Brisbane couple's mosque in Holland Park has offered Ms Eden and her partner an all-expenses-paid trip to visit the Gold Coast for a night and to see their mosque . Police are urging anyone who witnessed the attack, caught on camera, to come forward .
