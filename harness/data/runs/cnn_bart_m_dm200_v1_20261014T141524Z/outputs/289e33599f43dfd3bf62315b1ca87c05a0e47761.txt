More than 200 Yazidi prisoners have been set free in northern Iraq after nearly a year in Islamic State captivity . The 216 prisoners, made up of women, children and the elderly, are said to be in poor health and bearing signs of abuse and neglect . They were piled onto a minibus that drove them to peshmerga positions southwest of Kirkuk . The freed Yazidis were taken away by ambulances and buses to receive treatment and care .
