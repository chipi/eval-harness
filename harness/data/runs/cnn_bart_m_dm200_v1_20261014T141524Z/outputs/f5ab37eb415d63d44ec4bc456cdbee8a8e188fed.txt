Margaret Hesketh went in to the Royal Albert Edward Infirmary in Wigan on October 28 last year . She was admitted with an infected bed sore and suspected pneumonia . Ten days after she was admitted a scan had revealed she had cancer . Her family claim she was then put on a discredited Liverpool Care Pathway-style treatment for the dying . But they claim a post-mortem found no sign of cancer in the grandmother-of-six, and that she died of pneumonia while malnourished .
