Everton defender Leighton Baines is a big music lover . The Toffees defender is friends with rocker Miles Kane and Arctic Monkeys' front-man Alex Turner . Baines admits that he doesn't know any of One Direction's hit songs . He also reveals that Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars' Uptown Funk is regularly played in Everton's dressing room . The England international also gives his thoughts on Ed Sheeran and Meghan Trainor songs .
