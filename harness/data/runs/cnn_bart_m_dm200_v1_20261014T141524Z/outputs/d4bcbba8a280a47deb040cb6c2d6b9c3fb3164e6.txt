A six-month-old kitten was found lying on the ground covered in maggots and blood . It was taken in by a local vet clinic, My Vet Animal Hospital, who shared the story of aptly-named 'Seven' on social media . The cat had suffered a broken jaw, broken leg, broken hard palate and bruised lungs . Since then, the cat has won the hearts of many who have donated funds to help pay for his medical bills and surgeries . Doctors say Seven will be ready for his new home in about six weeks time .
