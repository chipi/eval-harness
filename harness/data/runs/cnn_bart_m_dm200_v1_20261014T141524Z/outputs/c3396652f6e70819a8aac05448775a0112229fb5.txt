Atletico Madrid face Real Madrid in the Champions League quarter-final first leg on Tuesday night . Diego Simeone has turned Atletico around since joining in December 2011 . The 44-year-old has signed a new four-year contract with the option of a fifth season . Atletico have not let Real win a single game in the last six matches between the two sides . The Argentinian manager has imposed his incredible work ethic on his players .
