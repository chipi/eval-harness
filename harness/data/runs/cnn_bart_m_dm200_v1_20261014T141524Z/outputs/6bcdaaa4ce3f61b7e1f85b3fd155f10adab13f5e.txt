Pep Guardiola remains Manchester City's first choice to replace Manuel Pellegrini . Bayern Munich boss has a year remaining on his contract but a quarter-final Champions League exit could help persuade his employers to let him go this summer . Guardiola is seen as a natural fit given his close relationship with two of City's executive pair Ferran Soriano and Txiki Begiristain, with whom he worked during a glittering stint at the Nou Camp . In 17 matches on the road in Europe, Guardiola's teams have won just four times, drawing eight and losing five .
