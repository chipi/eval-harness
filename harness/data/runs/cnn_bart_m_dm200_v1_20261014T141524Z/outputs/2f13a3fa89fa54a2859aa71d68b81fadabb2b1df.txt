Paris Saint-Germain ace Zlatan Ibrahimovic has been banned for four matches . Ibrahimovic lost his temper during PSG's 3-2 defeat at Bordeaux on March 15 . The Swede lambasted referee Lionel Jaffredo and one of his assistants . The 33-year-old has scored 102 goals for PSG since joining the club in July 2012 .
