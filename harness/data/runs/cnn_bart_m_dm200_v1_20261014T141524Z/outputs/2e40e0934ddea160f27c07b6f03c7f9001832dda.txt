Me-Bai was sold to provide rides for tourists in Thailand when she was three-and-a-half years old . She didn't see her mother, who also worked in the trade, for three years . The small elephant left the tourism trade 'because she was too young [and] began to lose weight and could not carry the tourists any longer' The emotional moment the pair were reunited was captured on camera in the Elephant Nature Park sanctuary in the north of the country .
