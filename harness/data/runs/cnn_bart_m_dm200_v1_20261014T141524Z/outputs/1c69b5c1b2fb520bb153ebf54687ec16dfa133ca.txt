Kim Davies bought Llanwenarth House, where Cecil Frances Alexander wrote the lyrics to All Things Bright and Beautiful, for £675,000 in 2008 . But he then made dozens of illegal alterations to the Tudor manor in rural Wales . He installed a whirlpool bath with shiny mosaic tiles, crystal chandeliers and spotlights in the ceiling . Davies, 60, initially denied five charges under the Planning (Listed Buildings and Conservation Areas) Act 1990 . But shortly before his trial was scheduled to start at Newport Crown Court he pleaded guilty to the charges .
