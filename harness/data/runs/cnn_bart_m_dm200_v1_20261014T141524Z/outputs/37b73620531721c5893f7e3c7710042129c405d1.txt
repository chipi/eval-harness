Ayyan Ali, 21, has been in Rawalpindi prison since March 14 after she was caught with over $500,000 in her carry-on bag at Benazir Bhutto International Airport . She was charged with attempting to illegally transport more than the legal $10,000 cash limit out of the country . Her lawyers say she had yet to check in and was going to hand the money over to her brother . They claim she is being targeted because of her celebrity status . Many in Pakistan's fashion world think she was manipulated into carrying the money .
