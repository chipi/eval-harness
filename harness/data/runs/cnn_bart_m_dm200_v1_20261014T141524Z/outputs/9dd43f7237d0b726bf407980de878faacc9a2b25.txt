Cortez Berry, 18, was serving time at the Burruss Correctional Training Center in Forsyth, Georgia, for his role in a 2011 carjacking and robbery . The disturbing image was uploaded to Facebook Friday using a cellphone that had been sneaked into the jail . It shows the 18-year-old Berry hunched down with a swollen eye in front of two shirtless young men, one of whom is holding the leash . The caption of the photo reads: 'When you disrespect the Nation, it brings nothing but pain and suffering' The Department of Corrections is carrying out an investigation into the incident and has the area of the center where the photo was taken under lockdown .
