Luis Suarez was clearly Liverpool's player of the season last season . The Uruguayan scored 31 goals in all competitions . Steven Gerrard, Daniel Sturridge, Raheem Sterling and Phillippe Coutinho also played well . Coutinho scored the winner in the FA Cup semi-final win at Ewood Park . The Brazilian had the most shots, most touches, the highest duel success, highest dribble completion and the most tackles .
