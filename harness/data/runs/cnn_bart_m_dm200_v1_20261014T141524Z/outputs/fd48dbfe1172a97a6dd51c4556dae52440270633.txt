Anne-Louise van den Nieuwenhof was diagnosed with a rare bone and soft tissue cancer, called Ewing’s sarcoma, aged just five . Her parents were told she only had three months to live and she was told she wouldn't even live to see her sixth birthday . She defied all the odds and moved to Canada where she worked as a nurse and began raising her own family . She died suddenly at a Canadian hospital six days after giving birth to her second child . Her husband Ryan is now tasked with raising their two-year-old son Emerson and newborn Elizabeth as a single parent .
