Ten former Atlanta public school educators, convicted earlier this month of racketeering in one of the largest U.S. test-cheating scandals, were told to consider plea deals or face significantly more time behind bars . Fulton County Judge Jerry Baxter urged the defendants during the sentencing hearing on Monday to accept deals with the prosecution in the trial over a widespread conspiracy to cheat on state tests . He also threatened prison sentences for them if they fail to reach those deals . 'I've got a fair sentence in my mind and it involves going to jail for everybody,' said Baxter . The sentencing will resume on Tuesday .
