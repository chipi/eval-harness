Hawaii scientists say a supervoid may account for an anomaly in the cosmic microwave background radiation (CMB) of the universe . The theory was proposed by astronomers seeking to understand what is causing this larger-than-expected cold area . In 2004 astronomers first found the 'Cold Spot' - a region 20 per cent less dense than elsewhere - and may now have an explanation for its formation . The research was carried out by the Institute for Astronomy at the University of Hawaii at Manoa . If confirmed, the supervoid would be the largest single structure ever found by humanity .
