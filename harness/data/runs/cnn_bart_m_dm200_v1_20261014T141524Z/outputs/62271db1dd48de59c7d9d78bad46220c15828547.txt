Frank Lampard spoke of his support for gay footballers in an interview on Alan Carr's Chatty Man . The Manchester City midfielder said he'd 'love' to see a gay footballer come out and be fully respected by the public . Lampard was joined on the show by Denise van Outen, Noel Fielding and Lethal Bizzle . The on-loan Manchester City star said that football is evolving for the better .
