Harold Ekeh, 18, is a straight A student at Elmont Memorial High School in Long Island, New York . He has been accepted by all 13 Ivy League schools, including Harvard, Yale, Harvard, Columbia, Cornell, Princeton, Princeton and NYU . The Nigerian-born Harold and his parents moved to the US when he was eight years ago . He says his parents' struggle to fit in inspired him to take every opportunity he was given . He plans to be a neurosurgeon to study Alzheimer's, a disease his grandmother suffers from .
