Glen Walford, 75, has been embroiled in a legal battle with Worcestershire County Council since her mother Mary moved to a care home in 2006 . The theatre director intended to retire to the £205,000 house in Stourport-on-Severn . Council agreed that the house should not be taken into account when means testing was carried out on her mother’s finances . But in 2013 they reversed their decision and said she would need to contribute towards bills . Council took the case to the Court of Appeal, arguing that although the property may be her home, it was not at the time her mother was taken into care . A judge ruled in favour of the council
