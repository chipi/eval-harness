Denise and Glen Higgs, from Braunton, Devon, had all but lost hope that they would ever be able to conceive . Glen was made infertile after his testicular cancer treatment at the age of 20 . Doctors successfully created eight embryos through IVF fertility treatment . The couple had a daughter Mazy, born three years ago and tried again using the same batch . Denise gave birth to twins Carter and Carson last week .
