Tottenham sold Gareth Bale to Real Madrid in 2013 for a world-record £85m transfer fee . Tottenham chairman Daniel Levy is planning a new transfer policy for the club . Levy reportedly told the Tottenham Hotspur Supporters' Trust that future transfer policy would involve the purchase of young players, priced between £10-15m with good potential for resale . The strategy would set Tottenham back a decade to a time when they traded stars such as Michael Carrick and Dimitar Berbatov to Manchester United .
