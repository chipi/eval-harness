More than 500,000 people knock on the doors of Abbey Road studios every year only to be told that it is closed to the public . The studios hosted the world's first live global broadcast - from none other than The Beatles - as well as playing a role in the invention of stereo by EMI engineer Alan Blumlein . With the aim of sharing some of this history with the public, Google has stepped in to showcase it to a range of ways . Inside Abbey Road is a new web app that takes users on an interactive, immersive and hugely detailed virtual tour of the studios .
