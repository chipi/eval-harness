FBI say they have recovered 26-pound gold bar - worth up to $500,000 - in South Florida . It is believed to be one of the gold bars stolen in $5 million armored truck robbery in North Carolina last month . Police have previously said they suspected it was an inside job because the 18-wheeler truck - owned by transport company TransValue - pulled over when one of its guards 'felt sick' The truck was carrying about $10 million worth of metal, so the thieves left half the load - mostly silver - behind . The gold totaled $4.8 million, but the truck was taking the precious from Florida to Massachusetts .
