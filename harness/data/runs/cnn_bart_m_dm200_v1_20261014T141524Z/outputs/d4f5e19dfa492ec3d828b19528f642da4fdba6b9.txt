Lucy Garrod, 27, developed an ulcer known as a corneal abrasion, caused by bacteria growing under her lens . She had worn contact lenses for 12 years with no problems, but woke up one day with an irritable right eye . She said it felt like it had swollen to the size of a golf ball and rushed to see her GP . She was given eye drops, but two days later her eye was so bloodshot she went back to hospital . Tests revealed she had a bacterial infection which grew under her right lens .
