Spanish police have killed a man in his 70s who barricaded himself into his home in Zaragoza . He shot at the officers who raided the home in the north-eastern city and shot him dead . The man yelled 'enter if you have the balls' before an intense firefight ensued . His daughter has been pictured lying next to her injured partner outside the house . The 50-year-old man is in a serious condition in hospital after being shot twice in the shoulder .
