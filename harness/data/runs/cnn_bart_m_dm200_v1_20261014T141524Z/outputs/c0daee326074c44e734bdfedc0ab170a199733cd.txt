Ancient Egyptians, Babylonians, Minoans and Mycenians were hit by a 'perfect storm' of disasters 3,200 years ago . Professor Eric Cline claims the collapse of these great civilisations was a domino-like effect . He claims a series of droughts, famines, climate change, earthquakes, invasions and internal rebellions happened in quick succession . Egypt's Old Kingdom collapsed because it failed to adapt to climate change .
