Tidal's $19.99-a-month app launched in New York on 30 March . Following launch the app jumped to 4th place on the iOS music chart but now ranks at 51 in the music category and 872 overall . While in the UK the situation is worse with the app falling from the overall app list entirely . By comparison, Pandora radio and Spotify occupy first and second spots respectively when the iOS and Google Play downloads are combined . It is not known why the app has dropped so significantly but the price may be too high for some users .
