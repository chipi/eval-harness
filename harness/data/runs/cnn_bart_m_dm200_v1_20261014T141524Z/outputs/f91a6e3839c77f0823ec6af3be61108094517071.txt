£135,000 was spent trapping and moving water voles from the Somerset Levels ahead of dredging work . Environment Agency says it has a legal duty under EU-backed regulations to protect the creatures as an endangered species . In January and February 2014 residents on the Levels were forced to abandon their homes after rivers burst their banks following years of insufficient dredging . More than 600 homes and 17,000 acres of farmland were inundated with quickly rising waters .
