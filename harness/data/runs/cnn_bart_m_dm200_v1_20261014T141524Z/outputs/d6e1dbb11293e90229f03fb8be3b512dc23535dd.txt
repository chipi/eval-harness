Ian Bell hit his 22nd Test match century to rescue England after a top-order collapse in Antigua . Bell and Joe Root put on 177 for the fourth wicket after England's top order collapsed . West Indies pace trio Jerome Taylor, Kemar Roach and Jason Holder bowled with discipline and skill . Jonathan Trott's return to the team ended with a three-ball duck when he fended his third ball to slip . Gary Ballance was the third to fall to a bad shot with just 34 on the board . England captain Alastair Cook was dismissed for 11 runs after making a mess of his batting order .
