Kathleen Blomberg, a resident at the heavily damaged 125 Second Ave., which is still under a full-vacate order, has been reunited with her two cats, Kitty Cordelia and Sebastian . The American Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals found the the traumatized animals under her bed and said it took about an hour to coax them out . Two people died and 22 were injured in last week's explosion, which authorities now believe was caused by an illegally tapped gas line .
