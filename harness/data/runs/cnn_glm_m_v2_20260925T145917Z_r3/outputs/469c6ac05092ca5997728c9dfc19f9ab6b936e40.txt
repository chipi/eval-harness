French authorities deny the existence of a cell phone video from the Germanwings crash site, contradicting reports by Bild and Paris Match claiming to have seen footage of the plane's final seconds. Meanwhile, Lufthansa confirmed it knew co-pilot Andreas Lubitz suffered severe depression in 2009 before allowing him to resume training. Investigators suspect Lubitz deliberately downed the plane, killing all 150 aboard, because he feared losing his pilot's license over his medical condition.
