Former TSA agent Daniel Boykin, 33, was sentenced to six months in jail and five and a half years of probation for secretly videotaping a female co-worker in a TSA-only bathroom and repeatedly entering her home to record videos and photos. Police found over 90 videos and 1,500 photos of the victim on his devices, and he pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary and violating the computer act. Boykin was fired last year when the investigation began.
