A year after Boko Haram abducted nearly 300 schoolgirls from Chibok, Nigeria, most remain missing, with fears they have been raped, enslaved or forced to convert. President-elect Muhammadu Buhari marked the anniversary by pledging to fight the militant group, which still controls parts of northeastern Nigeria. The hashtag #BringBackOurGirls continues to trend worldwide as families await news of their daughters.
