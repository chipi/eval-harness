Philadelphia woman Keonna Thomas was charged Thursday with attempting to provide material support to ISIS after allegedly purchasing tickets to travel overseas to join the group, citing social media posts dating to 2013. Two New York women, Noelle Velentzas and Asia Siddiqui, were also arrested this week and accused of plotting to build an explosive device for a domestic attack. Officials say more than 30 similar terror-related cases have been prosecuted in the past 18 months, 18 involving ISIS.
