Richard Dysart, the Emmy-winning actor best known for playing senior partner Leland McKenzie on NBC's "L.A. Law," has died at 86 at his Santa Monica home after a long illness. His prolific career spanned Broadway, film and television, including memorable roles in "The Hospital," "Being There," "Wall Street" and "Pale Rider." Dysart won an Emmy in 1992 for his "L.A. Law" role after four consecutive nominations, and was one of the few cast members to appear in every episode during the show's eight-season run.
