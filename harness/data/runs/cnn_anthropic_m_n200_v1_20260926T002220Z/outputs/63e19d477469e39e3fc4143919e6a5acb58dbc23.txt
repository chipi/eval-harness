Police in Malegaon, India, are requiring residents to photograph their cattle and submit "mugshots" with identifying details to help enforce a new state ban on beef sale and consumption. The Maharashtra law, prompted by a recent cow slaughter case, criminalizes killing bulls and bullocks and imposes up to five years in prison for beef consumption or sale, sparking a beef traders' strike and legal challenges. Other Indian states, including Haryana, have similarly tightened cow slaughter laws, with national officials considering a countrywide ban.
