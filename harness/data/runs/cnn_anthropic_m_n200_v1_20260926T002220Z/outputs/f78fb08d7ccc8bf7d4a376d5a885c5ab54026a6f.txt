Vijay Chokalingam, brother of actress Mindy Kaling, revealed he posed as a black applicant to gain admission to medical school, later planning a book about the experience. Critics note his scheme actually undermines his own argument, since despite claiming to be black he was accepted only by a lower-ranked school while facing racial profiling. The author argues affirmative action remains necessary to address stark underrepresentation of minorities in medicine, noting Kaling herself benefited from a diversity program early in her career.
