Critics argue Obama's political stake in securing an Iran nuclear legacy has weakened America's negotiating position, resulting in a framework deal that lets Iran keep significant nuclear infrastructure and enrichment capacity. The agreement, announced after a missed deadline, falls far short of Obama's earlier pledge to end Iran's nuclear program entirely, alarming regional allies like Saudi Arabia. Sanctions relief will begin quickly while Iran retains a "breakout" capability, prompting fears of regional instability and proliferation.
