Mark Ronson's "Uptown Funk!" featuring Bruno Mars logged its 13th week atop the Billboard Hot 100, making it the decade's longest-leading No. 1 and putting it among just 10 songs ever to hold the top spot for 13-plus weeks. Elsewhere in the top 10, Natalie La Rose's debut single "Somebody," featuring Jeremih, cracked the top 10 for the first time, while The Weeknd's "Earned It" rose to No. 5 and topped the R&B/Hip-Hop chart.
