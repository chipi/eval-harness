A bystander's video showing Officer Michael Slager shooting Walter Scott in the back as he fled contradicts the officer's self-defense claims and exposes alleged evidence tampering. The author argues that police body cameras could have prevented the shooting and would help address the broader crisis of racial bias and eroding trust between police and minority communities. While cameras are only an interim fix, tackling systemic bias requires tougher hiring standards, better training, and long-term social change.
