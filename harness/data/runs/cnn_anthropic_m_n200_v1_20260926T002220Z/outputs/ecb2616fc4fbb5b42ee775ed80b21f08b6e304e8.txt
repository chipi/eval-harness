Ryan Gosling's directorial debut "Lost River," booed at Cannes and panned by critics, is set in a decaying Detroit that Gosling says was the film's true inspiration. He began the project by filming the soon-to-be-demolished Brewster-Douglass Projects before realizing he was making a movie. Gosling says the film aims to show both Detroit's dereliction and its simultaneous resilience and rebirth.
