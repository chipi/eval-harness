Dzhokhar Tsarnaev was found guilty on all 30 counts, including 17 capital charges, in the Boston Marathon bombing trial after roughly 11½ hours of jury deliberation. The trial now moves to a penalty phase, where jurors will decide whether the 21-year-old should be sentenced to death. His defense, led by attorney Judy Clarke, had focused on portraying his late older brother as the plot's mastermind in hopes of sparing his life.
