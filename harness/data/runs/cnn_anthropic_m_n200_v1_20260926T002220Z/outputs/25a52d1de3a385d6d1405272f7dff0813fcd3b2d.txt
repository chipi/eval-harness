AMC's "Mad Men" begins its final season, capping eight years chronicling a 1960s ad agency and America's cultural upheaval. As the series picks up after the July 1969 moon landing, speculation swirls about major characters' fates amid the agency's turmoil following its merger and acquisition by McCann Erickson. Creator Matthew Weiner has kept plot details secret, but the show is expected to explore late-1960s events while resolving arcs for Don Draper, Peggy Olson, Roger Sterling and others.
