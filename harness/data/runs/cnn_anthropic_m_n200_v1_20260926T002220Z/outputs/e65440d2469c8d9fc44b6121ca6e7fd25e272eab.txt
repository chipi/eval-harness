The U.S. Department of Justice has indicted FedEx on 15 counts related to allegedly knowingly shipping controlled substances for illegal online pharmacies since 2004, despite warnings from the DEA, FDA and Congress. FedEx maintains its innocence, invoking the "common carrier" legal exception that shields companies from liability for contents they unknowingly transport. The case raises broader questions about corporate criminal liability and whether such prosecutions serve justice or primarily generate government revenue through settlements.
