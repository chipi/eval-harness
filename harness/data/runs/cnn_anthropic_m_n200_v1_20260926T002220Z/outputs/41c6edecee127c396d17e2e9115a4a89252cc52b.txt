The California Public Utilities Commission has fined Pacific Gas & Electric Co. a record $1.6 billion for unsafe operation of its gas transmission system, including the 2010 San Bruno pipeline explosion that killed eight people. Most of the penalty will fund pipeline safety improvements, with PG&E's CEO saying the company is working to become the safest energy provider in the U.S. PG&E has already paid over $500 million in victim claims and replaced more than 800 miles of pipe since the disaster.
