Amy Schumer hosted the 2015 MTV Movie Awards, delivering a hit opening segment and monologue, though a bow-and-arrow gag misfired literally. Highlights included Channing Tatum twerking for Jennifer Lopez, Rebel Wilson's slip-up introducing "Pitch Perfect 2," and Robert Downey Jr.'s Avengers castmates kneeling as he accepted the Generation Award. Weaker moments included Shailene Woodley's rambling speech and a recycled Zac Efron-Dave Franco gag.
