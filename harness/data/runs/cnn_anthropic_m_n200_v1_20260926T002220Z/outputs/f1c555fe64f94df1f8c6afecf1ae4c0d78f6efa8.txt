Anthony Ray Hinton, 58, was released Friday after nearly 30 years on Alabama's death row for two 1985 murders he says he didn't commit. Charges were dismissed after firearms experts determined the gun linked to the killings didn't match evidence, and prosecutors declined to retry the case. His attorney, Bryan Stevenson of the Equal Justice Initiative, cited race, poverty and prosecutorial indifference as factors in the "textbook example of injustice."
