Cuba attended the Summit of the Americas in Panama for the first time in over 50 years without U.S. objection, marking a diplomatic milestone amid ongoing negotiations to restore U.S.-Cuba relations. Cuban officials say full diplomatic ties depend on the U.S. removing Cuba from its terrorism sponsor list, a move the State Department has now recommended to the White House. Tensions also simmer over Cuba's alliance with Venezuela, which Washington recently deemed a national security concern, though officials have since softened that stance.
