Philadelphia police added attempted murder to charges against Nyia Parler, 41, accused of abandoning her quadriplegic, cerebral palsy-afflicted son in a wooded park for over four days while she visited her boyfriend in Maryland. The 21-year-old victim, found dehydrated and malnourished, cannot be communicated with about how he ended up there. Parler, in custody in Maryland, must complete unspecified treatment before extradition to Philadelphia.
