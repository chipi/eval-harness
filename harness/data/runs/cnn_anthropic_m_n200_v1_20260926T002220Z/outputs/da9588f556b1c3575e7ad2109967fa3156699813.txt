A Philadelphia mother has been charged after allegedly abandoning her quadriplegic, cerebral palsy-afflicted 21-year-old son in a wooded park for four days while she visited her boyfriend in Maryland. The man, found suffering from dehydration, malnutrition and other injuries, was hospitalized; police say relatives had been willing to care for him. The mother, arrested in Maryland, faces charges including aggravated assault, kidnapping and neglect, and awaits extradition to Philadelphia.
