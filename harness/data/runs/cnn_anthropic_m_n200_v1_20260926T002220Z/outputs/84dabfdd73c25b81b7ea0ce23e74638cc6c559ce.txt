Amnesty International's report "Their Lives on the Line" highlights escalating attacks on women's rights activists in Afghanistan by the Taliban, warlords, and government officials, noting perpetrators are rarely held accountable. The report follows the brutal mob killing of Farkhunda in Kabul, whose funeral saw women act as pallbearers for the first time, symbolizing defiance. Activists like Selay Ghaffer vow to continue the struggle despite personal threats, calling it the start of an uprising against violence and patriarchy.
