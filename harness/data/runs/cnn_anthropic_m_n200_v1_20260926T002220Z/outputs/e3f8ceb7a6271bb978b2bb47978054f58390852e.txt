Martin O'Malley and Jim Webb, both weighing 2016 presidential bids, appeared at a Democratic dinner in Des Moines, Iowa, showcasing contrasting styles—O'Malley eagerly worked the room while Webb remained reserved. Both trail Hillary Clinton badly in polls, with each registering around 1-3% support among Democrats. The two later pursued different paths, with O'Malley departing for New York while Webb continued campaigning in Iowa, including at a veterans' event where he seemed more comfortable.
