Nashville's district attorney banned prosecutors from offering female sterilization in plea deals, sparking outrage, though the practice—and male sterilization/castration in plea deals—has occurred nationwide for years. The Supreme Court has never fully addressed whether sterilization as punishment is constitutional, but voluntary waiver in plea deals likely makes it permissible. The author argues public outrage stems largely from gender bias, since sterilizing men has drawn far less scrutiny than sterilizing women.
