Hillary Clinton's presidential campaign announcement has made her an unfair proxy for assessing gender equality in America, a burden no male candidate faces. The author argues this is flawed since Clinton's extensive public record, not her gender, shapes voter perceptions, and presidential races are decided primarily by partisanship and economic conditions rather than sex. Regardless of the outcome, the piece contends, drawing broad conclusions about women's political viability from Clinton's individual campaign is misguided.
