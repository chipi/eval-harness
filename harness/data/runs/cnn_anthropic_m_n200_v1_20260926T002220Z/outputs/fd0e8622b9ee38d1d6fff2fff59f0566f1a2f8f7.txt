"Furious 7" is on track for a $135-138 million domestic opening weekend, the largest since fall 2013, bolstered by a record $60 million international take on Wednesday and Thursday. The film, the last to feature Paul Walker before his 2013 death, is playing in a franchise-record 4,003 domestic theaters and over 10,500 international screens. It stars Vin Diesel, Walker, Dwayne Johnson and Jason Statham.
