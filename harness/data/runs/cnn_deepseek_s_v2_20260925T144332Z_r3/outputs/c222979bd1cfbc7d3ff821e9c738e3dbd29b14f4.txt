On May 28, 2014, China publicly sentenced 55 people in Xinjiang, with three given death penalties, as part of a "Strike Hard" campaign against unrest. Amnesty International's 2014 global review shows a spike in death sentences and executions worldwide, with countries like Pakistan, Jordan, and Indonesia resuming or expanding capital punishment. Despite a drop in reported executions to 607, the report warns that governments are cynically using the death penalty to address crime and terrorism without evidence of deterrence.
