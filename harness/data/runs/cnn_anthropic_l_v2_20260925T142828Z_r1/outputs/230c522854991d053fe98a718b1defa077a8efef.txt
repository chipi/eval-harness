A stray dog in Washington State survived being struck by a car, apparently hit with a hammer in an attempted mercy killing and buried in a field, emerging four days later. Now named Theia, the one-year-old was taken in by Moses Lake resident Sara Mellado and is being treated at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital for a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity. An online fundraiser for her surgery has passed its $10,000 goal, with surplus funds to go to other animals.
