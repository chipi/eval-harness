Andrew Getty, 47, grandson of oil tycoon J. Paul Getty, was found dead at his Los Angeles home; police say he appears to have died of natural causes. The coroner's preliminary assessment found no foul play, and an autopsy is planned; Getty had "several health issues" and medication was recovered from his home. He had recently sought a restraining order against an ex-girlfriend, citing a 2013 diagnosis that made blood pressure spikes life-threatening.
