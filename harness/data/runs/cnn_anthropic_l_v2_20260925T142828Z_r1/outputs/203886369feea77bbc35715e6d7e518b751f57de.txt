New research from the Anne Frank House indicates Anne Frank and her sister Margot died of typhus at Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than the previously accepted date of March 31, 1945. Researchers reviewed Red Cross and Bergen-Belsen Memorial archives and survivor testimony, finding both sisters showed symptoms before February 7 and that typhus deaths typically occur around 12 days after onset. Exact dates of death remain unknown, though Margot died before Anne.
