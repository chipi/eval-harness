Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill helped prompt Gov. Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign it unless the language was changed. The retailer's stance, following its February pay rise for 500,000 workers, is being cited as a bellwether of shifting public opinion, with former Minnesota Gov. Tim Pawlenty saying it "foreshadows" where the GOP must move. Business opposition also forced Indiana Gov. Mike Pence to pledge a fix to his state's law, while Ted Cruz defended it and Hillary Clinton and Jeb Bush criticised the measures.
