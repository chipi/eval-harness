Televangelist Rev. Robert H. Schuller, founder of the "Hour of Power" broadcast and California's Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at 88 after being diagnosed with esophageal cancer in 2013.

Schuller rose from preaching atop a drive-in theater snack bar to reaching millions with an upbeat "possibility thinking" gospel that critics called weak theology but brilliant marketing.

His later years were marred by a family succession dispute and falling donations; Crystal Cathedral Ministries filed for bankruptcy in 2010 with debts over $43 million, and the cathedral is now Catholic-owned.
