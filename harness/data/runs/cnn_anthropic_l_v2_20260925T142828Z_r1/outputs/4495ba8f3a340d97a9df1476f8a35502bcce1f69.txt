Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif has emerged as the key counterpart to US Secretary of State John Kerry in nuclear talks, returning to Tehran to cheering crowds after this week's framework deal in Switzerland. Less widely known details include his US education at San Francisco State and the University of Denver, his American-born children, conflicting official birth dates placing him at 54 to 56, a US probe into his alleged role with the Alavi Foundation, and his participation in a takeover of Iran's San Francisco consulate during the revolution. Zarif and Kerry face a June 30 deadline to finalise the agreement's details.
