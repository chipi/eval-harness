Louisville, Kentucky, basketball recruit Trey Moses invited Ellie Meredith, an Eastern High School freshman with Down syndrome, to prom during her gym class, bearing flowers and a Taylor Swift-themed poster. He texted her parents for approval the night before; photos posted by a teacher went viral.

Moses, who will play at Ball State next year and plans to major in special education, said Meredith is "great... she listens and she's easy to talk to." Her mother credited the school's Best Buddies program with easing her daughter's struggles with friendship.
