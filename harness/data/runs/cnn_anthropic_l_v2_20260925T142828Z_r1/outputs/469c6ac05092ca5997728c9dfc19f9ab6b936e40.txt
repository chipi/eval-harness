Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin says no video footage has been used in the Germanwings crash investigation, after Bild and Paris Match reported a cell phone clip showing the plane's final seconds; French officials called the reports "completely wrong," though Bild's editor stands by them. Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered a "previous episode of severe depression," and is sharing his training and medical records with prosecutors. Investigators believe Lubitz may have feared losing his pilot's license over medical problems; Hollande said all 150 victims should be identified by DNA by week's end.
