Former Rep. Michele Bachmann compared President Obama's Iran nuclear negotiations to the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing Flight 9525, calling Obama "a deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks."

The Facebook comment, posted March 31, drew heavy criticism from commenters who called it inappropriate and a bid for relevance.

Bachmann has previously urged Obama to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities, saying he laughed at her at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
