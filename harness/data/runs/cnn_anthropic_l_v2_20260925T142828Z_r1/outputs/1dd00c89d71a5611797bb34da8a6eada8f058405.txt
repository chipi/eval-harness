Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's signing of a religious freedom law seen as enabling discrimination against gays and lesbians is read as a bid to shore up conservative credentials ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run. The ACLU says 24 similar bills have been introduced in 15 states this year, and commentators warn the controversy will push the entire Republican field rightward on LGBT rights. Despite polls showing growing national support for same-sex marriage, the state-level trend suggests progressives are "winning" but have not yet won.
