Mötley Crüe singer Vince Neil drew criticism this week for a poorly received rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas Outlaws Arena Football League game. His performance joins a long list of memorable anthem attempts, including Whitney Houston's acclaimed 1991 Super Bowl version and Roseanne Barr's 1990 rendition, which was booed and called "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush. Others include Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 ALCS performance, for which he needed written lyrics.
