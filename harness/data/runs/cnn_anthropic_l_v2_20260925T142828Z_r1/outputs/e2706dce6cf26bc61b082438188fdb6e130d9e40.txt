Amnesty International's annual report says governments worldwide are invoking terrorism threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's end to its moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang. Recorded executions fell about 22% to at least 607 in 2014, excluding China, which Amnesty believes kills thousands annually. Death sentences rose 28% to at least 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria; the US was the only country in the Americas to execute, putting 35 people to death.
