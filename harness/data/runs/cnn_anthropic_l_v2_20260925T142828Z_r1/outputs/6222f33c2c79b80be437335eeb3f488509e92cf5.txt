Tropical storm Maysak, known locally as Chedeng, is approaching the Philippines with steady winds of more than 70 mph and gusts up to 90 mph, prompting warnings of flash floods and landslides. Days earlier it was a super typhoon with 150 mph winds. Authorities have banned some outdoor water activities, turned away tourists in coastal areas and ordered precautionary evacuations ahead of an expected Sunday morning landfall in Isabela province.
