Actor Paul Walker, star of the "Fast and Furious" franchise, is being remembered by fans and colleagues on the release of "Furious 7", the film he was filming when he died in a car crash in 2013. The film's release has sparked tributes, including a tearful speech from co-star Vin Diesel, who said the movie is "more than a movie". Walker is being remembered for his kindness and humility, with many fans sharing their own tributes on social media.
