French prosecutor Brice Robin says he is not aware of any video footage from Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by two magazines that they obtained a cell phone video showing the plane's final moments. The magazines described the video as showing passengers screaming and metallic banging, but its existence has not been confirmed by authorities. The investigation into the crash, which killed 150 people, continues to focus on co-pilot Andreas Lubitz's mental state and motives.
