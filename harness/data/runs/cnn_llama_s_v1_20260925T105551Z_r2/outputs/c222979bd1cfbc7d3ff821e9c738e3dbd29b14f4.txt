Amnesty International's annual review found 607 executions were recorded worldwide in 2014, down from 778 in 2013, but noted a troubling trend of governments using the death penalty to tackle crime and terrorism. Several countries, including Pakistan, Iran, and Iraq, resumed or expanded executions, while others, like Jordan and Indonesia, reinstated or increased their use of the death penalty. Despite this, the global trend is towards abolition, with 140 states now abolitionist in law or practice.
