Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil drew criticism for his rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" before a Las Vegas Outlaws game, joining a long line of memorable national anthem performances. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl performance set the gold standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 rendition was called "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush. Other notable versions include Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 performance, during which he forgot the words.
