Amnesty International's annual review found governments increasingly used the death penalty in 2014 as a misguided response to terrorism and crime, citing Pakistan, Jordan, Indonesia, China, Iran and Iraq. Death sentences rose sharply, largely due to Egypt and Nigeria, though overall executions fell from 778 to 607 worldwide, excluding China. Amnesty maintains there is no evidence capital punishment deters crime and urges abolition, noting 140 countries have now ended the practice.
