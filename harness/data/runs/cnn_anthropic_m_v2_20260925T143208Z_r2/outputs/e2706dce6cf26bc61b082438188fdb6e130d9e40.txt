Amnesty International's annual report found global executions fell 22% in 2014 to at least 607, even as death sentences rose 28% to 2,466, partly due to mass sentencing in Egypt and Nigeria. The group warned that governments, including Pakistan and China, are increasingly invoking terrorism threats to justify executions. Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and the U.S. remained the leading executioners outside China, whose death penalty data remains a state secret.
