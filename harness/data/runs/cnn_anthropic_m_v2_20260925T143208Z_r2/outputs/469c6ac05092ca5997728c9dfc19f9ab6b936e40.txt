Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied reports of a cell phone video showing the final moments inside Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by Bild and Paris Match magazines. Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" during 2009 flight training, raising questions about why he was allowed to fly. Investigators believe Lubitz's fear of losing his pilot's license due to medical issues was the likely motive for deliberately crashing the plane, killing all 150 aboard.
