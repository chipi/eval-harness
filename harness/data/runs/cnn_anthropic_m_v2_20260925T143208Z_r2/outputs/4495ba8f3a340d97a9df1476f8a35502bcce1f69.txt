Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, key architect of the nuclear deal framework with the U.S., is often described as "jovial" but less-known facts complicate his profile. These include his education in the U.S. during the Iranian Revolution, involvement in a 1979 takeover of the Iranian consulate in San Francisco, alleged ties to a foundation investigated for laundering money, and even uncertainty over his exact birth year. Despite this, he has now spent more time negotiating with Secretary of State John Kerry than any other foreign minister, with more talks ahead before the June 30 deadline.
