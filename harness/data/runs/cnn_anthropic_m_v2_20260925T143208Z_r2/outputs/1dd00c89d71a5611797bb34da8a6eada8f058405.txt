Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash after signing a religious freedom law critics say allows discrimination against gays and lesbians, drawing scrutiny to his and other 2016 GOP contenders' stances on LGBT rights. The controversy highlights how the issue could force Republican candidates further right during the primary, even as national polling shows growing support for same-sex marriage. The author warns that despite progressive gains, opposition to LGBT rights remains a potent force in conservative politics.
