Amnesty International's annual review reveals governments worldwide increasingly used the death penalty in 2014 as a misguided response to terrorism and crime, with Pakistan lifting its moratorium and Egypt and Nigeria imposing hundreds of sentences. Despite a global drop in recorded executions from 778 to 607, death sentences spiked by over 500, driven by political instability. Amnesty urges leaders to abandon capital punishment, noting 140 states are now abolitionist.
