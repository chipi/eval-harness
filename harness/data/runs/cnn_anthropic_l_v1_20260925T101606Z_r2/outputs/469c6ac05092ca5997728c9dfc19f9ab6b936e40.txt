Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin said no video footage has been used in the Germanwings Flight 9525 investigation, contradicting reports by Bild and Paris Match that a cell phone video captured the crash's final seconds; French officials called the reports "completely wrong." Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had told its flight school in 2009 of a "previous episode of severe depression," though the airline had earlier said he was 100% fit to fly. President Francois Hollande said all 150 victims should be identified by DNA analysis by week's end.
