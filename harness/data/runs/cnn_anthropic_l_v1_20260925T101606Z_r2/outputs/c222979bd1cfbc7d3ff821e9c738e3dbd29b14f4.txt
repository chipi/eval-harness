Amnesty International's annual review finds governments increasingly using capital punishment as a political response to crime and terrorism. Pakistan lifted its moratorium on civilian executions after the Peshawar school attack, executing more than 60 people, while Jordan and Indonesia resumed executions citing crime and public safety. Death sentences rose by over 500 in 2014, driven by Egypt and Nigeria, though recorded executions fell from 778 to 607, excluding China, where figures remain a state secret.
