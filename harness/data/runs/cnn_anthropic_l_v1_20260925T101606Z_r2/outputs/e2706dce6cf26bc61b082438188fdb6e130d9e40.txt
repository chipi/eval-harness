Amnesty International says governments worldwide are invoking terrorism threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifting of a moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang. Executions fell 22% to at least 607 in 2014, excluding China, where thousands are believed killed annually. However, death sentences rose 28% to at least 2,466, driven by mass sentencing in Egypt and Nigeria; the US, with 35 executions, was the only country in the Americas to carry them out.
