Walmart's public opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill helped prompt Republican Gov. Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign it unless the language was changed. The retailer's stance, following its February decision to raise pay for 500,000 workers, marks it as a bellwether on divisive political issues, with figures such as former Minnesota Gov. Tim Pawlenty saying the GOP should take note. The backlash also spread to Indiana, where businesses including Angie's List and NASCAR criticised a similar law, while Republican presidential hopefuls split, with Jeb Bush softening his support and Ted Cruz attacking companies for following an "extreme left wing agenda."
