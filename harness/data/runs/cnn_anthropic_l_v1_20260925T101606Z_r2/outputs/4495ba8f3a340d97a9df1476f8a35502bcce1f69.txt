Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, celebrated at home after outlining a nuclear deal with world powers, has spent more time with US Secretary of State John Kerry than any other foreign minister. Zarif was educated in the United States, earning degrees at San Francisco State University and the University of Denver, and both his children were born there. Lesser-known details include his participation in a student takeover of Iran's San Francisco consulate after the revolution, a US probe into his alleged role at the Alavi Foundation, and conflicting official records of his birth year.
