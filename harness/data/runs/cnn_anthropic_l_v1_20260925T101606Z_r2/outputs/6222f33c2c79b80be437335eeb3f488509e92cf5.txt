Tropical storm Maysak, known locally as Chedeng, is bearing down on the Philippines with winds over 70 mph and gusts to 90 mph, prompting warnings of flash floods and landslides. Officials have ordered precautionary evacuations and banned swimming, surfing, diving and boating in some areas, while hotels in coastal Aurora were told to turn away arriving tourists. The storm, which was a super typhoon days ago with 150 mph winds, is expected to make landfall Sunday morning in Isabela province and clear the country by Monday.
