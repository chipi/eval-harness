Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's signing of a religious freedom law seen as enabling anti-LGBT discrimination is being read as an appeal to conservative primary voters ahead of a possible 2016 White House bid. Jeb Bush quickly defended the measure, while Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker have staked out similar positions. The ACLU says 24 such bills have been introduced in 15 states this year, suggesting LGBT rights will feature prominently in the Republican primary despite growing national support for same-sex marriage.
