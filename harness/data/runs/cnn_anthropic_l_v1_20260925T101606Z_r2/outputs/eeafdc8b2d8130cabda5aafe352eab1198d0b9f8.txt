Mötley Crüe singer Vince Neil drew criticism this week for his rendition of "The Star-Spangled Banner" at a Las Vegas Outlaws Arena Football League game. He joins a long list of celebrities whose anthem performances made headlines, including Roseanne Barr, booed in 1990 and called "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush, and Michael Bolton, who forgot the lyrics at the 2003 ALCS. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl XXV performance and Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock version remain the most celebrated.
