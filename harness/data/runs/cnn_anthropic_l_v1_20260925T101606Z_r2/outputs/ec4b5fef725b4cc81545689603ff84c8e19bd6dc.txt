Former Rep. Michele Bachmann compared President Obama's Iran nuclear negotiations to Andreas Lubitz, the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing Flight 9525, calling Obama "a deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks" in a March 31 Facebook post. Commenters on her page called the remarks despicable and accused her of seeking attention. Bachmann has previously urged Obama to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities, saying he laughed at her at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
