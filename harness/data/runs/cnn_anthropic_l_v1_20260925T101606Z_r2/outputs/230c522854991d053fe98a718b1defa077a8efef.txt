A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck with a hammer and buried, staggering to a nearby farm four days later. Named Theia, the one-year-old suffered a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity, and is being treated at Washington State University's Veterinary Teaching Hospital. Foster owner Sara Mellado's fundraising appeal for her surgery has passed its $10,000 target.
