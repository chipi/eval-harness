The Rev. Robert H. Schuller, the California televangelist who founded the "Hour of Power" broadcast and the Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at 88, his family said. He had been diagnosed with esophageal cancer in 2013.

Schuller began preaching from a drive-in theater snack bar roof and built a following with an upbeat "possibility thinking" message that critics dismissed as weak theology but effective marketing. A family succession dispute preceded a sharp fall in donations, and Crystal Cathedral Ministries filed for bankruptcy in 2010 with debts topping $43 million; the building is now owned by the Catholic Church.
