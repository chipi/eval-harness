New research from the Anne Frank House indicates Anne and her sister Margot died at Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than the previously accepted date of March 31, 1945. Researchers re-examined Red Cross, International Training Service and Bergen-Belsen Memorial archives along with survivor testimony, finding witnesses reported both sisters showing typhus symptoms before February 7. Most typhus deaths occur about 12 days after symptoms appear. Exact dates remain unknown; Margot died first.
