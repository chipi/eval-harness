Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, hailed as a hero for securing a nuclear breakthrough with the U.S., holds dual U.S. citizenship and was educated in America. Despite his polished diplomatic image, Zarif participated in the 1979 revolution and faced a U.S. investigation into an alleged sanctions-busting charity that settled without naming him. He now races against a June 30 deadline to finalize details of the nuclear agreement with Secretary of State John Kerry.
