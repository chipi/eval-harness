Mötley Crüe’s Vince Neil joins a roster of celebrities who faced public scrutiny for controversial renditions of the U.S. national anthem. Historical precedents range from Whitney Houston’s acclaimed Super Bowl performance to Roseanne Barr’s presidential rebuke and Jimi Hendrix’s psychedelic Woodstock interpretation. Michael Bolton also drew criticism for relying on crib notes during a 2003 baseball game, underscoring the high stakes of performing the patriotic standard.
