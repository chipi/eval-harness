Vince Neil's recent rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas arena football game joined a long list of infamous celebrity performances of "The Star-Spangled Banner," ranging from Whitney Houston's celebrated 1991 Super Bowl version to Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 performance that drew presidential criticism. The article notes that performing the anthem is a high-stakes endeavor for famous musicians, with past controversial versions including Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 performance marred by apparent lyrical errors.
