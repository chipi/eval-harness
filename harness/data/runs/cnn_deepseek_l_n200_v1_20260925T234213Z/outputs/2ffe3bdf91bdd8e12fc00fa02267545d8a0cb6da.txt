The Pentagon released a map showing coalition forces have retaken 25-30% of Iraqi territory previously held by ISIS, with gains concentrated in central and northern Iraq. A counterinsurgency expert cautioned that territorial gains against ISIS are strategically significant but that the group is adapting by staging high-profile attacks elsewhere to distract from losses. He also noted the coalition’s current strategy relies on frontal pushes rather than more creative maneuver warfare to break up ISIS-held areas.
