Guatemala faces epidemic levels of gender-based violence, ranking third globally in killings of women, fueled by a legacy of civil war impunity, deeply rooted machismo, and a weak justice system where 98% of cases never reach court. Survivors like Adelma Cifuentes are finding justice and empowerment through theater and community programs, while the government has introduced specialized femicide courts. Despite these steps, international NGOs and advocates stress that urgent action is still needed to protect millions of women trapped in cycles of abuse.
