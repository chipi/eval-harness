French prosecutor Brice Robin says he is not aware of any video footage from inside Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by two magazines that they saw a cellphone video of the plane's final moments. The crash, which killed 150 people, is under investigation, with authorities exploring co-pilot Andreas Lubitz's mental state and possible motives. Lubitz had battled depression and was deemed fit to fly by Lufthansa, the parent company of Germanwings.
