New research reveals Anne Frank and her sister Margot likely died of typhus in Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than previously believed, with witnesses placing their illnesses before February 1945. The Anne Frank House study, based on archival records and survivor testimonies, contradicts the long-held March 1945 death date. The sisters endured horrific conditions, including lice-infested tents and a deadly typhus outbreak, before perishing.
