Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil faced backlash for a poorly received rendition of the U.S. national anthem, joining a list of infamous performances by Whitney Houston, Roseanne Barr, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Bolton. Houston set the gold standard in 1991, while Barr's 1990 version was widely panned. Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock performance and Bolton's 2003 lyrics mix-up also sparked controversy.
