Amnesty International's annual review finds governments increasingly used the death penalty in 2014 as a misguided response to terrorism and crime, citing Pakistan's post-Peshawar moratorium lift, mass sentencing in Xinjiang, and executions in Iran, Iraq, Jordan and Indonesia. Death sentences spiked by over 500 from the previous year, largely due to Egypt and Nigeria, though recorded executions fell from 778 to 607. Amnesty urges abolition, noting 140 states are now abolitionist in law or practice.
