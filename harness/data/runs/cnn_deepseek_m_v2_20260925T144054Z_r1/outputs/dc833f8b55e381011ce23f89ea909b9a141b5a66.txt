Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill helped push Gov. Asa Hutchinson to seek changes to the legislation, underscoring the retailer's emergence as a bellwether on gay rights and other divisive issues. The backlash from major companies against similar laws in Indiana and Arkansas has forced Republicans, including likely presidential candidates, to balance religious liberty with nondiscrimination. Some in the GOP, like Tim Pawlenty, say the party must follow Walmart's lead and adapt to shifting public opinion.
