Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil joins a long list of famous singers who have delivered memorable—and often controversial—renditions of the U.S. national anthem. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl performance set the modern standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 version drew boos and a presidential rebuke. Jimi Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 flub are also among the most notable.
