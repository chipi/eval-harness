Paul Walker died in a car crash in November 2013 while on break from filming "Furious 7," which releases Friday and offers fans a chance to remember the actor. Producers rewrote the script and used special effects, along with Walker's brothers as body doubles, to complete his scenes and pay tribute to him in the film's ending. Co-star Vin Diesel, who named his newborn daughter after Walker, gave an emotional speech at the premiere, and fans have held tributes including a memorial attended by about 5,000 people.
