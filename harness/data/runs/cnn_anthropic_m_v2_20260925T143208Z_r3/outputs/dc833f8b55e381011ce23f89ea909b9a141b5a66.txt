Walmart joined Apple, the NCAA and other major companies in opposing religious freedom laws in Indiana and Arkansas, arguing they would enable discrimination against LGBT customers. The backlash prompted Arkansas Gov. Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign the bill without changes, while Indiana Gov. Mike Pence pledged to fix his state's similar law. The controversy has exposed divisions within the Republican Party, with some like Tim Pawlenty urging the GOP to embrace nondiscrimination policies while others like Ted Cruz defended the laws and criticized businesses for opposing them.
