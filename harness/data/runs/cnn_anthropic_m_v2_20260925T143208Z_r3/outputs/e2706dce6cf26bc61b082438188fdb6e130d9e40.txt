Amnesty International's annual report found global executions fell 22% in 2014 to at least 607, even as death sentences rose 28% to 2,466, partly due to mass sentencing in Egypt and Nigeria. The group warned that governments increasingly invoke terrorism threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifted moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang. Iran, Iraq, Saudi Arabia and the US remained the top executioners outside China, whose death penalty data remains secret.
