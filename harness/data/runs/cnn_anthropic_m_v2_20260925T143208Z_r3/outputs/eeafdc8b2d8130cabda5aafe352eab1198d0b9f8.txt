Vince Neil's rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas football game drew widespread criticism, joining a long list of memorable performances. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl performance set the modern standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 version was called "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush. Other notable renditions include Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 take and Michael Bolton's 2003 performance, during which he forgot the lyrics.
