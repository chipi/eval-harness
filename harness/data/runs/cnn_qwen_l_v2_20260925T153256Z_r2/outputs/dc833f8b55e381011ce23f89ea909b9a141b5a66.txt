Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill—echoed by major corporations and political figures—forced GOP Gov. Asa Hutchinson to demand revisions, highlighting growing business support for LGBTQ nondiscrimination. The backlash in Arkansas and Indiana underscores a shift in corporate America, with leaders arguing equality is good for business, even as some Republicans like Ted Cruz defend the laws. Former Walmart board member Hillary Clinton and ex-GOP governor Tim Pawlenty urged the Republican Party to adapt to evolving public opinion on gay rights and worker protections.
