On May 28, 2014, China staged a public mass sentencing in Xinjiang, convicting 55 people and sentencing three to death as part of its "Strike Hard" campaign. Amnesty International's 2014 review shows a global rise in death sentences, with governments in Pakistan, Iran, Iraq, Jordan, and Indonesia using executions as a political or anti-crime tool. Despite this, global executions fell to 607 recorded cases, with 140 countries now abolitionist in law or practice.
