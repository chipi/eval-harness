French prosecutor Brice Robin said no video from the Germanwings Flight 9525 crash has been used in the investigation, despite reports from Bild and Paris Match of a recovered cell phone video. French Gendarmerie spokesman Lt. Col. Jean-Marc Menichini called the reports "completely wrong," noting cell phones found at the site haven't been analyzed. Meanwhile, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had prior severe depression, and investigators are probing whether he feared losing his pilot's license due to medical issues.
