Indiana Governor Mike Pence signed a religious freedom law sparking backlash over LGBT discrimination, a move aimed at solidifying his conservative credentials for a potential 2016 presidential run. The controversy highlights a broader Republican primary battle where candidates are forced to take hardline stances on LGBT rights, potentially dragging the party further right. Despite national polls showing growing support for same-sex marriage, state-level "religious freedom" bills and GOP candidates' positions suggest the cultural war over LGBT rights will be a central issue in 2016.
