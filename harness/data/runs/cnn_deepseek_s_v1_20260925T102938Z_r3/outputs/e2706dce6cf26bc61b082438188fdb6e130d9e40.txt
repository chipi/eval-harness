Amnesty International reports that governments are using terrorism threats to justify executions, with a 28% spike in death sentences in 2014 despite a 22% drop in executions overall. Pakistan, China, and Egypt are cited for using capital punishment in security campaigns, while the U.S. remains one of the top executing nations. The report notes positive long-term trends toward abolition, but criticizes unfair trials and the irreversible nature of the penalty.
