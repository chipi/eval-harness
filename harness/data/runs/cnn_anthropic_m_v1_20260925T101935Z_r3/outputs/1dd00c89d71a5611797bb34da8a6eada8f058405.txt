Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash for signing a religious freedom law critics say enables discrimination against LGBT people, exposing divisions within the GOP ahead of 2016. While the move has drawn condemnation, several Republican presidential hopefuls, including Jeb Bush, Ted Cruz and Scott Walker, have echoed similarly conservative stances on same-sex marriage, suggesting the issue will dominate the primary race. The article argues that despite growing national acceptance of LGBT rights, the controversy shows conservative opposition remains a potent political force.
