Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash while on break from filming "Furious 7," prompting producers to rewrite scenes and use his brothers as body doubles to complete the film. The movie, released Friday, includes a tribute to Walker as his character is "retired," with co-star Vin Diesel calling it "more than a movie" at Wednesday's premiere. Fans and family have held memorials and tributes, with Walker's brother Cody saying he believes Walker "would be proud" of the finished film.
