Vince Neil of Mötley Crüe drew criticism for his rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas Outlaws game this week, joining a long history of memorable performances. Notable examples include Whitney Houston's celebrated 1991 Super Bowl performance, Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 rendition, Jimi Hendrix's controversial Woodstock version, and Michael Bolton's 2003 flub in which he forgot the lyrics.
