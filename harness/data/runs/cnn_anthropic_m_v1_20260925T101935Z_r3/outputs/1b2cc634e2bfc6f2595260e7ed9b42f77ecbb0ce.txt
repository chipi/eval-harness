Kentucky basketball star Trey Moses asked Ellie Meredith, a high school freshman with Down syndrome, to be his prom date, and photos of the "prom-posal" went viral. Moses, headed to play college ball at Ball State next year, said he wanted to take Meredith instead of his girlfriend because "she's great." Both families and school officials praised the gesture, which stemmed partly from Eastern High's Best Buddies program.
