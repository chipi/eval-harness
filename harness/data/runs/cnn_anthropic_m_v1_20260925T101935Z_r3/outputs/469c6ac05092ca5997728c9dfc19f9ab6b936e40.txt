Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied claims by Bild and Paris Match of a cell phone video showing the crash's final moments, calling reports "completely wrong." Meanwhile, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" to the airline's flight school in 2009. Investigators believe Lubitz's fear of losing his pilot's license due to medical issues was a primary motive for deliberately crashing the plane.
