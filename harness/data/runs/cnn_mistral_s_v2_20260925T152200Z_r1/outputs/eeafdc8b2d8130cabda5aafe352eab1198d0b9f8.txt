Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil faced backlash for a poorly received rendition of the U.S. national anthem, joining a list of infamous performances including Whitney Houston's iconic 1991 Super Bowl rendition, Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 version, Jimi Hendrix's psychedelic 1969 take, and Michael Bolton's 2003 lyrics mix-up. The anthem remains a high-stakes test for performers, often elevating or embarrassing them.
