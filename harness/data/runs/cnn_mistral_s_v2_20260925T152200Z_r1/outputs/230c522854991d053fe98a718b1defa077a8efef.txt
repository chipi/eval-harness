A stray dog in Washington State, named Theia, survived being hit by a car, struck with a hammer, and buried before digging herself out and staggering to a farm for help. Despite severe injuries, including a dislocated jaw and caved-in sinus, she is recovering with the aid of a fundraising campaign that has already exceeded $10,000. Theia's owner, Sara Mellado, vows to ensure her full recovery and placement in a safe home, with excess funds going to help other animals.
