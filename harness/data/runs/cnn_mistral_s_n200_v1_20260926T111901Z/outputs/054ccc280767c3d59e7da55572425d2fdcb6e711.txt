Former TSA agent Daniel Boykin, 33, was sentenced to six months in jail for secretly recording a female co-worker in a TSA-only restroom and burglarizing her home, where he stole videos, photos, and data. Prosecutors found over 90 videos and 1,500 images of the victim on his devices. Boykin pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary, and computer violations, receiving an additional five and a half years of probation with GPS monitoring.
