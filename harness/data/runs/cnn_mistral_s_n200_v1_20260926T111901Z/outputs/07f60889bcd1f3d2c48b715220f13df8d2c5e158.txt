Cape Verde, a small island nation with a rich musical heritage, is leveraging its cultural strengths to boost its economy, focusing on festivals like the Kriol Jazz Festival and the Atlantic Music Expo to promote tourism and global exposure. With limited natural resources, the country is banking on music, handicrafts, and arts to drive growth, though challenges remain in expanding the creative sector's economic impact. Officials and industry leaders hope international collaborations will elevate Cape Verdean artists and attract tourism, positioning music as a key economic driver.
