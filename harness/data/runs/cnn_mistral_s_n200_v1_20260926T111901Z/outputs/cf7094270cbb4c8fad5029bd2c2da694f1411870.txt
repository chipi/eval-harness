**Three sailors survived weeks adrift at sea by rationing supplies, catching fish, and relying on luck, while a tsunami survivor clung to debris for days before rescue. Louis Jordan, Jose Alvarenga, and Adrian Vasquez endured extreme hardship, with Alvarenga surviving 14 months at sea. A 67-year-old fisherman, Ron Ingraham, was rescued after 12 days lost in the Pacific, and a Japanese man survived a tsunami by holding onto a rooftop before being spotted at sea.**
