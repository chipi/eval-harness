Emmy-winning actor Richard Dysart, best known for his role as Leland McKenzie in *L.A. Law*, has died at 86. The veteran actor, who also starred in *That Championship Season* and *The Hospital*, passed away at home in Santa Monica after a long illness. Dysart, a frequent Emmy nominee, appeared in every episode of *L.A. Law* and played notable roles in films like *Pale Rider* and *Being There*.
