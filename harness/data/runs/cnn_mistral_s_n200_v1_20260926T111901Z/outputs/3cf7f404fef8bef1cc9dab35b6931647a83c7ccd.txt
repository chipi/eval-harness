Investigators have completed evidence collection at the Germanwings Flight 9525 crash site in the French Alps, with security firms now guarding the area. The co-pilot, Andreas Lubitz, is suspected of deliberately crashing the plane, with flight data showing he accelerated the descent. Authorities have recovered both black boxes and 150 DNA profiles from the wreckage, while Lubitz's history of depression and concealed medical leave is under scrutiny.
