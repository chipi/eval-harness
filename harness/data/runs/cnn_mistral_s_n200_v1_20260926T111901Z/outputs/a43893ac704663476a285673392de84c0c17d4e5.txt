Mark Ronson and Bruno Mars' "Uptown Funk!" extends its Hot 100 reign to 13 weeks, becoming the longest-running No. 1 of the 2010s and one of just 10 songs to top the chart for 13+ weeks. Natalie La Rose debuts in the top 10 with "Somebody," featuring Jeremih, while Maroon 5's "Sugar" and The Weeknd's "Earned It" climb.
