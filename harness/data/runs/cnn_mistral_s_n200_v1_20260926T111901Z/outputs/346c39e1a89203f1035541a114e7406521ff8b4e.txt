UNRWA Commissioner-General Pierre Krähenbühl will visit Syria's Yarmouk Palestinian refugee camp to assess the humanitarian crisis, where 18,000 civilians, including 3,500 children, face severe threats from armed groups and government airstrikes. The camp, under ISIS and Al-Nusra control, has seen widespread malnutrition and aid shortages since 2013. Krähenbühl will meet officials and displaced residents, vowing continued support.
