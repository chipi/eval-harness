A hooded black angel sculpture, created by artist Phillip Hyman, has become a symbol of the Black Lives Matter movement after appearing near the site where Walter Scott was fatally shot by police in North Charleston. Hyman crafted the piece from reclaimed wood and black paint, saying it represents mourning and the indignity of Scott’s death. The artwork has been prominently displayed at protests, with Hyman allowing its use as a statement on police violence and racial injustice.
