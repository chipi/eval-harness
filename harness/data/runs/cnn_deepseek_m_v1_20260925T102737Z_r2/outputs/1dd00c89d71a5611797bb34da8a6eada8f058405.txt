Indiana Gov. Mike Pence signed a religious freedom law that critics say permits discrimination against gays and lesbians, shoring up his conservative credentials ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run. The move, mirrored by similar bills in 15 states, forces the entire Republican field rightward on LGBT rights despite growing national acceptance of same-sex marriage. Conservatives may be winning the primary battle, but the backlash suggests progressives have not yet lost the war.
