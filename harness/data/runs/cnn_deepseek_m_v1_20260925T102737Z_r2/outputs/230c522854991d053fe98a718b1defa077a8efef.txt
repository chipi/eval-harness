A stray dog in Washington State survived being hit by a car, struck in the head with a hammer and buried in a field, then staggered to a farm four days later. The one-year-old bully breed mix, now named Theia, is being treated at Washington State University's veterinary hospital for a dislocated jaw, leg injuries and a caved-in sinus cavity. A fundraising page for her care has surpassed its $10,000 target.
