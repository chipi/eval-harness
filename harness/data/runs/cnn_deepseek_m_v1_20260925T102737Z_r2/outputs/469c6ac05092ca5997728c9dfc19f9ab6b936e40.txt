Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denies any video from Germanwings Flight 9525 is part of the investigation, after Bild and Paris Match claimed to have cell phone footage of the final seconds. French gendarmerie spokesman Lt. Col. Jean-Marc Menichini called the reports "completely wrong," saying recovered cell phones have not yet been analyzed. Lufthansa separately confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" in 2009.
