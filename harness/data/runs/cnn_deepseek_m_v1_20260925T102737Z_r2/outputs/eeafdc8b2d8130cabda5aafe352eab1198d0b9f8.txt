Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil delivered a widely panned rendition of the national anthem before a Las Vegas Outlaws arena football game, joining a long list of famous anthem performances ranging from Whitney Houston's celebrated 1991 Super Bowl version to Roseanne Barr's booed 1990 outing. Other notable renditions include Jimi Hendrix's controversial 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 performance, during which he flubbed the lyrics.
