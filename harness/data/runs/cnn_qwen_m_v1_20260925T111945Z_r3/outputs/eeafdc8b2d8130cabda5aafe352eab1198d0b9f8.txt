Mötley Crüe’s Vince Neil joined a roster of celebrities who faced public scrutiny for controversial renditions of the U.S. national anthem. High-profile performances by Whitney Houston, Roseanne Barr, Jimi Hendrix, and Michael Bolton illustrate the polarizing nature of singing "The Star-Spangled Banner." These instances highlight how such acts can swiftly elevate artists to hero status or condemn them as national disgraces.
