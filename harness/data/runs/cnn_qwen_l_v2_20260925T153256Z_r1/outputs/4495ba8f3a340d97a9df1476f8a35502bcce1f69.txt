Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, a key figure in nuclear talks with the U.S., is known for his diplomatic finesse and rare public gestures like wishing Jews a happy Rosh Hashanah. Less known are his U.S. education, disputed birth year, past revolutionary activities, and an unproven link to a sanctioned Iranian foundation. He now works closely with U.S. Secretary of State John Kerry to finalize a deal by June 30.
