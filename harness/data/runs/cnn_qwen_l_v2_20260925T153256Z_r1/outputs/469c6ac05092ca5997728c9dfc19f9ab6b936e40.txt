French prosecutor Brice Robin and aviation authorities deny the existence of any onboard video from Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by Bild and Paris Match that a cell phone video recovered at the crash site shows the plane's final moments. Investigators state no phones have yet been analyzed, while Lufthansa confirms co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had a history of depression and suicidal tendencies, with evidence suggesting he may have crashed the plane fearing he’d lose his pilot’s license.
