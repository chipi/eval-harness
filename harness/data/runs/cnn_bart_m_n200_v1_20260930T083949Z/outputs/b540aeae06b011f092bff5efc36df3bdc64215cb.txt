There were plenty of highlights and misfires from the 2015 MTV Movie Awards . Amy Schumer's monologue was as reliably hilarious and inventive as her Comedy Central show . Channing Tatum twerking for Jennifer Lopez was an awkward moment . Tinashe and Charli XCX attacked a pop song with the abandon of a rock 'n' roll tidal wave . Kevin Hart brought his kids onstage to accept his Golden Popcorn .
