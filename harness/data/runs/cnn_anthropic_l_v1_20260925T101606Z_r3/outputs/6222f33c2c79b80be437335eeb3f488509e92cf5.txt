Tropical storm Maysak — known locally as Chedeng — was bearing down on the Philippines on Saturday, with officials warning of flash floods and landslides. Once a super typhoon with 150 mph winds, it had weakened to sustained winds of over 70 mph and gusts up to 90 mph. Authorities ordered precautionary evacuations and banned swimming, surfing, diving and boating in some areas, with landfall expected Sunday morning in Isabela province.
