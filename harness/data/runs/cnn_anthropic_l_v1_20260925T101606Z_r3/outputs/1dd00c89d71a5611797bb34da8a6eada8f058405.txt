Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's decision to sign a religious freedom law seen as enabling discrimination against gays and lesbians is being read as a bid to court ultraconservative Republican voters ahead of 2016, with Jeb Bush quickly defending him. Similar measures have been introduced in 15 states, making LGBT rights a likely litmus test in the GOP primary, with Ted Cruz, Bobby Jindal, Mike Huckabee and Scott Walker all staking out opposition to same-sex marriage. The commentary warns that progressives' recent gains on LGBT issues shouldn't be mistaken for victory, as conservative backlash could mobilise voters in 2016.
