New research by the Anne Frank House indicates Anne Frank and her sister Margot died at Bergen-Belsen at least a month earlier than the previously accepted date of March 31, 1945. Researchers reviewed Red Cross and Bergen-Belsen Memorial archives and survivor testimony, finding witnesses saw both sisters showing typhus symptoms before February 7; most typhus deaths occur about 12 days after onset. Exact dates of death remain unknown, though Margot died first.
