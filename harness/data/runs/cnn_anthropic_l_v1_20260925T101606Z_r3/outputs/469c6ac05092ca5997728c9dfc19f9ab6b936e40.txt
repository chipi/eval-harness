Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin told CNN no video footage has been used in the Germanwings Flight 9525 crash investigation, disputing reports by Bild and Paris Match that a cell phone video captured the plane's final seconds. French officials called the reports "completely wrong," saying recovered cell phones have not yet been analysed.

Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz told its flight school in 2009 he had suffered a "previous episode of severe depression." Investigators believe he may have feared losing his pilot's licence over medical problems; all 150 on board died in the French Alps crash.
