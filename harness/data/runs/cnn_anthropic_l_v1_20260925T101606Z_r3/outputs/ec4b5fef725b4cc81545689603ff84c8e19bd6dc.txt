Former GOP Rep. Michele Bachmann likened President Obama to Andreas Lubitz, the Germanwings co-pilot accused of deliberately crashing Flight 9525, saying Obama's Iran deal makes him "a deranged pilot flying his entire nation into the rocks."

The March 31 Facebook post drew heavy criticism from commenters who called it inappropriate and an attempt to stay relevant.

Bachmann has previously urged Obama to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities, saying he laughed at her when she raised it at a 2014 White House Christmas party.
