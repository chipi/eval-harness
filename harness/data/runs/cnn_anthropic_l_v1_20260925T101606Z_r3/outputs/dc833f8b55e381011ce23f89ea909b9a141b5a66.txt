Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill, along with criticism from Apple's Tim Cook and the NCAA, prompted Governor Asa Hutchinson to refuse to sign the legislation unless its language was changed. The retailer's stance, following its February pay rise for 500,000 workers, marks it as a bellwether for shifting views in conservative states, with former Minnesota Governor Tim Pawlenty saying the moves "foreshadow where the Republican Party will need to move." The backlash has split Republican presidential hopefuls, with Jeb Bush softening his initial support for Indiana's similar law while Ted Cruz attacked companies for heeding an "aggressive gay marriage agenda."
