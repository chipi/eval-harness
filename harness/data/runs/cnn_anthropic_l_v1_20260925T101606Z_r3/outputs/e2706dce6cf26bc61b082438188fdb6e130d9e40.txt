Amnesty International's annual report says governments are invoking real or perceived terrorist threats to justify executions, citing Pakistan's lifting of its moratorium after the Peshawar school attack and China's "Strike Hard" campaign in Xinjiang. Executions recorded worldwide fell 22% to at least 607 in 2014, but death sentences rose 28% to at least 2,466, driven by mass sentencings in Egypt and Nigeria. Iran, Iraq and Saudi Arabia executed more people than the US, the only country in the Americas to carry out executions, while China is believed to kill thousands annually.
