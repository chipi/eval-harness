"Furious 7" opens Friday, more than a year after star Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40. Producers declined to kill off his character, rewriting the script and using special effects and Walker's brothers as body doubles to complete his scenes. At the Hollywood premiere, co-star Vin Diesel gave a tearful tribute, while Walker's brother Cody said the film is "bittersweet" but "Paul would be proud."
