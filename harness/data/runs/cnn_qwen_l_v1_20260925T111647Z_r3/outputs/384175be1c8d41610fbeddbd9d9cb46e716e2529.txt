Paul Walker's death in 2013 during a break from filming "Furious 7" deeply affected fans and co-stars, prompting an outpouring of tributes. The film was completed using CGI and his brothers as stand-ins, culminating in a poignant farewell to his character. At the premiere, Vin Diesel called it "more than a movie," reflecting widespread grief over the loss of an actor widely regarded as humble and kind.
