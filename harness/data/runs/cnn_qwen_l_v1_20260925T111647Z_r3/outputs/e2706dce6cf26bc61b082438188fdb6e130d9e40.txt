Amnesty International reports a global decline in executions in 2014 but warns of rising death sentences, citing governments' use of capital punishment to counter terrorism and unrest. While executions fell 22% worldwide—excluding China, which executes thousands secretly—death sentences surged 28%, driven by mass trials in Egypt and Nigeria. The U.S., Iran, Iraq, and Saudi Arabia remain among the top executioners, and most death penalty cases globally failed to meet fair trial standards.
