Hong Kong police found two dead women, one on the floor with neck and buttock cuts and the other in a suitcase on the balcony, after responding to a call at a Wan Chai apartment. Rurik Jutting, a 29-year-old former banker, was charged with two counts of murder; a court hearing was adjourned until May. The victims, identified as Sumarti Ningsih and Seneng Mujiasih, were Indonesian domestic workers who had worked in Hong Kong.
