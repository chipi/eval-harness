The film "Furious 7" earned a record $146 million over the weekend, with many moviegoers saying the final scenes, which paid tribute to late star Paul Walker, were emotionally moving. The film's makers used body doubles, computer-generated images, and Walker's brothers to complete his scenes, and chose to retire his character rather than kill him off. The movie's ending, which showed Walker as having retired to family life, was widely praised as a fitting tribute.
