French prosecutor Brice Robin says he is not aware of any video footage from Germanwings Flight 9525, contradicting claims by two magazines that they have seen a cell phone video of the plane's final moments. The magazines, Bild and Paris Match, described the video as showing passengers screaming and cries of "My God" in several languages. Germanwings co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had battled depression and had a valid medical certificate, Lufthansa said.
