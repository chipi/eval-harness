NASA scientists project that while primitive life is likely abundant across the Milky Way's estimated 40 billion habitable-zone planets, intelligent civilizations remain statistically rare and temporally isolated. Upcoming missions, including the James Webb Space Telescope, will prioritize detecting atmospheric biosignatures on exoplanets and subsurface oceans within our solar system over searching for communicative beings. Consequently, the discovery of simple extraterrestrial life appears imminent, but meaningful contact with advanced intelligence remains highly improbable in the near future.
