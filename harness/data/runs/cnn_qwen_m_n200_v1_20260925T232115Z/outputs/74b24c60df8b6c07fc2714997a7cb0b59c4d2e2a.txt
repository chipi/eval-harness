Marvel Studios unveiled Paul Bettany as Vision in a new "Avengers: Age of Ultron" poster, ending months of speculation about the android's appearance. The studio also released the first official look at Charlie Cox’s Daredevil suit ahead of the Netflix series premiere. Concurrently, 20th Century Fox announced Ben Hardy as Angel for "X-Men: Apocalypse" and revealed Jamie Bell’s CGI-rendered Thing for the "Fantastic Four" reboot.
