One year after Boko Haram abducted 276 Nigerian schoolgirls in Chibok, over 200 remain missing despite global outrage and the #BringBackOurGirls campaign. President-elect Muhammadu Buhari marked the anniversary with pledges to aggressively combat the militants, who continue to control significant territory and perpetrate violence. The unresolved crisis contributed to the defeat of incumbent President Goodluck Jonathan, while families endure ongoing grief and uncertainty.
