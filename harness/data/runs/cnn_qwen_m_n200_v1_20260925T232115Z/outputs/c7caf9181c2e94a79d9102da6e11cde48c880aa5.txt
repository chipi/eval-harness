The U.S. and P5+1 partners secured a framework agreement with Iran in Lausanne that curbs its nuclear program to prevent weapons development through strict limits and permanent inspections. Critics in Congress face rebuttal on claims regarding the deal's objectives, scope, and durability, as experts affirm the accord effectively blocks covert arsenal creation. Rejecting the agreement risks unraveling international sanctions and NATO cohesion without addressing the core security goal of preventing a nuclear-armed Iran.
