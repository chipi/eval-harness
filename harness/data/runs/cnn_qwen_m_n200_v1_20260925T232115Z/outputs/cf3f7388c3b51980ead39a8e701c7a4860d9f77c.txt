"Game of Thrones" returns for its fifth season with a simultaneous global premiere across 170 countries, marking the first time major plotlines advance beyond George R.R. Martin's published books. Key story arcs will converge as Tyrion meets Daenerys and Stannis clashes with the Night's Watch, while producers promise escalating drama and battles. The season also shifts focus to the Stark daughters, Cersei, and the growing unrest among Westeros' common folk.
