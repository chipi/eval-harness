Decorated veteran Emy Afalava and fellow American Samoans challenge their non-citizen status in federal court, arguing the 14th Amendment guarantees birthright citizenship. The lawsuit targets a legal exception rooted in early 20th-century racial precedents that denies full constitutional rights to residents of the U.S. territory. Plaintiffs seek to overturn this disparity, noting American Samoans remain the only inhabitants of U.S. island territories without automatic citizenship.
