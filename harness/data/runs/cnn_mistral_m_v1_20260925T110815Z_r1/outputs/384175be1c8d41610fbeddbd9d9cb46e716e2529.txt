Actor Paul Walker, star of the "Fast & Furious" franchise, died in a 2013 car crash mid-filming "Furious 7"; the film used CGI and body doubles to complete his role and includes a tribute to him. Fans and co-stars, including Vin Diesel, have honored his legacy, with tributes and emotional premieres marking the release. Walker, remembered for his kindness, left a lasting impact on colleagues and admirers.
