China held a mass public sentencing in Xinjiang in 2014, executing three under its "Strike Hard" anti-terror campaign, as global death penalty use rose in 2014, with Pakistan, Jordan, and Indonesia resuming executions amid crime and terrorism concerns. Amnesty International reports a 28% drop in global executions but notes China’s secretive, high execution rates. The trend reflects governments misusing capital punishment as a political tool despite no evidence of deterrence.
