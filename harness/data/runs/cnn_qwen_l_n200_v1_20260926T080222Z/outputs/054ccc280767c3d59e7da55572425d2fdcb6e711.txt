A former TSA agent, Daniel Boykin, 33, was sentenced to six months in jail and 5.5 years of probation for secretly recording a female co-worker in a TSA-only restroom and burglarizing her home to obtain over 90 videos and 1,500 photos. Boykin pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary, and computer crimes; the TSA confirmed he held an administrative role and was fired when the investigation began.
