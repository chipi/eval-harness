Intense Saudi-led airstrikes and ground fighting in Yemen have killed an estimated 600 people in under two weeks, devastated infrastructure, and left 16 million without electricity as the Houthis retain control of Sanaa. The ICRC urgently appeals for a humanitarian ceasefire to deliver medical aid, while the UN Security Council remains deadlocked over a Russian-drafted resolution calling for a halt to bombing. Meanwhile, al Qaeda in the Arabian Peninsula exploits the chaos, expanding its influence amid the collapse of central authority.
