The FBI confirmed that Zulkifli bin Hir, aka Marwan—a top wanted terrorist and bomb maker linked to Jemaah Islamiyah—was killed in a January raid in the southern Philippines. The operation, which targeted Marwan in a Bangsamoro Islamic Freedom Fighters stronghold, resulted in 44 elite Philippine police commandos dead and jeopardized a fragile peace deal with the Moro Islamic Liberation Front.
