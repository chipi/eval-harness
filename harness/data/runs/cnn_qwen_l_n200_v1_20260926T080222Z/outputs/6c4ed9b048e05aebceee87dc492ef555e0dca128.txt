Aaron Hernandez’s defense rested after less than a day of testimony in his murder trial for the 2013 killing of Odin Lloyd, following months of prosecution evidence largely based on circumstantial details. Key points include Hernandez allegedly lying to Patriots owner Robert Kraft about his alibi before the murder time was public, his fiancée’s disposal of a suspicious box, and disputed forensic evidence linking his shoes and a possible gun to the crime scene. The jury is expected to begin deliberations soon after closing arguments.
