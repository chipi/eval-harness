French prosecutor Brice Robin says no video footage from Germanwings Flight 9525 has been used in the crash investigation, contradicting claims by two magazines that they obtained a cell phone video showing the plane's final seconds. The magazines, Bild and Paris Match, described the video as showing passengers screaming and metallic banging, but did not post it online. Germanwings co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had battled depression years before the crash, according to Lufthansa, the parent company of Germanwings.
