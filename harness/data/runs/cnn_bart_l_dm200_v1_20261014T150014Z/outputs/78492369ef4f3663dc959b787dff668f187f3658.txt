Sir Alex Ferguson enjoyed a trophy-laden 26-and-a-half years in the Manchester United hotseat between 1986-2013. The Scot was appointed as the Red Devils manager in November 1986 at the age of 45 before announcing his retirement at the end of the 2012-13 season aged 71. Ferguson revealed that he had to modify his sleeping pattern to ensure he still had the same energy for the consuming role at United.
