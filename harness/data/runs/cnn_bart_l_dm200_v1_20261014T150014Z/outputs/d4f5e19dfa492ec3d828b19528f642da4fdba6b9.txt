Lucy Garrod, 27, developed an ulcer known as a corneal abrasion. It was caused by a bacterial infection which grew under her right lens. She woke up one day with an itchy and irritable right eye. Doctors said the ulcer was growing on the surface of her eye, millimetres away from permanently blinding her. She spent a week in hospital, where medics broke the news that she was still partially blind. She feared she would not be able to complete the last year of her photography degree. But she learned to take photographs with her left eye and passed with first class honours.
