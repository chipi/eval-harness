Hamza Parvez, 22, travelled to Syria in May 2014, telling his parent he was going to Germany. Nearly 11 months later, he still hasn't found a wife and has been living with a group of unmarried fighters in Raqqa, Syria. Like Hamza, all his friends appear to be struggling with life as a bachelor, with several taking to social media to voice their annoyance at being unable to escape their lonely life in Syria.
