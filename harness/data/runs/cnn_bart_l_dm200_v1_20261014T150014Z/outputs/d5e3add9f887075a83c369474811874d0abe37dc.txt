Fossicker Ashley Manzie found the medal in an east Melbourne park last week. It belonged to Frederick Biddulph, who served with the 23rd Australian Infantry Battalion at Gallipolli between 1914 and 1915. Mr Manzie posted photos of the medal to an online fossicking forum, which led him to Sydney man Stephen Norton - Bidulph's great-nephew. Mr Norton thinks the medal doesn't belong to Frederick at all, but to his brother Lewis William BiddULph.
