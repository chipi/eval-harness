Lawyers for Robert Durst say he got the money legally and it is not needed for evidence or subject to forfeiture in any of his legal proceedings. Authorities who arrested Durst in mid-March found $44,000, marijuana and a revolver at his hotel room in New Orleans. They also found maps of Florida and Cuba, leading some to believe he was ready for a life on the run. Durst is being held without bond on the California murder warrant and on state and federal weapons charges in Louisiana.
