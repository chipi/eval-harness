Aaron Lennon, 28, is on loan to Everton from Tottenham Hotspur. He went to Suede nightclub in Manchester with friends on April 4. The venue is popular with Premier League footballers and singer Trey Songz. But waitress claims she was attacked by the player in the VIP section. She fled to the toilet in tears and told police Lennon had left her with a bruise below her eye. Lennon was interviewed by officers under caution over the alleged assault.
