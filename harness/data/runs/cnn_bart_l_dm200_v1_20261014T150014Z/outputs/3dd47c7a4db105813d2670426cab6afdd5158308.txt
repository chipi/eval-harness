Daniel Levy reportedly told the Tottenham Hotspur Supporters' Trust that future transfer policy would entail the purchase of young players, priced between £10-15million with good potential for resale. This may be financially sensible, but it is hardly reassuring. Elite clubs, those who challenge for the biggest trophies, do not buy with one eye on selling. Spurs' best chance of securing regular Champions League football came when Luka Modric played for them.
