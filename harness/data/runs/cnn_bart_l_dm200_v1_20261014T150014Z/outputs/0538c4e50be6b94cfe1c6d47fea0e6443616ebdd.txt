Gina Maria Schumacher, 17, took part in the NRHA European Futurity Horse Show in the Bavarian town of Kreuth yesterday afternoon. Dressed in a variety of elaborately decorated cowboy-style outfits and wearing a brown stetson hat, she was seen riding a number of different horses. She is expected to compete in the grand final of the tournament on Saturday evening. Comes just days after her 16-year-old brother Mick returned to the racing circuit just weeks after he was involved in a 100mph crash.
