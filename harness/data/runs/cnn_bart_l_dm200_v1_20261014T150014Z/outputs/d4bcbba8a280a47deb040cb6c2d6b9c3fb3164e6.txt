A six-month-old kitten was found lying on the ground covered in maggots and blood. The cat had fallen from a building in Zetland, inner-east Sydney, last month. It was taken in by a vet clinic, My Vet Animal Hospital, who shared the story of aptly-named 'Seven' on social media. Since then, the cat has won the hearts of many who have donated funds to help pay for the his hefty medical bills and surgeries. And now he is on the hunt for a new home.
