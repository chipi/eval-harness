Baby girl was found face down in a toilet bowl at Silcock's Amusement Arcade in Southport, Merseyside. It is believed the girl, named April by staff at the Ormskirk hospital where she is being treated, might have been delivered at the arcade hours before she was discovered. Police had released an image of the 6lb 9oz baby girl, wearing a pink playsuit, as they sought to identify her mother. Officers said they had traced the mother to an address in the Merseysides area.
