Professor Eric Cline claims a 'perfect storm' of disasters caused the collapse of Ancient Egypt, the Babylonians, Minoans and Mycenians. He claims a series of droughts, famines, climate change, earthquakes, invasions and internal rebellions between 1225BC and 1177 BC happened in quick succession. The collapse of one also affected the others, creating a domino-like effect, he claims. The demise of the powerful ancient civilisations has long been blamed on a mysterious group of seafaring raiders known as the Sea Peoples. But Professor Cline says the Sea People's may have been unfairly blamed for causing the collapse.
