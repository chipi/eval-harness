Margaret Hesketh went in to the Royal Albert Edward Infirmary in Wigan. The 70-year-old had an infected bed sore and suspected pneumonia. Doctors ruled out surgery to remove pressure sores because they feared she would not survive the operation. Ten days after she was admitted a consultant summoned the family to tell them a scan had revealed cancer. A Macmillan nurse allegedly told the family Mrs HesKeth probably had less than three months to live.
