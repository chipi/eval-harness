Ayyan Ali has been in jail since March 14 when she was caught with over $500,000 in her carry-on bag in Islamabad airport. She was charged with attempting to illegally transport more than the legal $10,000 cash limit out of the country. She has been housed in Adiala which is the central prison of the garrison city Rawalpindi. Her lawyers say she had yet to check in and was going to hand the money over to her brother before flying.
