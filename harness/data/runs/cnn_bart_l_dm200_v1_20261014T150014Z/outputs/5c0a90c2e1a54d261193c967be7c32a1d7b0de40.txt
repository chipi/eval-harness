Two-year-old girl from Gardena, California, disappeared from a car wash on April 2. She was found two hours later in a parking lot at Jim's Burgers, 13 miles away. The little girl had scratches on her legs and was screaming and putting her hands over her eyes, witnesses said. Police initially said the person responsible for the kidnapping was a serial predator. Detectives worked around the clock to find the kidnapper. A tip came in on April 5 from a man who had seen the footage on the Nissan Altima on TV, and said his neighbor, Ikela, had been making changes to his car, which fit the description. Undercover officers began investigating.
