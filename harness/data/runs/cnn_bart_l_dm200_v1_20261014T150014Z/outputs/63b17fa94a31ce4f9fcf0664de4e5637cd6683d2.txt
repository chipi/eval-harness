A 38-year-old man jumped into Brush Creek in Kansas City, Missouri, early Friday morning after being stopped by police. He spent 10-15 minutes swimming in chest-deep water, with officers waiting for him on north and south sides of the creek. Police officers armed with a BB gun followed the man into the creek, got him in a choke hold and pulled him out. The suspect was taken to an area hospital to be treated for injuries to his arm and leg.
