Jessica McCarty faces three counts of first-degree murder for the deaths of her three children in Palm Bay, Florida, on March 20. McCarty's partner, Christopher Swist, who was dating McCarty at the time of the alleged murders, found the children unresponsive and called the police. Lacey, seven, Phillip, six, and Christopher, five months, were found dead in the same room at the home. Police say none of the three children were shot or stabbed, but lacerations were found. Swist said that he had a close bond with all of the children and gave them all nicknames to go by.
