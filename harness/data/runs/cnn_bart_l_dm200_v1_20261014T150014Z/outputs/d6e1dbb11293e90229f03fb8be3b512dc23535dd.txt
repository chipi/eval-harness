Ian Bell hit his 22nd Test century to rescue England after a top-order collapse. Bell and Joe Root put on 177 for the fourth wicket after England's top order collapsed in Antigua. Jonathan Trott's return to the team ended with a three-ball duck when he was dismissed in the first over. England captain Alastair Cook was dismissed for just 11 runs by West Indies fast bowler Kemar Roach. Gary Ballance was the third to fall to a bad shot with just 34 on the board.
