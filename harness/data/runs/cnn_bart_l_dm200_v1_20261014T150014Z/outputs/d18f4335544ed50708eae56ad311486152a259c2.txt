Summer Michelle Hansen, 32, was sentenced on Friday to three years in state prison and must also register as a sex offender for life. Hansen entered a guilty plea in February to the 16 felony counts related to sex acts with her five male students. The crimes took place in her classroom, a school utility room, her car and at one victim's home between May 2012 and 2013. Parents of the victims were upset that Hansen's plea agreement was approved, which was made directly to Riverside County Superior Court Judge Becky Dugan. If she had been convicted at trial, Hansen would have faced 13 years in prison.
