Samantha Crossland, 30, pocketed parents' fees being paid to the Child's Play day nursery she worked at in a trusted position. Crossland had been promoted to manager at the nursery by its owner who knew her and trusted her. Much of the money was taken from a cash box on the wall that parents left their fees in. Nursery owner Lynda Quigley confirmed that the amount stolen - £22,642 - had been paid into her bank account on from an 'S Crossland' Crossland was sentenced to a 12-month community order and 180 hours unpaid work.
