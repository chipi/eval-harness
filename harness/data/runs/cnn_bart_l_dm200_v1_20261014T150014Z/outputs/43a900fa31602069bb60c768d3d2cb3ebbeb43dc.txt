Dimitri Harrell, 21, allegedly shot dead fiancée Samirria White, 19, on Sunday. Allegedly pulled out his gun and pointed it at her in bedroom of St Paul apartment. Carrying couple's three-month-old child, he then shot Miss White in face, police said. When medics arrived at scene, they pronounced the young mother dead. Now, Harrell has been charged with one count of second-degree murder. If convicted, he faces up to 40 years in prison. Miss White had 'trust issues' with Harrell after having two children by another woman.
