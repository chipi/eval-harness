Anne-Louise van den Nieuwenhof was diagnosed with a rare bone and soft tissue cancer, called Ewing’s sarcoma, aged just five. Her parents were informed she only had three months to live. She defied all the odds and moved to Canada where she worked as a nurse and began raising her own family. However, she tragically died during a routine hospital check-up for her baby girl Elizabeth Kelly, near her home in Rossland, British Columbia in Canada.
