Sonia Sharrock watched in horror as her stud farm in Woodberry, in Maitland in the Hunter Region of New South Wales rapidly flooded. Her brother-in-law Steve Spowart dived down into the freezing cold water to cut the barbed wire and free the horses that had become tangled in fences. The animals' owner, Sonia Sharrock, praised her ‘absolutely amazing’ brother, saying ‘I don’t know what I would have done without him and my sister’
