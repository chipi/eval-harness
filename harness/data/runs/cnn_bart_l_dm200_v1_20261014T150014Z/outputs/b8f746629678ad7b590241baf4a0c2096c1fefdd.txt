Noaa Fisheries in Maryland says the humpback whale is no longer endangered. They want to break up global population into 14 sub-populations. Ten of these will be 'not at risk', two 'threatened' and two still 'endangered' It follows a conservation 'success story' that raised numbers to 90,000. Some experts have warned it is too soon to declare the conservation project a success, and that more information is needed to make such a decision.
