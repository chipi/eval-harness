Aaron Hernandez was sentenced to life in prison without parole on Wednesday for the 2013 murder of Odin Lloyd. He was booked at MCI Cedar Junction in Walpole, Massachusetts, where he will serve his sentence. The prison is about a mile and a half from Gillette Stadium, where Hernandez used to play tight end for the New England Patriots. Hernandez hasn't set foot in the stadium since his arrest in June 2013.
