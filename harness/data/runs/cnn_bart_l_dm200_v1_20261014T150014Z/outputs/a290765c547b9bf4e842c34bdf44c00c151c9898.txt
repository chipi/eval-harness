An FBI affidavit from November 2014 that was recently unsealed reveals translator Xiaoming Gao shared information with an alleged Chinese spy. In the affidavit, Timothy S. Pappa, an FBI agent, claims that Gao was paid 'thousands of dollars to provide information on U.S. persons and a government employee' Gao admitted to the meetings, and also admitted to reporting on her 'social contacts' in the United States, according to the affidavit. Despite these claims however, she was never prosecuted by the government.
