More than 500,000 people knock on the doors of Abbey Road studios every year only to be told that it is closed to the public. The studios hosted the world's first live global broadcast - from none other than The Beatles - as well as playing a role in the invention of stereo. With the aim of sharing some of this history, Google has stepped in to showcase as much of it as possible. The result is Inside Abbey Road, a new web app that takes users on an interactive, immersive and hugely detailed virtual tour of the inner workings of the studios.
