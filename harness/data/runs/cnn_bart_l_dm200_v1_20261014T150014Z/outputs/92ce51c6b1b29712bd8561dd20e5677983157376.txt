Finder.com.au money and real estate expert Michelle Hutchison says there are easy ways to improve the look and feel of your home. 'Spending a relatively small amount can go a seriously long way when it comes to the returns you could make on the value of your house,' she told Daily Mail Australia. Changing the door handles is her first tip. 'Replacing tired, grubby and dated door handles to shiny, matching new ones throughout your home can transform your doors and hallways,' she said.
