Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash for signing a "religious freedom" law critics say permits anti-LGBT discrimination, drawing defense from 2016 GOP hopeful Jeb Bush. The controversy highlights how the issue could push Republican presidential candidates further right, with figures like Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker all voicing opposition to same-sex marriage rights. Similar bills are pending in roughly 15 states, suggesting LGBT rights will remain a contentious issue in the 2016 race despite growing national acceptance of same-sex marriage.
