Paul Walker died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40 while on break from filming "Furious 7," which releases Friday. Producers rewrote the script and used his brothers as body doubles to complete his scenes, and the film includes a tribute to Walker as his character is "retired." Fans and co-stars, including Vin Diesel, have paid emotional tributes to the actor, who was widely remembered for his kindness.
