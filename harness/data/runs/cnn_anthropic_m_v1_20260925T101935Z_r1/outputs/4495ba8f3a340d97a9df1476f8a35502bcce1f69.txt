Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, key negotiator in the Iran nuclear deal alongside U.S. Secretary of State John Kerry, is often described as "polished" and "jovial," but lesser-known facts reveal a more complex figure. These include his education in the U.S. during Iran's 1979 revolution, conflicting official birth years, past investigation over ties to a foundation accused of laundering money for Iran, and his early activism seizing the Iranian consulate in San Francisco.
