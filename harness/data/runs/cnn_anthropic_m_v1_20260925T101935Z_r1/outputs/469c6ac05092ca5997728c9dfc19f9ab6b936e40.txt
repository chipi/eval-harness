Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied reports by Bild and Paris Match of a cell phone video showing the final moments inside Germanwings Flight 9525, calling on anyone with such footage to hand it to investigators. Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a history of severe depression to the airline's flight school in 2009, raising questions about how he was cleared to fly. Investigators believe Lubitz may have deliberately crashed the plane out of fear of losing his pilot's license due to health issues.
