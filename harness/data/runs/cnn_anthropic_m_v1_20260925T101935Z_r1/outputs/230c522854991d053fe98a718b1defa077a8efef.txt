A stray dog named Theia survived being hit by a car, hit with a hammer and buried in Washington State, later staggering to a farm where she was rescued. She is receiving treatment at Washington State University's veterinary hospital for injuries including a dislocated jaw and caved-in sinus cavity, with fundraising efforts surpassing their $10,000 goal. The case echoes a similar incident in Florida where a cat survived being buried after being hit by a car.
