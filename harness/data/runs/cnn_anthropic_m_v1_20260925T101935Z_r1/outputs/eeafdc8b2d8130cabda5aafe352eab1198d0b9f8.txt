Mötley Crüe's Vince Neil drew criticism for a botched national anthem performance at a Las Vegas Outlaws game, joining a long list of notable anthem flops and triumphs. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl rendition set the gold standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 performance was called "disgraceful" by President George H.W. Bush. Jimi Hendrix's Woodstock version remains iconic, while Michael Bolton famously forgot the lyrics in 2003.
