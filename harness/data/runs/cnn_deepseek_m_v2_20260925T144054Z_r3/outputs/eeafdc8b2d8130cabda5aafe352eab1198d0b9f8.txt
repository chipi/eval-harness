Vince Neil's rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas Outlaws game drew criticism, adding him to a list of famous singers who have delivered memorable performances of "The Star-Spangled Banner." Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl version set the modern standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 Padres performance drew boos and a presidential rebuke. Jimi Hendrix's 1969 Woodstock take and Michael Bolton's 2003 word-flubbing rendition also rank among the most notable.
