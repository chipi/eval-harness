Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denies any video from Germanwings Flight 9525 is part of the investigation, demanding anyone with footage turn it over, after Bild and Paris Match claimed to have cell phone video of the final seconds. French gendarmerie and BEA officials call the reports "completely wrong," saying recovered cell phones have not yet been analyzed. Separately, Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz disclosed a 2009 episode of severe depression during training and was allowed to continue flying.
