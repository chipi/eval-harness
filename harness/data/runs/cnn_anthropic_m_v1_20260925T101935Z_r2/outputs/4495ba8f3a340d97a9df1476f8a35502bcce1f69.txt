Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, hailed as a hero in Iran after helping broker a nuclear framework with the US, has a lesser-known past that includes studying in America during the 1979 revolution and taking part in a takeover of Iran's San Francisco consulate. He was also investigated by US federal authorities over alleged ties to a foundation accused of laundering money for Iran, though he was not named in the eventual settlement. Even his birth year remains disputed, with Iran's own Foreign Ministry citing different dates.
