Rev. Robert H. Schuller, founder of the "Hour of Power" television ministry and Crystal Cathedral megachurch, died Thursday at age 88 after battling esophageal cancer. Schuller built a preaching empire from humble beginnings, pioneering televised Sunday services and popularizing an uplifting "possibility thinking" gospel, but his later years were marred by a family succession dispute and the 2010 bankruptcy of Crystal Cathedral Ministries.
