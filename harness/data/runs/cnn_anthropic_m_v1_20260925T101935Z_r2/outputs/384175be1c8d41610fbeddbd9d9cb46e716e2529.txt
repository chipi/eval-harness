Paul Walker, who died in a November 2013 car crash at age 40, is remembered by fans and colleagues as production of "Furious 7" concludes with tributes to him. Producers rewrote the script and used special effects and Walker's brothers as body doubles to complete his scenes, while co-star Vin Diesel named his newborn daughter after him. The film's release has sparked renewed grief and homage from fans who felt a personal connection to the actor.
