Indiana Gov. Mike Pence faces backlash for signing a "religious freedom" law critics say permits anti-LGBT discrimination, even as he draws support from conservatives eyeing him as a 2016 presidential contender. The controversy highlights how LGBT rights, including similar bills in other states and potential constitutional amendments on marriage, could become a defining and divisive issue in the Republican primary. Despite national polling showing growing acceptance of same-sex marriage, the article argues conservative "winning" on this issue should not be mistaken for having "won."
