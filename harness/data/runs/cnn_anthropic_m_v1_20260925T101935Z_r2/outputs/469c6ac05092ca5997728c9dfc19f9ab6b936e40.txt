Marseille prosecutor Brice Robin denied claims by Bild and Paris Match that a cell phone video from inside Germanwings Flight 9525 during its crash exists, calling for anyone with such footage to hand it to investigators. Lufthansa confirmed co-pilot Andreas Lubitz had disclosed a "previous episode of severe depression" to its flight school in 2009, raising questions about how he was cleared to fly. Investigators believe Lubitz's fear of losing his pilot's license due to medical issues may have been a motive for deliberately crashing the plane, killing all 150 aboard.
