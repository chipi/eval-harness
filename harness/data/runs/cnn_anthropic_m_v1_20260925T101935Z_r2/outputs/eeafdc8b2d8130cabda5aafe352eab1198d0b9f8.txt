Vince Neil's rendition of the national anthem at a Las Vegas football game was widely mocked, continuing a long tradition of memorable performances gone wrong. Whitney Houston's 1991 Super Bowl performance remains the gold standard, while Roseanne Barr's 1990 version drew boos and presidential condemnation. Jimi Hendrix's Woodstock rendition and Michael Bolton's lyric-flubbing 2003 performance round out the list of notable anthem moments.
