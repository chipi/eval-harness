"Game of Thrones" season five premieres Sunday across 170 countries, with major story arcs converging and battles escalating toward the series' end after seven seasons. Key plotlines include Stannis at the Wall, Tyrion meeting Daenerys, and the Stark sisters and Cersei taking focus, while the show moves ahead of George R.R. Martin's books. The season also explores common folk revolting against noble-driven wars.
