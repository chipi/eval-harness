One year after Boko Haram abducted nearly 300 schoolgirls in Chibok, Nigeria, most remain missing, with fears of rape, enslavement, and forced conversion. President-elect Muhammadu Buhari marked the anniversary with condolences, while global figures like Malala Yousafzai voiced support. The group still controls parts of the northeast, and the #BringBackOurGirls campaign continues.
