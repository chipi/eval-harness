Mötley Crüe’s Vince Neil joins a roster of celebrities who faced public backlash for botched renditions of "The Star-Spangled Banner." High-profile failures by Roseanne Barr and Michael Bolton contrast sharply with Whitney Houston’s acclaimed 1991 Super Bowl performance. Jimi Hendrix’s 1969 Woodstock interpretation remains a polarizing yet iconic entry in the anthem's controversial history.
