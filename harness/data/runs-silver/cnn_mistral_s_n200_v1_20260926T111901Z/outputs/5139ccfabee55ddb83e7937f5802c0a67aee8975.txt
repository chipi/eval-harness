U.S. complains to Russia after a Russian Su-27 intercepted a U.S. RC-135U reconnaissance plane in an "unsafe and unprofessional" manner over the Baltic Sea, with both sides disputing details. Pentagon calls the Russian jet's maneuvers aggressive, while Moscow claims the U.S. plane had its transponder off. Similar incidents have occurred before, including a 2023 close encounter over the Sea of Okhotsk.
