Rurik Jutting, a 29-year-old British former banker, is charged with murdering two women, Sumarti Ningsih and Seneng Mujiasih, whose bodies were found at his Hong Kong apartment last November. A court hearing to assess evidence before trial was adjourned until May, pending analysis of over 200 forensic and DNA samples. Both victims were Indonesian women working in Hong Kong; their bodies have since been repatriated and buried.
