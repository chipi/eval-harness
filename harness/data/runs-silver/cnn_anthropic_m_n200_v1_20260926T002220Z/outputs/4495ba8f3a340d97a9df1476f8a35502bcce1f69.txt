Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif, key negotiator in the Iran nuclear talks with US Secretary of State John Kerry, is known publicly as polished and jovial, but lesser-known facts reveal a more complex history. These include his education in the US during Iran's 1979 revolution, conflicting birth years listed by his own ministry, past investigation over an Iranian charity accused of laundering money, and his early activism seizing the Iranian consulate in San Francisco. He has now spent more face time with Kerry than any other foreign minister as they work toward a June 30 deadline.
