"Grace of Monaco," the Nicole Kidman-led biopic that opened last year's Cannes Film Festival, will bypass U.S. theaters and premiere on Lifetime May 25, following poor international box office and a public feud between director Olivier Dahan and Weinstein Co. chief Harvey Weinstein over the final cut. The film, which drew criticism from Grace Kelly's own children for being "historically inaccurate," also stars Tim Roth, Frank Langella and Parker Posey.
