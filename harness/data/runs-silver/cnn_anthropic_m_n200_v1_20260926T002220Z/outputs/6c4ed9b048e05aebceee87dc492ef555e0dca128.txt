Aaron Hernandez's defense rested Monday after less than a day of testimony in his murder trial for the 2013 killing of Odin Lloyd, following months of prosecution witnesses. Key disputes include Hernandez's contradictory statements to Patriots owner Robert Kraft, his fiancee's testimony about disposing of a box, footwear impression analysis, and whether he was holding a gun or an iPad in home security footage. Closing arguments are set for Tuesday, with Hernandez still facing a separate double-murder trial after this case concludes.
