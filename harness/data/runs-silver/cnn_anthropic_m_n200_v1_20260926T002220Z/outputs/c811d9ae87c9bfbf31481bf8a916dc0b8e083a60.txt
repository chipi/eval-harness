Rolling Stone retracted its article on an alleged UVA gang rape after a Columbia Journalism School review found major reporting failures, including not contacting Jackie's friends who disputed her account. Despite the discredited story, the underlying issue of UVA's handling of sexual assault remains under investigation by the state attorney general and the U.S. Department of Education, with the university admitting it has never expelled a student for sexual assault. Survivors and advocates worry the retraction could discourage future victims from coming forward, even as officials note legitimate concerns about UVA's practices remain unresolved.
