UNRWA Commissioner-General Pierre Krähenbühl will make an emergency visit to Syria's Yarmouk refugee camp to assess the humanitarian crisis affecting 18,000 remaining Palestinians and Syrians. The camp, largely controlled by ISIS and Al-Nusra Front, has been besieged since 2013, with reports of malnutrition and barrel bombings. Its population has fallen from 160,000 in 2011 to roughly 18,000 today.
