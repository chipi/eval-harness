Easter combines Christian religious observance—marking Christ's crucifixion and resurrection—with folk traditions like eggs and bunnies that likely originated in German culture. Its date, tied to Passover and lunar cycles, varies annually and differs between Western and Orthodox churches, which use different calendars. Pope Francis marked Holy Week at the Vatican with rain-soaked services and calls for peace amid global conflicts, while Jerusalem's Church of the Holy Sepulcher hosted separate celebrations for Catholics, Armenians, and Eastern Orthodox Christians.
