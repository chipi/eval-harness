Amnesty International reports governments are increasingly using the threat of terrorism to justify executions, even as global killings fell by 22 percent in 2014. The organization warned that flawed trials often precede death sentences, which spiked by 28 percent due to mass sentencing in nations like Egypt and Nigeria. China, Iran, Iraq, and Saudi Arabia remained the top executors, while the U.S. saw a slight decline in its use of capital punishment.
