Ryan Gosling’s directorial debut, "Lost River," is garnering attention for its depiction of a decaying Detroit, which served as the film’s primary inspiration and setting. Gosling began filming the city’s abandoned Brewster-Douglass Projects fearing their demolition, and the project evolved into a story about resilience amidst urban decline. Despite critical panning, the film aims to portray both the hardship and potential rebirth occurring in Detroit.
