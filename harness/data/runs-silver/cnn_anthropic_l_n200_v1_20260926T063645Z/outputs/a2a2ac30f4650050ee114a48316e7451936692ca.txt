Massachusetts man Carlos Colina, 32, will be arraigned April 14 on a murder charge after human remains were found Saturday in a duffel bag in Cambridge, prosecutors said. Police found additional body parts in a nearby apartment building after reviewing surveillance video; the remains were identified as Jonathan Camilien, 26, who knew Colina. Colina was earlier arraigned on assault and battery and improper disposal of a body charges, and a judge revoked his bail in a separate assault case.
