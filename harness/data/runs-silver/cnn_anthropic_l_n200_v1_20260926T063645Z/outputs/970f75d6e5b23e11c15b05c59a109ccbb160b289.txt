Iran will lift its ban on women attending some men's sports events within the next year, Deputy Sports Minister Abdolhamid Ahmadi said, with restrictions reportedly eased for indoor events. Women have been barred from most men's sports since the 1979 Islamic Revolution, with a brief exception for volleyball under President Khatami. The move follows international pressure, including FIFA President Sepp Blatter's criticism of the "intolerable" ban and Iran's failed bid to host the 2019 Asian Cup.
