Louis Jordan, 37, was rescued by a container ship crew hundreds of miles offshore after 66 days adrift on his disabled 35-foot sailboat, which capsized three times and lost its mast after he set out from Conway, South Carolina. Jordan, who broke a shoulder, survived on collected rainwater and fish he netted after they were drawn to his laundry trailed in the water. Rescuers and his father said he was in surprisingly good condition; the Coast Guard says it has no reason to doubt his account.
