Chinese state broadcaster CCTV said it will investigate popular host Bi Fujian after a leaked video showed him cursing Mao Zedong at a private dinner party, calling the late leader an "old son of a b***h." Bi apologized on Weibo, saying his remarks had "grave social consequences." The clip has been scrubbed from Chinese video sites and split opinion online, with some demanding punishment and others saying he was set up.
