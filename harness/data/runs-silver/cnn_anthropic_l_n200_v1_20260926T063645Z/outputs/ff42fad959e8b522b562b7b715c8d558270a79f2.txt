A Secret Service supervisor, Xavier Morales, has been placed on administrative leave and had his security clearance suspended after a female subordinate accused him of making sexual advances and grabbing her arms during a struggle at the agency's office following a March 31 party. Director Joe Clancy called the allegations "very disturbing," saying workplace threats or violence "will not be tolerated." The case, to be probed by the Office of the Inspector General, is the latest in a string of scandals that have prompted a leadership change and warnings the agency is stretched "beyond its limits."
