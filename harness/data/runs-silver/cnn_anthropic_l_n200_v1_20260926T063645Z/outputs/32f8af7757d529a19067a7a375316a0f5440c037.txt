Multiple tornadoes struck the rural Midwest on Thursday, killing one person in the Illinois hamlet of Fairdale and destroying homes and businesses. A twister cut a 22-mile path through Ogle County, where about 20 homes were destroyed — including the newly elected sheriff's — and 50 to 100 more damaged. Eight tornadoes were reported Wednesday in Oklahoma, Kansas and Missouri, with no other deaths reported; officials credited National Weather Service warnings with saving lives.
