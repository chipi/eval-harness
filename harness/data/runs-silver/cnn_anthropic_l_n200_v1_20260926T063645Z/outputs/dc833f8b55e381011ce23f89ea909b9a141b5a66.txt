Walmart's opposition to Arkansas' religious freedom bill, coming weeks after it raised wages for 500,000 workers, marked the retailer as a bellwether of shifting public opinion in conservative states. Arkansas Gov. Asa Hutchinson said he would not sign the bill unless its language was changed, hours after Walmart CEO Doug McMillon urged a veto.

Business backlash also forced Indiana Gov. Mike Pence to pledge changes to his state's law, while Republican presidential hopefuls split, with Jeb Bush softening his support and Ted Cruz attacking companies for following an "extreme left wing agenda."
