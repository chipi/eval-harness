CNN profiles several people who survived being lost at sea, including Louis Jordan, rescued off North Carolina after 66 days on his capsized sailboat, and Jose Salvador Alvarenga, who drifted across the Pacific for more than a year before washing up in the Marshall Islands.

Others include Hawaii fisherman Ron Ingraham, found alive 12 days after a search was called off, and Panamanian teenager Adrian Vasquez, rescued near the Galapagos after 26 days adrift in which his two companions died.

The article also cites Hiromitsu Shinkawa, swept nine miles out to sea by Japan's 2011 tsunami and rescued two days later clinging to his roof.
