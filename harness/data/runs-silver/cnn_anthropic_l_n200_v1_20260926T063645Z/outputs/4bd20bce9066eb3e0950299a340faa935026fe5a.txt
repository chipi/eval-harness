American Samoan veteran Emy Afalava and other residents of the U.S. territory are suing in the D.C. Circuit, arguing the Fourteenth Amendment's birthright clause makes them U.S. citizens. The federal government deems them non-citizen nationals, denying them the right to vote. Advocates trace the policy to early 20th-century Supreme Court rulings on island territories rooted in racial prejudice, noting American Samoans are the only U.S. territory residents without citizenship.
