Indian software pioneer Ramalinga Raju and nine others were sentenced to seven years in jail over India's biggest corporate fraud in memory, police said. Raju, former chairman of Satyam Computer Services, was also fined $804,000 after admitting in 2009 to inflating profits with fictitious assets in a $1.6 billion scam. Satyam was then India's fourth-largest software services provider; it was later sold to Tech Mahindra after the government fired its board.
