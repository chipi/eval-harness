One hundred people aboard the cruise ship Celebrity Infinity have fallen ill with vomiting, diarrhea and other gastrointestinal symptoms, the CDC said — 95 of 2,117 passengers and five of 964 crew. The cause is undetermined; two CDC staffers will meet the ship in San Diego on Monday. Celebrity Cruises has stepped up cleaning and disinfection and collected specimens for testing; it is the ship's third such outbreak after 2006 and 2013.
