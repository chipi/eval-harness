Indiana Gov. Mike Pence's signing of a religious freedom law seen as enabling anti-LGBT discrimination is read as an effort to court ultraconservative voters and donors ahead of a possible 2016 presidential run.

The move drew swift backing from Jeb Bush and echoes positions from Ted Cruz, Mike Huckabee, Bobby Jindal and Scott Walker, with similar bills advancing in about 15 states.

Analysts warn the fight will pull the GOP field rightward and keep LGBT rights central to the 2016 campaign, despite polls showing growing national support for same-sex marriage.
