A former TSA agent received a six-month jail sentence and five-and-a-half years of probation for secretly videotaping a female co-worker in a restroom and burglarizing her home. Daniel Boykin pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary, and computer violations after police discovered over 90 videos and 1,500 photos of the victim on his devices. The judge condemned the "egregious" invasion of privacy, while the TSA reaffirmed its zero-tolerance policy for employee misconduct following Boykin's termination.
