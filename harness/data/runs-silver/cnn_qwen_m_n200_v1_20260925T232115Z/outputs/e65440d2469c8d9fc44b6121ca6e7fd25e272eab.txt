The U.S. Department of Justice has indicted FedEx on 15 criminal counts, alleging the courier service knowingly facilitated illegal prescription drug distribution despite prior warnings from federal agencies. FedEx maintains its innocence, arguing it qualifies for a "common carrier" exemption that protects transporters unaware of package contents from possession charges. The case highlights the legal complexities of corporate criminal liability and the government's strategy of targeting supply chains to combat the illicit pharmaceutical market.
