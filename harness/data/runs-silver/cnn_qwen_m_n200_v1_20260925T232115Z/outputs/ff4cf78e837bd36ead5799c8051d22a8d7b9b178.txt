Hillary Clinton officially enters the 2016 presidential race, leveraging extensive political experience and strong poll numbers despite lingering concerns over authenticity and trust. To avoid the pitfalls of her 2008 campaign and Al Gore’s 2000 bid, she must energize the Democratic base with a clear policy vision rather than relying on inevitability. Her path forward requires navigating challenges including gender bias, skepticism from progressive voters, and potential liabilities associated with her husband and the Clinton Foundation.
