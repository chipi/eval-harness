Hundreds of mourners gathered in South Carolina for the funeral of Walter Scott, whose fatal shooting by a police officer has sparked national debate on race and law enforcement. Former Officer Michael Slager faces murder charges after bystander video contradicted his initial account of the incident, though defense attorneys cite witness reports of a prior struggle. Family members and officials hailed Scott’s death as a catalyst for change while legal experts anticipate challenges to the murder charge based on claims of self-defense.
