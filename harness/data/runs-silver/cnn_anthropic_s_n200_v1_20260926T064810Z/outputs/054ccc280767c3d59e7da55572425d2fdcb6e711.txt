A former TSA agent in Tennessee was sentenced to six months in jail for secretly videotaping a female co-worker in a bathroom and breaking into her home, where he collected over 90 videos and 1,500 photos. Daniel Boykin pleaded guilty to unlawful photography, aggravated burglary and computer crimes, and will serve five and a half years of probation with GPS monitoring. The judge called the invasion of privacy "egregious," and the TSA terminated Boykin when the investigation began.
