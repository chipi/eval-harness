Multiple tornadoes swept through the rural Midwest on Thursday, with a particularly destructive twister cutting a 22-mile path through Illinois's Ogle County near Rochelle and Fairdale, destroying homes and killing one person. The storm damaged or destroyed approximately 70 houses in the area, though National Weather Service warnings helped minimize casualties. The severe weather also affected Oklahoma, Kansas, Missouri, and Iowa, with hail the size of tennis balls reported in some areas.
