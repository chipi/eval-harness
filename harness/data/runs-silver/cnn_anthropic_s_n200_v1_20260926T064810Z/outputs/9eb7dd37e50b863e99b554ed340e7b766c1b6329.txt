A Delaware family fell ill during a U.S. Virgin Islands vacation, possibly from methyl bromide pesticide exposure at their rented villa; the father is stable while his two teenage sons remain in critical condition. The EPA confirmed the presence of the restricted pesticide at the resort, which is banned for indoor use in the U.S. due to its acute toxicity and neurotoxic effects. The Department of Justice has launched a criminal investigation into how the odorless chemical was applied in a residential area.
