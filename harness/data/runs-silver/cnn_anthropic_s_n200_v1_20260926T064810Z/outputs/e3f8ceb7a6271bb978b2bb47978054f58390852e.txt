Former Maryland Gov. Martin O'Malley and ex-Virginia Sen. Jim Webb, both considering 2016 Democratic presidential bids, displayed starkly different campaign styles at an Iowa Democratic event Friday—O'Malley working the room with handshakes and selfies while Webb remained reserved and biographical in his approach. Both long-shot candidates face steep odds against frontrunner Hillary Clinton, polling at just 1-3% among Democrats. Webb emphasized governance and criminal justice reform, while O'Malley delivered applause-line-heavy rhetoric about the American Dream and workers' wages.
