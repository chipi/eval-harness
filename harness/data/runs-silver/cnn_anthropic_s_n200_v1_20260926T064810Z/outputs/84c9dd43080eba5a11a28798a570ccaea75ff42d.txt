A judge ruled Monday that Craig Hicks, charged in the February deaths of three Muslim college students in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, is eligible for the death penalty. Hicks, 21, shot neighbors Yusor Mohammad, Deah Shaddy Barakat, and Razan Mohammad Abu-Salha in the head; authorities are investigating whether the killings were motivated by a parking dispute or hate crime. The FBI and Department of Justice have opened a parallel inquiry into potential federal hate crime violations.
