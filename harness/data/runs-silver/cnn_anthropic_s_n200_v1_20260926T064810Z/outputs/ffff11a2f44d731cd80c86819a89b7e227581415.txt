A 37-year-old unemployed truck driver was rescued after drifting at sea for 66 days in a damaged sailboat off the Carolina coast. Louis Jordan left Conway, South Carolina in January to fish but was capsized by a storm that broke his mast and shoulder; he survived by collecting rainwater and catching fish attracted to his laundry. The container ship Houston Express spotted him signaling for help hundreds of miles from land, and he was airlifted to a Norfolk hospital in surprisingly good physical and mental condition.
