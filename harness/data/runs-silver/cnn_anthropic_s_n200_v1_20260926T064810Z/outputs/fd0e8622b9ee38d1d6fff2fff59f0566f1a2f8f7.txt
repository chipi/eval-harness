"Furious 7" is projected to open with $135-$138 million domestically this weekend, the largest North American opening since fall 2013, with a possible worldwide debut approaching $300 million. The final film featuring late actor Paul Walker is receiving Universal's widest release ever across 4,003 domestic theaters and more than 10,500 international screens. The movie's massive box office performance is driven by the franchise's popularity and public interest surrounding Walker's death in 2013.
