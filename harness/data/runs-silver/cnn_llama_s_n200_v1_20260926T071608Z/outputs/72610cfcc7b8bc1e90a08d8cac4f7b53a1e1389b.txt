The mother of Odin Lloyd, a 27-year-old man killed in 2013, spoke emotionally in court about her son's life and legacy after Aaron Hernandez was convicted and sentenced to life in prison for his murder. Odin Lloyd was described by family members as a champion of family, a gifted athlete, and a hard worker with a sense of humor. Hernandez was sentenced to life in prison for the first-degree murder of Lloyd.
