Maryland police have taken Nyia Parler, 41, into custody to face charges in Philadelphia for allegedly abandoning her 21-year-old quadriplegic son in woods for over four days. Parler cannot be extradited until she completes unspecified "treatment" for a reason that is not being disclosed due to federal health care privacy laws. If extradited, she will face charges including aggravated assault and recklessly endangering another person.
