Hundreds gathered in Summerville, South Carolina, for the funeral of Walter Scott, a Black man fatally shot in the back by a white police officer during a traffic stop, an act mourners and clergy condemned as racially motivated. Officer Michael Slager was swiftly charged with murder after a bystander’s video contradicted his account, though a witness reported a prior scuffle, potentially complicating the case. Scott’s family and supporters vowed his death would spur national change, calling it both a racial and human rights issue.
