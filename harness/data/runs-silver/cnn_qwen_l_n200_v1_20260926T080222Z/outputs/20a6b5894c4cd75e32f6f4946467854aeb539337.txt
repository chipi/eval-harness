Two Troy University students, Ryan Calhoun and Delonte Martistee, were arrested and charged with gang rape during spring break in Panama City Beach, Florida. The Bay County Sheriff’s Office identified the victim from a video found in an unrelated investigation and confirmed the assault occurred between March 10–12, 2015; both students have been suspended, and Martistee removed from the track team. Authorities say more arrests are expected as the investigation continues.
