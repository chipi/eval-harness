Thai Smile, a subsidiary of Thai Airways, unveiled an Adventure Time-themed Airbus A320 at Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi International Airport, featuring Jake, Finn and Princess Bubblegum on the exterior and show characters throughout the interior. The plane results from a partnership between Thai Smile and Cartoon Network Amazone, a new water park near Pattaya. Its inaugural flight runs from Bangkok to Phuket on April 4.
