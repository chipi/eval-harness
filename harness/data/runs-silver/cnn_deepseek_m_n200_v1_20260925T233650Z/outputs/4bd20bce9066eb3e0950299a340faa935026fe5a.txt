American Samoan veteran Emy Afalava is suing the federal government for denying him citizenship and voting rights despite his birth on U.S. soil, a case now before the D.C. Circuit. The Fourteenth Amendment grants citizenship to all persons born in U.S. jurisdictions, yet American Samoans remain the only inhabitants of a U.S. island territory still denied that right. The lawsuit seeks to overturn a century-old legacy of racially motivated rulings that have excluded islanders from full citizenship.
