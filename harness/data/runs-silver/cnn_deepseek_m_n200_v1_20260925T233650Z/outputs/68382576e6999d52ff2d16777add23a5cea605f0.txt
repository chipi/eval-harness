A daring Easter weekend raid on a London vault in the jewelry district saw thieves crack dozens of safety deposit boxes, an "Ocean's 11"-style heist not seen in the city for over 40 years. Former Scotland Yard commander Roy Ramm explains that such sophisticated jobs require meticulous planning, inside information and tight-knit teams, unlike typical burglaries. Detectives will scrutinize inside involvement, forensic evidence and known criminal networks in a relentless investigation.
