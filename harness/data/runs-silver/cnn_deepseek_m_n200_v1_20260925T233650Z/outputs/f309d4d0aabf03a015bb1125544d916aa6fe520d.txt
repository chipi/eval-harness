Most American taxpayers receive refunds averaging nearly $3,000, and free filing services have made Tax Day a happy spending spree rather than a dreaded chore. But the U.S. tax system remains deeply flawed—biased against two-earner couples, overly complex, and favoring the rich who earn from capital rather than wages. Happy endings, as Kahneman's research shows, help taxpayers forget the unpleasantness that came before.
