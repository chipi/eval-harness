Framework agreed in Lausanne severely limits Iran’s capacity to build a nuclear bomb, cutting enriched uranium stockpiles and centrifuges while imposing permanent expanded inspections. Opponents’ claims that the deal aimed to eliminate Iran’s entire program or that it contains a sunset clause are factually incorrect. The agreement is a multilateral P5+1 deal, not a bilateral US pact, and mixing in unrelated issues would derail its core nonproliferation goal.
