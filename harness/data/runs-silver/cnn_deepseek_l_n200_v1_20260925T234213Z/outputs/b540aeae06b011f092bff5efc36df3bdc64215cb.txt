Amy Schumer's opening monologue and Channing Tatum's impromptu twerk for Jennifer Lopez were highlights of the 2015 MTV Movie Awards. Rebel Wilson's censored joke and Fall Out Boy's mashup with Fetty Wap also landed well. Misfires included a botched archery gag, Shailene Woodley's rambling speech, and a tired ball-grabbing bit from Zac Efron and Dave Franco.
