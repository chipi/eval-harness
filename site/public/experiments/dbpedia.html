03Findings

What this experiment found.

  1. No winner: a group of nineteen.

    qwen_m leads at 0.9929, statistically tied with 19 others, including the free fine-tune.

  2. The dearest model finished 2nd of 28.

    anthropic_l billed $0.3784 for 280 items. The cheapest model statistically tied with the leader, gemma_m, cost 116× less.

  3. The benchmark is saturated, and the gold is sometimes wrong.

    Three items are disputed by 24 to 25 of 26 arms, and reading them, the gold label is the error.

04Reproduce it

Check it yourself.

The 4 free arms rerun on a laptop CPU for $0, pinned to the weights that were measured. The 24 hosted arms billed $1.17 when measured and rerun with one OpenRouter key.

make compare writes a replication card: each rerun against its committed run, item by item, and every separation and tie on this page re-tested with your reruns in place.

The guideThis example's code
$ make plan      EXP=dbpedia
$ make data      EXP=dbpedia
$ make reproduce EXP=dbpedia LEVEL=local
# with OPENROUTER_API_KEY set
$ make reproduce EXP=dbpedia LEVEL=hosted
$ make compare   EXP=dbpedia
