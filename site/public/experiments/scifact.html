03Findings

What this experiment found.

  1. LLM rerankers lead — inside a twelve-way tie.

    glm_s reranking BM25's top 20 scores 0.7437 nDCG@10; 12 arms cannot be separated from it, including the free dense retriever e5_base.

  2. The dearest model finished 2nd of 19.

    glm_m billed $0.4576 for 200 items. The cheapest model statistically tied with the leader, deepseek_s, cost 7.6× less.

  3. The dev slice picked the wrong winner.

    Tuned on a 40-query dev slice, the leaderboard's winner there was not the winner on the 160 queries it never saw.

05Reproduce it

Check it yourself.

The 7 free arms rerun on a laptop CPU for $0, pinned to the weights that were measured. The 12 hosted arms billed $1.94 when measured and rerun with one OpenRouter key.

make compare writes a replication card: each rerun against its committed run, item by item, and every separation and tie on this page re-tested with your reruns in place.
