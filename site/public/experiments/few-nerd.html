03Findings

What this experiment found.

  1. The only unambiguous winner in the repo.

    span_marker, fine-tuned on Few-NERD's training split, scores 0.7674 and is separated from all 27 other arms.

  2. The dearest model finished 3rd of 28.

    anthropic_l billed $0.6897 for 280 items. No paid model is statistically tied with the leader.

  3. But its margin is partly the corpus's own conventions.

    57% of its margin is one entity type, other, whose meaning exists only in this corpus, and 38% of its lead over the best hosted model is agreeing with annotation the rest of the field rejects.

04The method, here

Silver is not gold.

57% of the winner's margin is one entity type whose meaning exists only in that corpus. A clean win can still be partly a win at the annotation, not the task.

05Reproduce it

Check it yourself.

The 4 free arms rerun on a laptop CPU for $0, pinned to the weights that were measured. The 24 hosted arms billed $2.61 when measured and rerun with one OpenRouter key.

make compare writes a replication card: each rerun against its committed run, item by item, and every separation and tie on this page re-tested with your reruns in place.
