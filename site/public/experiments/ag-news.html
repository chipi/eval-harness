03Findings

What this experiment found.

  1. Three small fine-tuned classifiers take the top three places.

    bert_base_ta leads at 0.9600; the best hosted model, anthropic_m, scores 0.9100.

  2. The dearest model finished 5th of 30.

    anthropic_l billed $0.1750 for 200 items. The cheapest model statistically tied with the leader, anthropic_m, cost 2.5× less.

  3. Most of the lead is the dataset's labelling convention.

    17 items are labelled against the judgement of most of the hosted field — business news about technology companies filed as Sci/Tech, Olympic results filed as World. The fine-tunes agree with the gold on most of them.

05Reproduce it

Check it yourself.

The 6 free arms rerun on a laptop CPU for $0, pinned to the weights that were measured. The 24 hosted arms billed $0.58 when measured and rerun with one OpenRouter key.

make compare writes a replication card: each rerun against its committed run, item by item, and every separation and tie on this page re-tested with your reruns in place.
